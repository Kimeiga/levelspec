# Architectural review

Judge whether the modeled place reads as plausible architecture for its brief,
setting and intended degree of stylization. A blockout may be coarse, but it should
convey an actual building or outdoor environment rather than only floor planes and
partitions. This review concerns modeled architecture, not an obligatory visual style.

## Independent scope and evidence

For whole-map/building blockouts and broad architectural revisions, use an independent
architecture reviewer before delivery. Give the brief, revision, editable source and
actual compiled views before author rationale. The reviewer may also cover structural
circulation when explicitly assigned, but this pass does not replace FPS spatial and
tactical reviews or a requested source-fidelity review. Keep local rechecks local.

Inspect ordinary non-cutaway views, useful sections and source geometry, with interior
views where interiors are modeled. Verify whether a roof/ceiling is really
missing or merely hidden for inspection; a roofless screenshot alone is inconclusive.
If evidence is unavailable, retain that uncertainty rather than inventing acceptance.

## Architectural questions

- **Enclosure:** do indoor settings have credible floors, walls, ceilings/roofs and
  openings? Laboratories and enclosed buildings generally need overhead enclosure;
  atria, ruins, construction or other intentional openings follow the brief. Do not
  omit roofs merely to expose a layout in an overview; use cutaways/layer isolation.
- **Outdoor context:** streets and courts can remain open to the sky. Inaccessible
  building masses need not contain invented interiors, but their visible facades,
  tops/rooflines, bases and entrances should read as coherent buildings.
- **Volumes and levels:** do storeys, slabs, stairs, landings, terraces and overhead
  spaces fit together? Check purposeful height relationships, plausible support and
  connections to ground/terrain. A flat playable floor can still have convincing
  architectural volume; extra walkable elevations are not compulsory decoration.
  Allow intentional spatial invention and modest departures from literal realism
  for play and interest; plausibility does not require every room to be rectangular.
- **Use and surroundings:** do room groupings, circulation, architectural features
  and selected props suggest the place's function? Use structures, machinery,
  furniture, service elements or site context where helpful, rather than scattering
  arbitrary objects. No prop quota, photorealism or engineering/code certification
  is implied. Let real reference study inform uncertain assemblies.
- **Whole composition:** do masses, open space, transitions and detail form a place
  with a legible identity? Technical validity or polished shading alone does not
  establish architectural completeness. Honor stylized and unusual briefs instead
  of forcing one building type or roof form.

## Findings and repairs

Return pending, changes required or accepted for the inspected revision, with
consequential findings tied to objects/regions and actual views. Distinguish missing
geometry, a camera/cutaway issue, intentional design, uncertain construction and an
art preference. Prioritize enclosure, major volumes and contextual coherence before
trim. Accept supported defenses and do not manufacture flaws.

Use [level-authoring](../level-authoring/SKILL.md) for repairs and physical verification.
If a change affects gameplay, follow [fps-map-workflow](../fps-map-workflow/SKILL.md)
for its drawing and affected review scopes. Roofs, ceilings, openings and props can
change sightlines, clearance and utility paths; recheck those interfaces. Larger
structural repairs are allowed within the brief. Reuse unaffected evidence and
obtain a return assessment of consequential repairs.

Use [reference-level-authoring](../reference-level-authoring/SKILL.md) for image study
or source correspondence, and [level-preview-art](../level-preview-art/SKILL.md) for
render styling. Preserve source constraints; unseen construction is not a proven fact.
Architectural acceptance does not certify navigation, competitive balance or fidelity.
