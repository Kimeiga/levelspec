## Clarification / selected comparison

**LS2 — East ground strip bypasses the trench.** The drawing leaves a continuous
Z=0 strip x[22,26] west of the tall bus equipment. It connects Cooling to the
same B entry x[22,26], y=7 used by E port. Consequently the lowered trench is
optional even without reactor control. This is not automatically a defect: it
could be an effective local high/low choice. However the contract currently names
B's west entry as the reactor approach and its south approach as the trench.
Choose explicitly whether to retain the ground strip as a shared connector,
with its exposure/cost represented, or alter its physical access. If retained,
compare the ground strip with the trench for exposure, follower support and
return cost; do not assume players will descend merely because the trench exists.
A label-only correction is sufficient if this extra route is intentional.

**LS3 — Gallery screen comparison.** Preserve the screen initially, then compare
opposing views from both lateral sides and the upper A landing. Its footprint
leaves a passable east half of the gallery; that supports movement, but may also
permit a simultaneous A/W-port view depending on actual walls, guard/sill heights
and shell occlusion. This remains a compiled question, not a required wall extension
based solely on a top-down sketch.
