# L04 strategic hypotheses

Read the actual drawing before this rationale. The expanded city preserves a manageable pair of external T frontiers per site and CT-side reinforcement, while adding decisions within districts. West players can follow the exposed curved Sea Ribbon, enter Laundry/Winery toward Bell/mid, or reset between them through Wine Lane. The extra interior clearing space costs time and information; it does not grant a new doorway straight behind A. East players stage in Taverna Court and choose Harbor's gradual coastal reveal or the bent Arcade toward Market. Customs offers a split around an opaque civic building and rejoins before B. Post Office provides a roofed upper rotation alternative. Chapel and boathouse interiors add site clearing/postplant repositioning and doorway commitments.

Possible 5v5 CT2A/1mid/2B vs T1west/2mid/2east remains an initial playtest hypothesis. More district space asks Ts to choose what they clear/hold, while CTs can concede outer territory and preserve site fronts. This could become too large for five-player information and clock budgets; measure access and rotations, then test defaults/late rotates in the target game. No further universal flank doorway is planned without evidence.

A execute can split the cliff switchback and Bell ascent, with Winery control enabling a western reset after contact. Cost: separate climbs and interior re-clears. A defenders can shift around Cistern or through Chapel, but doorways create entry and exit exposure. B execute can split Harbor and Market; Taverna/Arcade choice permits a plan change before committed B entry. Customs mass should break a universal Market/B line. B stair-foot is still a strong exposed anchor to inspect from both T approaches and CT height. The boathouse's doors create clearance decisions and counterflashes, not automatically a safe postplant fortress.

Mid→Gate still commits to an exposed ascent before threatening upper rotation. Post Office adds a sheltered route around the upper corner, while both exits remain part of the same defending district. Ascent support, retreat, alternative threats and casualty coverage require compiled views. 3T/2CT A split,3v3 B plant,2v2 postplant,1v2 kit retake,CT mid push,aborted outer approaches and late site rotation are the initial stress cases.
