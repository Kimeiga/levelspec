# Three-map reboot results

The reboot sources are separate projects; the original three AML files and their
layout drawings remain unchanged. These are native architectural blockouts with
bounded movement and visibility evidence. Target-engine combat and multiplayer
playtesting remain necessary.

| Reboot | Preferred editable source | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ninefold / Loft Street | [R08 AML](../ninefold-reboot/level.aml) | Independent strategy, local/visibility and architecture acceptance. |
| Galini / Ravine Quarter | [C11 AML](../galini-reboot/level.aml) | Independent bounded strategy, local/visibility and architecture acceptance. Ordinary CLI publication remains blocked. |
| Last Light / Twin Atrium Exchange | [C02 AML](../last-light-reboot/level-c02.aml) | Curved-atrium variant adopted by all three independent review scopes. C01 is retained for comparison. |

## Ninefold — occupied city layers

![Ninefold R08, ordinary roof-on render](../ninefold-reboot/reviews/evidence/compiled-r08/architecture-roofs.png)

![Ninefold R08, native plan](../ninefold-reboot/reviews/evidence/compiled-r08/native-plan.png)

The factory is an occupied upper destination above a continuing street. Its
lightwell, Boiler receiving volume, Sign level and separate roof commitment give
the layers different uses. The proposed quarter-turn/gallery extension remains
deferred because it consumes School staging and changes the roof route's
information relationship with the lower court.

[Roof-off view](../ninefold-reboot/reviews/evidence/compiled-r08/global-roof-off.png)
and [factory close view](../ninefold-reboot/reviews/evidence/compiled-r08/factory-roofs.png)
show the shared building more clearly. The native plan projects multiple levels;
use the [labeled floor/section packet](../ninefold-reboot/layout-l02/review-packet.md)
with the [native repair overlay](../ninefold-reboot/production-layout/physical-repairs-r08.png)
for height-specific interpretation.

The retained run passes strict native validation/build and 108 physical walking
checks. Independent reviews add clearance and finite spawn-view probes.
[Project and final review links](../ninefold-reboot/README.md).

## Galini — inhabited coastal terraces

![Galini C11, roof-on native render](../galini-reboot/reviews/evidence/c11/roof-on.png)

![Galini C11, native plan](../galini-reboot/reviews/evidence/c11/native-plan.png)

The town uses six quarter/half-turn stair systems, two different bridge arrivals,
a committed shoreline journey and a lower arcade beneath the School. The roads,
retaining walls and pitched-roof masses form inhabited terraces. The bridge arches
and suspended arcade are real geometry.

[Cutaway](../galini-reboot/reviews/evidence/c11/cutaway.png),
[School support](../galini-reboot/reviews/evidence/c11/school-suspended-support.png),
and [turns/neighboring routes](../galini-reboot/planning/l03/turns-and-neighbors.png)
show complementary architectural relationships. Final images use the unmodified
reviewer with the exact compiled native artifact; they do not represent a successful
ordinary CLI build.

The standard CLI stops at its physical-work budget. A larger-budget native API run
produces the geometry but reports two sub-millimetre boundary samples. Exact-edge
and starting-position limitations are retained in the local evidence; full-width
admission and whole-floor navigation are not claimed. All three independent review scopes are closed with no required map geometry
repair. The [final decision](../galini-reboot/reviews/adoption-c11.md) links them and
retains the failed checks. The original suite remains136/158 directions, plus
separately successful A returns and independent continuous Customs traversals.
The final strategy replay confirms early selective road contest and later Salt
support; those remain tactical hypotheses for target-game play.

For interactive inspection, run `node projects/galini-reboot/preview.mjs` from the
repository root.

## Last Light — curved atrium and lower transfer

![Last Light C02, actual roofed west atrium](../last-light-reboot/reviews/evidence/c02/normal-preview/atrium-broad-w.png)

![Last Light C02, native floor projection](../last-light-reboot/reviews/evidence/c02/normal-preview/native-plan.png)

The west stair follows an 80-degree native curve. The lower G1 route runs alongside
it and beneath its upper end; the tangent landing separates the point approach
from the northward return. Turning changes leader/follower exposure, but the stair
guard is not assumed to hide players automatically.

[Whole-mall cutaway](../last-light-reboot/reviews/evidence/c02/normal-preview/cutaway.png),
[roof-on exterior](../last-light-reboot/reviews/evidence/c02/normal-preview/roof-on.png),
and [exact local plan/section](../last-light-reboot/experiments/c02/layout-c02.png)
provide complementary views. The native plan is an all-level floor projection;
it does not show every enclosing solid. The local drawing retains its pre-review
caption; the later [adoption record](../last-light-reboot/reviews/adoption-c02.json)
is the current decision.

Ordinary strict CLI build passes. The author records 44 passing movement chains;
the independent return adds 14 changed-path walks and 492 lower-corridor capsule
probes. The measured western public return becomes 4.547 seconds faster and its
service-route lag grows to 13.280 seconds. Those are inspection-controller results,
not established game balance. [Project and final review links](../last-light-reboot/README.md).

## Evidence and comparison limits

The [baseline observations](baseline-observations.md) explain what each reboot
tested. The original and reboot images use different cameras and cutaways, so they
are not controlled renderer comparisons. More paths, curves or floors alone do
not demonstrate better play.

The [circulation dispositions](circulation-development.md) distinguish adopted and
deferred proposals. The [compiler record](compiler-compatibility/README.md) keeps
failed and successful runs separate. Optional Blender UV baking was unavailable.
Pre-review frozen packets retain their historical open-gate wording; project
landing pages and final independent reports record the subsequent decisions.
