# Return spatial-interest review — Saltturn P02

**Verdict: promising.** P02 addresses both P01 refinement priorities through actual changes to building masses and outdoor thresholds. The revised lower journey has a legible sequence, and the Wash/Auction relationship is clearer without losing the useful central connections. No consequential phase-1 repair remains.

Reviewed the three raw P02 PNG sheets, including section D–D, before any accompanying rationale; also inspected the ground-plan SVG's revised footprints and roof distinction. This is a focused return review of the affected relationships. The unaffected P01 findings stand. No author rationale was needed to interpret these changes.

## Lower-yard sequence — addressed

The extended Filleting + dispatch building and the northward Net lofts wing now make Dispatch throat a real constriction between solid buildings. Road court first opens into Net court; the journey then passes through the drawn 7 m throat, opens into Repair court, and turns around the dispatch building's southeast corner toward Weighbridge yard and Ice apron. The change is carried by opposing frontages and the building corner, rather than relying on different labels for one broad void.

This also preserves a useful difference between the lower delivery journey and the central journey. Fillet walk still runs north beside the workshops into Wash court, and Wash court still reaches Scales cut along the Cold store's west side. Those journeys meet toward Weighbridge yard after approaching different faces of the dispatch mass. The revision creates a more deliberate lower passage without disconnecting the central opportunity to change direction.

Net court and Repair court remain open, moderately spacious places rather than being subdivided into a chain of small rooms. That fits the district's working-yard character and the outdoor brief. A constricted passage followed by an opening is sufficient here; further subdivisions are not required to establish interest.

## Wash/Auction interface — addressed

The fish-shop wash wing now extends eastward below Auction court. Its solid face separates much of the two courts, with Cart gate marking the open-sky transition beside the loggia. Section D–D makes the distinction understandable in volume: solid working wing, 6 m open strip, then the covered ground of the loggia's south arm. Wash court no longer reads as simply the unbounded southern continuation of Auction court.

The roofed margin remains an alternate way through the auction complex. Its open southern edge means the Cart gate is not the sole possible crossing between the courts; the drawings support a threshold made from a solid wing and a change of enclosure, rather than a sealed gateway. That is a coherent spatial choice and preserves the market's shared public edge.

## Remaining scope

The changes preserve the mostly-flat brief: all main journeys stay at grade, the modest loading ledge is unchanged, and the additions are inaccessible building masses shaping open-air circulation. The loggia, market back lane and separate Sea walk still retain their earlier relationships.

Later layout work should develop the passage mouths, the dispatch corner and the loggia's open south edge at player scale. In particular, any tactical assumptions must account for the broad loggia connection as well as Cart gate. Actual exposure, contact timing, objective boundaries and teammate use remain later-stage questions. They are not missing phase-1 proof or reasons to hold this proposal for another spatial-interest pass.
