# Baseline observations before reboot selection

Inspected the three original labeled plans and source-matched compiled overviews.
Source identities are in `originals.json`; images are in
`generated/reboot-study-originals/`. These observations concern spatial organization,
not competitive balance or a new physical audit of the old maps.

**Ninefold.** The plan has a west Drain/Market journey toward the factory, a central
Pipe/Mid route and an east Dentist/Laundry ascent toward Waterworks. There are useful
intermediate levels, but the upper gallery is a small local branch and much of the
urban mass remains inaccessible enclosure around the routes. The roof-on overview
reads as a largely continuous tall perimeter. A reboot can test whether occupied
building layers, inner streets and bounded views across light wells produce richer
relationships without becoming an unreadable tangle.

**Galini.** Sea Ribbon, the central Olive Piazza and Harbor Crescent feed the two
upper/lower objectives through a broad network of courts and stairs. The enlarged
outline supplies travel distance, but much of the plan's width remains open floor
between wall ribbons rather than streets formed by convincing village buildings.
The court sequence and coastal setting are strengths to reconsider, not a required
topology. The reboot should be assessed as a town in cliff terrain, including how
its masses create preparation, views, changes of plan and distinct arrivals.

**Last Light.** The two diagonal home loops feed one small octagonal court, with a
loft on either flank. The paired wings are similar in scale and encounter sequence;
the shared volume and mall-specific circulation are limited. A reboot can test a
more developed public concourse and service structure, with support, retreat and
re-engagement through substantial intermediate space. More rooms alone would not
establish that improvement, and adding upper routes must not invalidate ground-level
support or turn the whole objective into one panoramic firing position.

These are opportunities to test. The new authors may propose other organizations;
no listed mechanism is a mandatory feature or a predetermined acceptance criterion.
