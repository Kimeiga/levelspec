## Strategic hypotheses and scenarios

Distance-only early estimates: W primary roughly100m/18s, E primary roughly95–105m/17–19s at5.5m/s. Perimeter variants ~20–26s. These are sketch estimates, to be replaced with actual replay distances; unlock25s does not prevent early contact. Larger district requires regrouping discipline and may lengthen wipe recovery; no competitive balance claim.

5v5 opening: three through main bent street/copper gap, two toward roof/book or appliance side. A 4-point/1-flank posture keeps scoring presence but cannot watch every street. A 2-roof/3-ground posture gains information while risking a numerical ground contest and broken healing sight around B06/cupboard. Occupants gaining tea must choose a viewing bay, protect the other stair with attention/resources, or cede it while preserving point; this is a hypothesis for play.

Retake: stage behind CARPET-LOW or FRIDGES, show one main-front threat, synchronize a second group through Book Walk/Copper. A deep street wrap costs roughly one 10s respawn wave or more, so a lone late flank is not assumed effective. 3v5: give outer blocks, regroup at protected yard or a screen and wait for wave; don't send one player to every entry. Losing point ownership: fall around the cupboard/market block and select a new entry rather than circling endlessly on point. Full wipe: inspect how ~18–23s travel plus10s wave affects control percentage. Spawn push: a close hold at one building corner should not cover both around-building exits without moving; actual optical/timing check required. Spawn protection remains mandatory engine work.
