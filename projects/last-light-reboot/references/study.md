# Architectural image study — inspiration mode

Eight actual photographs were downloaded and visually inspected on 2026-11-09. Two revive the original study; six are targeted fresh selections of galleries, thresholds, food-court structure and the back of a mall. This is invented architecture informed by photographs, not a reconstruction. Images depict historical mall conditions; this packet makes no claim about current site access or condition. The [manifest](images.json) retains exact source-image URLs, source pages, credits, local copies and hashes. Copies are research evidence, not game textures or a grant of redistribution rights.

## Architectural synthesis, with inference boundaries

Photographic observations support three recurring spatial facts: mall circulation has extended public fronts, shop edges have depth, and upper galleries belong to a roofed shared volume. Those are the reference-derived ideas carried into both alternatives. Exact footprints, stair widths, structural grids, store programs, food-court elevations, service loops and tactical intent are the author's inventions.

A tests a large occupied department store beside a public promenade. Its retained lift/escalator core and partial gallery must be part of one enclosed interior, not scenery around an outdoor arena. B tests a raised food court between two atria with a ground transfer underneath. Its atria need real visible ceilings/skylights and shop fronts at both levels; the roof cannot disappear merely to make a plan readable.

Later architectural review should compare roof-on mass, orthographic cutaway and opposing interior views against these observations. The references do not validate combat distances, balance, sightline safety or reachability.
