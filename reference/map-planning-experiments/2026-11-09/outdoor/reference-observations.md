The following are visual observations; dimensions, hidden connections, and map relationships are invented. Image search captions were used to find sources, not as spatial evidence.

The other pages were accessible. None of the images establishes a plan, exact dimensions, or the rules of a bomb-defusal game.

A remaining reference gap is the cold-chain service assembly: the cold store, ice plant, loading ledge and ramp geometry are authored hypotheses. Targeted service-yard/loading-dock photographs would be useful during later detailed development. This trial stops at the initial proposal.

- Source: [Collectif Architectes — Port du Bloscon, Roscoff](https://www.collectif-architectes.fr/port-du-bloscon-roscoff).

- Observed by opening the downloaded image with `view_image`: a long, mostly solid industrial frontage; a single prominent numbered service door; stacked boxes and nets against the wall; broad level paved apron with large painted ground markings. This particular view shows no projecting canopy or courtyard enclosure.
- Use: a working building's extended solid face and apron can organize a service journey without a loose field of crates. P02's dispatch wing and the turn around its end are invented spatial relationships. The image does not establish the net court, throat widths, repair-court plan, or any proposed dimensions.
