# S02 spatial-interest review — Dragon Ford

Independent reviewer; stage: early spatial-interest judgment of the first developed proposal (`planning/s02/`, revision S02). Not a mechanics, visibility or balance audit. Inspected in order: brief, user direction on interweaving grids, `references/study.md`, four reference images (`plan-streets-1980s`, `aerial-1989`, `park-yamen-overview-2018`, `alley-covered-1993`), then `plan-0.png`, `plan-1.png`, three sections, packet README and `layout-data.json`. Connection claims below were checked against polygon coordinates by hand (edge interpolation), not by a tool run.

## 1. First impressions (written before reading any earlier review or alternative)

- **What reads immediately:** a stepped Yamen compound in the middle (Cannon Court z0 → front court z0 → middle hall z1.7 → rear terrace strip z3.4), a tunnel street heading west to a lit Well Court and a climbing lane to a temple-lot site (A, z3.4), a long factory tunnel on the east ending at a double-height foundry with a mezzanine (B, z0/3.4), an open apron in the south and a street frontage in the north. The stepped site and the "ground floor meets the next block's first floor" idea from the study are real in the sections. That is a recognisable piece of the reference: low compound in a sheer void, sloping ground, tunnel alleys, a factory street.
- **What does not read:** the Walled City as *one solid mass threaded by interlocking alley systems*. The plan is four districts floating in a very large dark field, each a small family of rectangles sharing one rotation: Yamen +5°, west −9°, east about +12° off north, north −3°. The districts meet only through thin door slivers drawn in yellow. The seams between grids are buried in solid mass rather than played.
- **The angles are timid.** 3° to 17° of relative rotation shows on an overview but will mostly read on foot as "slightly skew door".
- **A ladder inside the central grid.** Four parallel south→north ascents within 38 m of X (School x≈30–36, West lane x≈36–41, Yamen axis x≈43–63, East lane x≈63–68), all with their flights at the same two Y stations (y≈39–43 and y≈52–57). Ten of fifteen flights are the same 10-riser 3.4 m unit facing the same way.
- **Macro suspicion:** T south, CT north, A north-west, B north-east, a mid between, one feed per side plus the middle, and a straight east–west line at y≈57–61 joining A, mid top and B. That smells like the three-feed-plus-lateral template the brief warned about; to be tested by tracing.
- **Best single thing:** the foundry (B). Arriving at floor level behind a boiler under a north mezzanine that defenders reach first, with a second arrival on a west mezzanine from the Yamen top, is a real shared volume.
- **Weakest single thing:** A. A flat open rectangle with a notch; all three entries arrive on its east/south-east side.
- **Vertical character missing:** nothing crosses over anything except the foundry mezzanine. No bridging corridor over an alley, no shared stair shaft, no roof. "Storey 1" is just the uphill ground.

## 2. Verdict: **rework** (targeted; keep the centre and the foundry)

S02 has enough material (about 24 distinct places, roughly 40 transfers, 15 flights, a 96 x 92 m used extent; comparable in count to Spillway's 19 spaces / 25 connections) and it answers many S01 local requests. It is not thin. The reasons for another planning pass are organizational:

1. **The grids do not interweave; they are zoned.** Each of the four rotations owns one quadrant and no street, block or court of one grid enters another grid's territory. Collisions exist only as seven slivers: Tooth shop (the one real wedge room, pentagon at x 24.4–29.9, y 35.8–44.1), Well Court (trapezoid), the Lung Chun W / Cannon Court wall meeting at x≈43–45, `A_EAST` (a 5 m tapered door-tube, x 23.5–28.6), `YOUTH_SW` (skew door), `FB_YAMEN` (tapered door, x 69–73.8) and Coffin lane (tapered tube). Everything else that the rotations produce is solid wedge between districts. In `plan-streets-1980s` the differently-angled systems share alleys, produce tapering lots, dog-legs and leftover triangular yards *in the walkable network*. Here the answer to the user's direction is close to "four tilted rectangles' worth of boxes", which the spatial guide names directly: rotating a repeated layout leaves its organization intact.
2. **The macro graph is S01-A's, and still the forbidden one.** South attackers, north defenders, three south→north feeds (Lung Chun W → Well → Sai Shing; Gate → Yamen; Kwong Ming), an upper site on the north-west, a ground factory site on the north-east, lateral links between. That is Ninefold's stated arrangement mirrored (ground factory on one side, upper site on the other, south-to-north three-feed; checked against `projects/ninefold/README.md` only, not its plan), which the brief excludes and the S01 spatial reviewer already flagged. S02 improves it into a lattice with three tiered laterals (z0 at y 30–45, z1.7 at y 36–53, z3.4 at y 57–61), which is richer than one bridge, but it is the same family.
3. **A ladder in the centre.** School, West lane, Yamen axis and East lane are four parallel ascents 5–20 m apart with flights at identical Y stations; the stepped-site idea becomes two contour lines crossed by nine identical 10-riser flights.
4. **Long constant tubes got longer, only tilted.** `KWONG` is 31 m x 3.5 m straight; `LUNG_W` 35 m x 3.5 m straight; at z 3.4 `A_EAST` → `BACKDOOR` → `Y_STRIP` → Coffin lane → `B_MEZZ_W` is collinear within door widths from x≈21 (A screen) to x≈82 (mezzanine edge), about 60 m (plan geometry only; not a ray study).
5. **Walled-City volume is mostly absent.** One stacked relationship (foundry mezzanine). No corridor over an alley, no shared stair shaft, no roof; S01's roof layer was dropped rather than reshaped. Alleys are tunnels (good) but 3–3.6 m wide and uniform.

What is promising and should survive: the stepped compound as the middle; Cannon Court's T-shaped ruin closing the gate axis; Well Court as a lit junction with the Tooth-shop cut-through to the School at the half level; the Fishball workshop as a three-door staging room; the foundry with boiler, two descents and two arrival levels; the closed rear hall forcing defenders onto the strip; the Youth Centre and Dentist as occupied defender interiors.

## 4. The questions

- **Coherence as a Walled-City place.** Centre: yes (low compound in a void, stepping up; front alley; school on the west; youth centre on the north, all matching the exhibit plan). Flanks: by label. The dark field between districts is so wide (e.g. x 45–72 between Cannon Court and Kwong Ming; x 40–62, y 61.7–69 rear hall; the entire band y 46–66, x 68–75 except two doors) that the plan reads as rooms scattered in rock rather than alleys between abutting lots. The middle hall is still a blank open rectangle labelled "(roofed)".
- **Do the grids interweave?** No; see verdict 1. Angles of 3°–17° and collisions confined to door slivers. The Tooth shop shows what a played collision gives; there is one of it.
- **Substance.** Adequate against the benchmarks by count and extent. Undeveloped: A (blank), Youth hall (blank 22 x 12.5 m, two doors), both spawns (830 and 608 m2 blank), the two long tunnels.
- **Connection choices.** Good in the centre-west (J2, J5). Thin on the west face after Well Court, thin on Kwong before y 33, thin for defenders (three single-thread exits; one door to mid).
- **Spatial variety.** Sky/tunnel alternation is deliberate and works on J1 and J2. Stairs are one unit repeated. Only B has an above/below relationship.
- **Template.** Still three south→north feeds with laterals and corner sites. More cross-links than S01, same skeleton.
- **S01 requests answered:** apron wrapped to the west with Lung Chun as a through-street (S7 part), gate-axis ruin (L8), Well Court and jogged Sai Shing (S3 part, L2), shop cut-through (S3, moved to the half level), closed rear hall with balustrade strip (T1), Youth Centre hall (S8 part), Dentist through-shop and jogged NW approach (S9 part), oblique Kwong with fish-ball workshop and jog (S5/T6 part), second descent at B (T5). **Not answered:** Yamen as a building standing in the void with drawn hall (S1), temple standing free in its lot with gaps round it (S4/L1; only a north notch), bridge corridor / shared shaft (S3), street passing through the works and a bridge room (S5), frontage depth (S9), Youth hall east door and rear-hall link (S8), any roofscape (S6; removed), straight constant tubes (spatial defect 1; worse), and the macro challenge (spatial defect 7 and the "fourth option").

## 5. Suggestions (at most eight; coordinates approximate, for the author to resolve)

1. **Let one grid's street cut through another grid's court.** Continue Lung Chun Road on its own −9° line east of x 44: it crosses the south part of Cannon Court obliquely and reaches Kwong Ming Street near (74–75, 16). Cannon Court becomes a trapezoid with a triangular residue against the tower wall, the study's "one long east–west spine across the compound front" exists, and Kwong Ming gains a decision 7 m in instead of 24 m in. Then reconsider whether the Gate tunnel and both apron ends are all still needed; removing one feed is the cheapest way out of the three-feed skeleton.
2. **Play the seams instead of burying them.** (a) West/Yamen seam: replace the 5 m `A_EAST` tube with a tapering slot yard between A's east wall and the gallery's west wall (x 23–29, y 50–61, about 4 m wide at the south, 6 m at the north), and turn at least one gallery window west onto it. (b) Yamen/east seam: the triangle between East lane and the Fishball workshop (x 68–74, y 33–46) as a wedge yard that both open onto, instead of `FB_YAMEN`'s door-tube. (c) Give at least one building two faces on two grids (the Fishball workshop is the obvious one: west wall on +5°, east wall on the Kwong grid, making it a trapezoid).
3. **Break the 60 m line at z 3.4** (x 21–82, y 57–61) with a real grid kink rather than cover: e.g. bring Coffin lane into the mezzanine on the east grid's own direction from East lane top's north end (about (64,66) → (78,63)) instead of from the strip's east end, so strip, lane and mezzanine no longer share an axis; similarly shift `A_EAST` off the `BACKDOOR` line (suggestion 2a does this).
4. **Rework A as a building in a lot.** Stand the Tin Hau hall free at about x 11–20, y 61–69 with 3 m gaps west and north; continue the dead north-west prong (x 10–13, y 65.5–70.9) round to the Dentist door so defenders have a second arrival; bring `SS_UP` in at the south-west (kink the upper Sai Shing lane west on the west grid to about x 8–11, y 51–53) so attackers' two arrivals are on different faces rather than 6–8 m apart at the south-east.
5. **De-synchronise the central ladder and draw the hall.** Four parallel ascents are one too many: make West lane the Yamen's west wing by removing the 1 m wall between `WL_MID` and `Y_MID` (x 41–43, y 43–52) so wing and court are one volume with a column line, and shift its upper flight to a different station or a quarter turn. Draw the middle hall as a roofed, open-fronted, columned building over the head of `SY1` (about y 43–48) with the inner court behind it (y 48–54), so "roofed hall then court" is a sequence, not a label.
6. **Give the two long tunnels changing section.** `KWONG` (31 m) and `LUNG_W` (35 m): vary width 2.5–4.5 m along the run, add one double-height workshop mouth opening off Kwong at about y 15–22 on the west side (lit, seen-into, no through route), and one dead-end shuttered spur off Lung Chun near x 27–30. These are the study's relationships 4 and 8, and they are what the tilt alone does not supply.
7. **Open the Youth hall to the east.** A door from its south-east corner (59.2, 69.1) to East lane top's north end (61.8, 68.2): 2.6 m of wall. Defenders then choose which end of the strip to arrive at, East lane top stops being an unexplained 7 m dead end, and the centre no longer depends on `YOUTH_SW` alone. If the dead end is intentional, say so and close it visibly.
8. **Restore one overhead relationship outside B.** Cheapest candidate with compatible levels: the Fishball workshop's roof (x 72–80, y 33–46, top at z 3.4) as a low pit-roof terrace reached from mezzanine W by a 2 m catwalk along the foundry's west wall (x 75.5–78, y 49–56), overlooking East lane (z 0) and crossing above the `FB_YAMEN`/`FB_B` ground link. This is a strategy-relevant addition (an upper position over the attackers' east staging) and needs the strategy reviewer's view; the point here is that a Walled-City map with nothing passing over anything is missing a signature.

# Return review — S03

Same reviewer. Raw packet inspected first (`planning/s03/` plans, five sections, `layout-data.json`); the author's disposition was compared only afterwards. Connections were checked by hand interpolation of the polygon coordinates; no ray study, walk or timing was run.

## Verdict: **promising**

The two organizational objections from S02 are answered in drawn geometry rather than in labels.

- **Do the grids interweave now? Yes, in the south and at four seams; still zoned in the north.** Lung Chun Road now runs on the west grid's −9° line from the West slot (x 6) through the +5° Cannon Court to the east grid's Kwong Ming Street (x 77), about 72 m, interrupted by a ruin block standing on that line. One system's street crossing another system's court is a real collision: the court has an oblique entry and exit, and its west third becomes a junction (Gate arriving, Lung Chun leaving west, the ruin wall to pass round to reach the Yamen gate). Played seams: Slot yard (trapezoid x 23.3–28.3, y 52–61) between the temple lot and the School, Rubbish yard (x 69.3–73.4, y 37.4–46.8) between East lane and the Fishball workshop, Tooth shop, and the Back yard wedge (x 35.4–42.7, y 66–70) between West lane top and the Youth hall. Sai Shing upper is now a true diagonal dog-leg (about 26° off the west grid, 2.9–3.4 m wide) arriving at A's south-west corner. Not yet: no building has faces on two grids (Fishball is unchanged), the rear-hall block and everything north of y 62 between x 40 and 62 is still one solid, and relative angles remain modest. Enough to proceed; worth keeping in mind at layout.
- **Macro.** Two south feeds instead of three; Kwong Ming no longer reaches the apron (it is fed from Lung Chun E and the Yamen side). The skeleton is now a cross-street inside the wall distributing to three climbs, which is the study's "one east–west spine across the compound front" and is no longer Ninefold's arrangement as described in its README.
- **A** is now a lot round a free-standing cella with arrivals on three different faces (SW corner, east via Slot yard, north from the Dentist). **Centre:** the ladder is reduced to three ascents; the middle hall is a roofed volume (ceil 4.8) with the L-shaped wing/court round it and the upper flights split west (SLW2) and east (SY2b). **Coffin lane** now leaves East lane top's north end, so that dead end and the 60 m A-to-B line are both gone.
- Disposition check: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 verified as drawn. 6 partly as stated (see issue 4). 7: the decline is a reasoned strategy judgment and East lane top is no longer a dead end; accepted. 8: drawn, with a blocking error (below). Mezzanine guards verified; the stair-headroom claim is consistent with section SS but cannot be verified from the data alone.

## Remaining consequential issues

1. **Attacker travel is long to both sites.** Plan estimates from the anchors: T → A about 85 m (Gate, Lung Chun W, Well, Sai Shing), T → B floor about 100 m (Gate, round the ruin, Lung Chun E, Kwong, jog, mouth); CT → A about 55 m, CT → B mezzanine about 50 m. Removing the east slot lengthened B. Not a timing claim, but the ratio is large enough to examine first at the layout stage (apron extent x 4–62 and T anchor at x 40, Gate at x 46–50 are the levers).
2. **The "through street" passes one 3.3–3.7 m gap in Cannon Court.** `RUIN` (x 52.4–57.3, y 15.7–22.4) meets the court's south wall, so everything going east passes between its north face and `RUIN_WALL` (y 25.7–27.3). That gap carries all attacker traffic to B and half the traffic to the Yamen gate. Pulling the ruin 2.5–3 m off the south wall would let the street split round it.
3. **A 44 m door-aligned line at z 3.4 remains:** `A_EAST` (y 56.8–59.6) → `GAL_W` (56.4–59.1) → `BACKDOOR` (56.4–58.7) → strip → East lane top's east wall (x 23.5 to 67.8). Moving `GAL_W` into the yard's taper (about y 54–56) would break it and give the Slot yard's south pocket a use.
4. **Lung Chun W is still a 38 m straight tunnel, 2.9–3.5 m wide.** Its "standpipe niche" (x 30.1–34.4, y 24–27.3) coincides with the School lane mouth, so it is a widened junction, not an additional event; nothing changes between the West slot corner and Well Court.
5. **Hall enclosure is undecided and now matters.** With `SLW1` removed, the 6 m `SY1` into the hall is the only central climb from the front court; whether the hall's west side and rear (behind the screen) are open or walled decides whether the west wing is a flank or a cul-de-sac reached only from the School landing. No apertures are declared on either shared edge.
6. **Defender side and both spawns are still undeveloped:** Youth hall is a blank 22 x 12.5 m room with two openings, `CT_STREET` and `T_APRON` are blank strips, and the roof layer is deferred, so the only over/under relationships are at B and the new catwalk.
