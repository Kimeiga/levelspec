## P01 focused experiment readiness

The [raw comparison](../planning/stair-comparison-p01.png) and [exact proposal](../planning/stair-comparison-p01.json) are sufficient for a bounded native X1 experiment. The 80° sweep, 13 m center radius and 4 m nominal width create an ordinary broad ascent that shares the west atrium with the lower G1 approach. The proposed 0.640 m inner and 0.873 m outer treads do not create a pinched inner walking line on paper. A 3.1 m tangent landing can make a credible connection to the existing z3.6 gallery, with onward north receiving/return space. Other three stairs and the whole shell need not be redesigned to test it.

The construction return must inspect `CX1-LAND`, the old y56 exit closure, trimmed `GU-0-0-1`/upper-slab edges and the final 5.294 m raised soffit. The section's transition from filled support to 2.2–3.25 m soffit is plausible as an open inclined underside at blockout detail, but only the actual body path can establish where the lower route has sufficient overhead clearance. Inspect the underside spring across the body width, inner/outer treads, guard bearing and both landing exits in native roof-on/section views. The paper's 2.35 m conservative lower-route bound is not a compiled measurement. The prototype's narrower clear width and changed approach/arrival are local/strategy review issues; this report does not override those separate gates.

The original P01 documents say unselected; the current task assignment identifies it as selected conditionally for native experiment after those paper gates. This readiness finding follows that current disposition. Keep C01 as the source-matched baseline until the native variant passes its focused construction and affected gameplay returns.
