## Consequential finding and return check

**V1 — r02 front-flight spawn channel (production blocker, resolved in r03 drawing).** HALL-FOYER has an opening x51–57 at y44, while FRONT-STAIR occupies x54–60 between y26–34. Their overlapping x54–57 strip admits a predicted ray from hall feet `[56,45,3]` / eye `[56,45,4.86]` to T spawn feet `[58,11,0]` / eye `[58,11,1.86]`. At y34 the ray is around z3.89 above the upper landing; at y26 it is around z3.18, above the lower flight. ROAD-VAN at x64–68 cannot intercept it. This invalidated the r02 claim that offset thresholds protected T spawn.

r03 adds FOYER-SCREEN x51–62, y38–40, base3, height3.4. It spans the full straight-channel interpolation between the front flight and hall arch and rises well above both eye rays. Hall now starts at y44, eliminating the old overlapping floor/bypass. In plan the west aisle is 3 m and east aisle 2 m before body erosion, leaving about 2.4/1.4 m for the 0.6 m body. Both connect around the screen's north face; the east diagonal foyer boundary still leaves a usable turn. The west aisle permits a less constrained pair, while the east aisle asks tighter sequencing. **Drawing repair accepted; compile and inspect every spawn offset through the arch and around both screen ends before closing physical verification.**
