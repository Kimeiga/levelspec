# Greywatch — gameplay brief, layout r5

Original abandoned medieval European castle for 5v5 Counter-Terrorists versus Terrorists, two-site bomb defuse. Source is an AML geometry blockout, not an engine game-mode implementation.

Terrorists approach from the south gatehouse. CTs deploy through the north rear cloister. A is the roofless rose courtyard; B is a ruined chapel. Solid keep masonry, curtain walls, stair towers and a wall walk organize the place. The gatehouse and nave retain recognizable building purposes; the rest is open sky.

Provisional CS-style round assumptions: one bomb, either plant zone, no mid-round respawn, conventional rifles/snipers and smoke/flash/HE utility. Round/plant/defuse clocks, weapon penetration, grenade collision, boost and fall rules require target-engine integration. No route relies on a boost, jump or one-way drop. Static LevelSpec geometry does not implement planting, gunplay or a match.

Inspection body: radius 0.30 m, standing 2.0 m, crouch 1.1 m, step 0.25 m, slope 42°. Harness standing/crouch speeds 3.4/1.7 m/s, eye at 93% of active body height. These are declared harness dimensions, not a claim of Counter-Strike parity. Study views use 90° horizontal and 65° vertical FOV. All routes are land routes.

Target case 5v5: CT default 2 A / 2 B / 1 rear flex, T 2–1–2 probe or 4+1 execute. Stress cases: 5-player rush at either site; 3v5 after an early loss; 2v2 planted retake with one exhausted utility player; isolated 1v2 site hold. Human multiplayer testing is not performed.

First spatial sketch r1 earned a promising independent verdict. Developed r5 preserves the interlocking wall walk/underpass, adds real lower stair receiving pads and doors, a nave screen, differentiated low cover, and spawn gates. Layout and blockout each need independent spatial/visibility and tactical review.
