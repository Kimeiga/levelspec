## Reference comparison and declared limits

Revisited the actual `vessel-0.webp`, `assembly-0.webp` and `assembly-2.webp` references. The vessel reference distinguishes the tall D-shaped chamber, straight inboard side, curved outboard bulge and radial ports from the separate central bore. Assembly images show the concrete pit, concentric service access, lifting equipment and tall roofed hall. Their machinery and architectural frames have different radial and rectilinear orders.

The B05 reactor preserves those distinctions. The reactor-hall, crown-in-hall and north-vessel-interior views show the darker large D coils outside the lighter vessel, the separate solenoid, the concrete bioshield, and exposed lower hardware. The north interior remains a enclosing curved chamber. The southern unfinished state exposes the internal decks and central stair instead of replacing the toroid with a circular pipe. The crown is a separate outer access structure, below the coil tops, rather than a walkway on the vessel roof.

Published anchors remain those recorded in the source brief and previously checked in B04: 19.4×11.4 m vessel envelope, nine 40° vessel sectors, 18 coil slots with 9×17 m D profiles, 4×13 m solenoid excluding support, and an approximately 30 m cryostat. B05's ten installed coil stations and incomplete southern shell/cryostat state are fictional game adaptations, not a claim about ITER's actual construction schedule.

Intermediate section coordinates, wall/coil thicknesses, port details, temporary deck arrangement, shielding panels and surrounding rooms remain inferred or invented. This is dimensional and topological blockout fidelity, not engineering CAD, a source-building reconstruction, structural certification, or a model of an operating reactor.
