# Apogee — final blockout brief L06 / R06

Original observatory and research laboratory atop a snowy mountain, Counter-Terrorists versus Terrorists, 5v5 two-site bomb defusal. A is the upper telescope service floor (+4 m); B the lower cryogenic instrument laboratory (0 m). Terrorists arrive by cable station in the south; CTs begin in northern operations. Extent 104×102 m; 26 meaningful spaces/37 movement connections plus the internal Optics stair/landing seam. All ordinary routes bidirectional; no mandatory jump, ladder, boost or drop.

Current geometry: level.aml, layout.svg, plan-data.json, inventory.md. Sketch S01 and its seeded candidates are retained separately. Read the current drawing and inventory before author rationale in design.md. Earlier review records are revision-scoped; final R06 appendices supersede open R05 findings.

Harness player: radius 0.30 m, standing 2 m, crouch 1.1 m, step 0.25 m, slope 42°, standing/crouch speed 3.4/1.7 m/s. Eyes are 93% of active body height (1.86/1.023 m). Static visibility study uses 90° horizontal/65° vertical warning spans; captured perspective views use reviewer camera parameter fov 90 at aspect 1.5. These are inspection assumptions, not authenticated Counter-Strike movement/camera parity.

Provisional game assumptions: single-life rounds, one bomb, either site; rifles/sniper and smoke/flash/incendiary utility. Timers, weapons, economy, penetration, audio, utility, plant/defuse logic and multiplayer crowd collision require target-engine integration. This deliverable provides geometry, navigation and marker metadata.

Scenario set: 5v5 CT default: two at A, one Data–Archive flex, two at B; flex covers Mid exits rather than claiming early Calibration control. T 2-1-2 probe, 4+1 split, 5 T rush at either site, 3v5 retake, 2v2 postplant and 1v2 retake. Full-site rotations are costly; target-engine rotate+clear+defuse timing is a playtest priority.

Construction contracts: bridge top Z 4/soffit 3.65; lower walls inside x 42..68/y 78..84 cap at 3.65. Separate upper enclosure starts 4. Bridge south/east screens 2.8 m; north guard 1.35 m faces inaccessible ravine. CT stairs physically x 68.30..74, 16 m long; enclosure follows actual tread edges. Optics has a flat lower landing then 12 m flight; Rim is a 16 m ramp. Lab enclosures top 6.4; Operations top 7.2.

R06 widens Intake screen IT-S1 to x 42..46/y 20..26, closing the measured diagonal Mid-to-T pocket leak. CT spawn can become exposed after A Control or Archive is conquered; no permanent spawn immunity is promised. A deliberate 69.26 m Transit-to-Boot-exit rifle channel and a bounded Optics hold remain playtest hypotheses. No balance claim or human playtest result is made.
