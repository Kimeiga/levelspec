# Switchback — L01 design brief

Original 5v5 terrorist versus counter-terrorist, two-site bomb defusal blockout. No respawns. Sites A Reservoir (+16m) and B Turbine (+20m); T freight yard (+0m), CT service road (+24m). User asks for a really steep incline that materially shapes gameplay. The main hillside gains 16m over 37m (43%); flights rise 8m over 11–12m and the cistern flight rises 12m over 16m. These are stairs with actual horizontal treads, not slippery ramps. Overall playable extent 72 × 89m. All necessary routes are reversible; no boost, drop, ladder or jump required.

Working movement contract: 1.8m standing body, 1.1m crouch, radius .3m, step .25m, maximum slope 42°. Inspection eyes 1.674m/1.023m; speed 3.4m/s standing, 1.7 crouched. These are LevelSpec harness dimensions, not an authenticated CS2 configuration. Design assumes rifles and conventional smoke/flash utility, but no specific grenade radius or penetration behavior is required by geometry. No bomb rules, shooting, sound, multiplayer blocking, damage, or economy simulation exists in this deliverable. Target-engine integration and ten-player playtesting remain necessary.

Review original 5v5 defaults, commits, defender pushes, rotation after loss of Mid/Valve, two-site retakes, 2v2 and 1v2 post-plant. Layout-data.json gives exact room/edge/obstacle coordinates; layout.svg uses the same origin and elevations. Movement graph edges are explicit and bidirectional. Optical visibility is not an additional connection. All gray/colored floor rooms are playable; headhouse and terrain are inaccessible/scenic.

Geometry contract: rooms have 5m enclosing walls, door widths equal connector width (4–5m) and nominal 3.2m head clearance. Stairs have 3.2m side screens. Lower rooms, staging and spawns are roofed at 3.6m clear height; sites are open topped. CT service route has opaque 3.2m screens. Each ascent ends in a level staging room with an offset onward doorway. Exposed low refuges are 1.25m high; elevated views can defeat them and must be inspected. No universal crouch protection is assumed.

Independent layout review first: macro spatial/visibility, macro strategy/player behavior, and local A/B entrance–grade–retake interfaces. Give initial findings before reading design.md or other verdicts. Height-dependent questions remain pending compiled inspection. Source is not modeled until layout gates pass.
