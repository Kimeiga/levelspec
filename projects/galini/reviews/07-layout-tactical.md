## District relationships and manpower

- **Spawn decisions and 5v5 defaults.** T can choose Sea/Laundry, Bakery/Olive or Taverna/Harbor/Arcade. CT can concede these outer districts and defend site frontiers plus Gate. A 2A/1Gate/2B distribution remains plausible because the added districts mostly create local splits and rejoins rather than independent doors behind each site. Five players cannot continuously observe every new court/interior. The role split is a starting hypothesis; it does not require simultaneous ownership of all named spaces. Test a patient T default as well as a fast grouped attack.

- **Sea, Laundry, Winery and A.** Laundry has two genuine interfaces with Sea Ribbon and connects to Winery; Wine Lane adds another approach into the vault district. These are actual reset/reposition choices, not just a larger curved road. Winery also meets Bell Alley and western Olive Piazza. Consequently, Sea-side Ts can threaten Bell **without first controlling Olive Piazza**. The Gate defender's Mid information cannot be assumed to warn A of every Bell split. Two A defenders must account for both stair heads, or concede one and retake deeper court. That is an intentional changed information relationship, not a layout defect. A CT pushing Winery spends time/attention away from A and risks a Sea-side return behind it.

- **A court and chapel interior.** The two chapel doors connect different sides of the same site court rather than adding another external approach. They allow a defender to leave a known cistern-side angle and make attackers re-clear an interior; entering the chapel also commits to door exits. In 3T/2CT, a Cliff/Bell split can force a defending pair to reposition, but neither stair team's follower support is proven by width. In 2v2 postplant, Ts must choose between holding the upper entrance, the stair side or the chapel; they cannot safely cover every route. CTs may clear together through the upper entrance or invest time in Bell. Captures must establish whether a chapel door offers a useful plant view and what the opposite door exposes.

- **Bakery, Olive, Customs and Gate.** The piazza still enables A pressure, B pressure or a climb into CT reinforcement. Customs adds a north/south split around an opaque office and meets both Gate Foot and the western B district. It does not itself remove the exposed Gate ascent. A CT descending Gate can threaten these routes but cannot simultaneously watch the upper lane and clear both sides of Customs. The mass should break through-information, with a cost of re-clearing the other side. Verify the actual approach frontiers and the high Gate body exposure in 3D rather than crediting the office name as protection.

- **Taverna, Arcade and Harbor.** The repaired street mouth makes a credible plan change before committed B entry. Harbor reveals in coastal stages; Arcade offers a roofed route toward Fishmarket. T can switch at the court after information, but returning from advanced Harbor to this junction spends meaningful time and relinquishes pressure. In a 3v3 execute, a Market/Harbor split remains different from three players entering one frontier. CTs can contest Arcade early only by spending a player away from B or Gate. Interior ceiling/visibility and follower support require actual inspection; no grenade immunity is inferred from the roof.

- **B, Customs frontier, boathouse and Boat Stair.** The enlarged west district still converges into B instead of giving Customs an unguarded CT-spawn door. The two-door boathouse introduces a real clearing/reposition loop within B. A defender near the Boat stair foot may still watch the western and Harbor fronts in a modest sector; that is the primary strong-hold test, not proof of an unbeatable hold. Inspect its body exposure to both fronts and what changes after it uses the boathouse for cover. In a 2v2 postplant, a T in the boathouse must account for both doors and may lose direct plant information. A CT descending Boat may see over salt bins; h1.1 bins are not universal cover from elevation. A 1v2 kit retake may deliberately be difficult without implying that another flank doorway is needed.

- **Upper Ribbon, Post Office and rotations.** Post Office creates a northern alternative around the corner screen and rejoins the same upper district. This reduces dependence on the narrow southern O-ROTATE bypass; it does not eliminate Gate's ability to threaten the rotation after climbing. A Gate-cut T must choose which upper exit to watch, giving CTs a reposition possibility at the cost of clearing another interior. Inspect both passage ends from Gate and from each rotating side, including a follower. Do not claim both alternatives remain safe against the same opponent merely because they are connected.

## Clock and casualty limits

The old B04 measured A↔B path was 75.75m. A pure 1.3 XY expansion would put it near98m: about20s at assumed5m/s or29s at3.4m/s inspection walking, before clearing/utility; new passages can alter the actual route. These are extrapolations, **not L04 measurements**. Under the provisional40s postplant, prompt rotation plus a kit attempt can remain feasible, while a late call, no-kit attempt or clearing two site interiors may make a retake unrealistic. That is a meaningful larger-map cost. Measure the final routes and test late calls; do not describe Post Office as an instant reinforcement shortcut.

Likewise, a failed outer attack can reset through Laundry/Winery or Taverna/Arcade earlier than retreating to Ferry, but those loops must still be cleared and held. A 2v2 team has to choose what it gives up. This is additional playable depth with a manpower price, not a promise that every new connection will be used equally. Multiplayer playtests should check whether teams understand and exploit these choices within115s, or consistently avoid them because of clearing time.
