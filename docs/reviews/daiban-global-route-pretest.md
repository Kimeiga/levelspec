## Envelope and corner method

A finite list of center samples cannot prove continuous clearance. Use swept route segments and turns against actual obstacle triangles/solids, or adaptively refine with a stated conservative distance bound; retain critical exact stations at every riser, soffit discontinuity, portal and corner. Broad-phase bounds are an acceleration only. Triangle/envelope intersection plus closed-solid containment catches bodies wholly inside an obstacle; open soffit sheets still require surface intersection checks. Center-only vertical rays, sparse radial spokes or a rendered capsule screenshot cannot establish the full0.25m footprint.

Keep three questions separate: support/contact on actual tread tops, permitted riser traversal, and clearance from non-support structural obstacles. Permit contact with the identified underfoot support; do not exclude all floors or an entire upper stair owner from obstruction testing. A step straddling the footprint needs explicit step traversal treatment, not a test that silently raises the whole body until clear. Overhead clearance must retain the full2.10m height above the actual supported pose. Record both standing/step-envelope limits; these static checks do not certify gameplay movement.

For the Fan-side turn, test the swept disk around the **actual** flank end and guard return, including the intermediate turn poses: two individually clear straight corridors can still have an impassable corner. Guard top/side/end faces must not intersect the body or leave an unowned pit-side opening. Verify nominated thickness and local corner closure with sections/solid containment; cap polylines and route arrows are not physical boundaries. Do not add a front-plane longitudinal base as a shortcut to close a guard.

State numeric tolerance separately from source uncertainty. Use scale-appropriate GLB/Float32 bounds and the repository's authoring lattice/compiled simplification limits; report a marginal result rather than shrinking the0.25m radius or2.10m height. Actual triangle ordering/count may change under legitimate compiler retriangulation; compare coverage, level, area, bounds and ownership instead.

## Small controls that make the checks meaningful

Keep the project-specific geometry audit beside this trial, with a few small mathematical controls rather than a new general test framework:

- A beam/soffit fragment just inside the body's radius but off the center ray must fail envelope clearance, including when its prop is `collision="none"`. A nearby known-clear case must pass. The obstacle owner must appear in the failure.
- An upper-flat duplicate must fail overlap/ownership checks; removing a narrow strip at a full-width terminal must fail support coverage even if a centerline or nav path remains. Use independently known dimensions.
- A corner where each straight leg clears but the swept0.25m disk clips the return must fail. The coherent open-corner control must pass. These protect the method, not this builder's formulas.

Use existing [flight](../../tests/vector-flights.test.ts), [floor-gap](../../tests/vector-floor-gaps.test.ts), [navigation](../../tests/vector-navigation.test.ts) and [surface-contact](../../src/vector/surface-contacts.ts) coverage as repository contracts. No core cleanup or redundant broad suite is warranted unless the trial reproduces a core defect. Nav remains useful for connectivity, player settings and reported exclusions, but voxelized/sampled coverage can exclude occupied floor, omit collision-none structure or smooth nearby steps; a green report is not a physical/source headroom proof.
