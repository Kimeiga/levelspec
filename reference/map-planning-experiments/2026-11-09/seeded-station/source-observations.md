# Real-image study

Mode: themed invention. I directly viewed the five local original photographs below, through the neutral `reference-evidence.md` packet. No drawings, design rationale or reviews from the other station/outdoor trials were read. These images inform relationships and building character; they do not establish dimensions or constrain the new layout to a source building.
