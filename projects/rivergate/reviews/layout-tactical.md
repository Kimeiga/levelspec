**Final verdict: R02 accepted for layout-stage blockout.** This is a drawing hypothesis, not multiplayer acceptance. ## Macro relationships

The river is consequential terrain rather than a decorative line: three discrete, reversible crossings connect a quieter western distribution area to an eastern territory that offers both site splits. Ground north uses an open parapeted stone bridge with an offset toll obstruction; south uses a roofed linear lock; center commits through a shaped island and then permits A-short or B-short. Important journeys therefore differ in exposure and follow-up rather than repeating detached rooms and identical corridors.

Both bombsites east of the river is coherent. Holding a crossing is not equivalent to holding its site: `A-customs` and `B-turbine` turn the final approaches and separate direct bridge pressure from gallery pressure. `CT-admin` divides a forward east-gallery route from the protected rear rotation. Losing mid changes short access and information without automatically deleting the rear route. Western riverfront masses keep a team from observing all bridges from one quay position. This provides a credible objective/control structure without requiring artificial doors or swimming.

## Timing and commitment predictions

`CT-admin` makes CT mid access travel around a building instead of straight through spawn. That is acceptable provided the compiled routes confirm defenders can establish useful short-side contact before attackers freely occupy both site splits. A rough map-scale reading suggests CT spawn-to-site positions are shorter than T spawn-to-site approaches. This is a hypothesis, not a measured spawn-timing result.

The rear site-center rotation is approximately 70m by a coarse waypoint trace around the outside of `CT-admin`, or roughly 14s at the provisional 5m/s target running speed. The real path, held weapon, acceleration, combat and audio can change this substantially. A committed bridge fake has a substantial western recrossing cost; gallery control can shorten the attacking reroute while placing the bomb closer to defenders. These costs make a fake a commitment rather than a free map-wide switch. The mode clock is not configured, so late-round feasibility remains an explicit engine test.

## Risks retained for blockout verification

1. **A-long / A-hold:** `A-hold` eye Z2.83 can expose low obstacles, especially `A-refuge` and `A-long`. Inspect long and short simultaneously from several actual upper positions. The upper position may pressure long, but should not safely watch every plant/entry without repositioning or a teammate.
2. **Mid island:** `mid-pier` and `mid-island` may leave a narrow north-side travel/peek channel. Verify passing and follower support as well as a viable southern route around the pier. This is a concrete local check, not a macro rejection of a dogleg crossing.
3. **CT rear:** the long north–south rear lane makes rotations readable but may become a strong sniper lane after an attacker reaches a site edge. Inspect offset positions and whether a deep attacker can lock both site rear entrances while remaining protected from the plant. A long lane alone is not a defect.
4. **B site:** the larger open southern yard has fewer full-height interruption points than A. Inspect bridge exit, short exit and generator/rear views in both objective phases before deciding whether a focused architectural change is needed.
5. **Opening timings:** retain route-length/first-contact evidence for both spawns to each bridge and short junction; do not infer fairness from three crossing options.

## R02 focused return review

Independently inspected the R02 rendered plan and updated inventory after the initial R01 assessment above; no other reviewer's verdict was read. `rear-north-wing` X94–100/Y66–70, H4.8m and `rear-south-wing` X100–104/Y34–38, H4.8m introduce alternating east and west passage in the CT rear. Both leave a nominal4m route, enough to preserve an intentional passing/support corridor with the stated .30m body radius, subject to actual compiled corners.

The wings address the long rear sniper-lane risk without deleting the rotation or introducing a new river crossing. They should prevent one deep site-edge player watching the entire rear traverse from a single position. The north wing also bounds A-hold views toward CT spawn, making A-hold's advantage more local to its own site and long approach. A rotating defender must expose at each wing corner, which offers a local contest and a retreat threshold rather than a bare sixty-meter channel. They do not automatically make retakes safe: site holders can still contest their own rear entry.

Retain the same macro allocation, split and post-plant scenarios. Rear travel length rises modestly and now requires two corner clears; the earlier approximate14s unopposed rotation is provisional and must be replaced by actual path length/timing evidence. Neither wing blocks A-short or B-short because both sit behind the eastern site edges, so gallery control keeps its attack value. Verify four-meter passages, reverse corner exposure, A-hold-to-spawn occlusion from offset positions, and the wing interfaces in compiled views. No supported tactical layout defect remains that should hold production modeling.
