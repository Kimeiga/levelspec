# Simulated modular canvas awnings

This is generic asset design informed by the Fa Yuen Street photo and the existing balcony concept, not a measured reconstruction. The photo supports the shallow sloped canopy, lightweight steel perimeter supports, extended cloth edges, and variation in tension. The concept supports the front valance and fabric striping; its precise folds are an illustration, not physical evidence.

Reference dimensions are 3.00 m width, 1.30 m projection, and 0.45 m front drop. Axes are X across, Y out from the wall, Z up. Rear attachments lie at Z=0; the front support line lies at Z=-0.45. No wind animation is required at runtime. Blender Cloth produces four static rest shapes, retaining editable simulation cages and settled meshes in tarps.blend.

Planned forms: (1) taut with subtle wrinkles, (2) loose central sag between front supports, (3) asymmetric front tension, (4) hanging rumpled skirt. Each uses actual Blender simulation, with frame count, solver values, pin groups and rest-space coordinates stored in JSON metadata. Target is roughly 1,200–1,800 visible triangles after reduction per form before underside. No global smoothing/subdivision should erase folds; canopy cloth is deliberately an open shell and runtime may draw its back face.

The layout SVG is a construction sketch, not a predicted settled silhouette. The simulation may refine sag limits and pin locations; record any changes here. Actual settled attachments and extents are authoritative in cloth-forms.json. Simple cloth geometry makes photo contour tracing and a separate structural wireframe image unnecessary; coordinator supplies concept art and independently reviews the prototype.

Cloth settings reference: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.1/physics/cloth/settings/shape.html and https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/physics/cloth/settings/physical_properties.html.

Production clearance: canopy front rail is at reference Z=-0.45 m. The cloth must remain above Z=-0.60 m (15 cm below rail); the connected skirt is at most 0.14 m. Side braces terminate no lower than -0.58 m. Front/rear attachment X coordinates are inset 0.03 m. The first prototype exceeded the loose-form clearance, so it is rejected; production uses intermediate front/side ties and front-rail collision. Source sketch curves are explanatory, not predicted fabric bounds.

Runtime reduction targets 1,800 front triangles per form and protects the perimeter plus every pinned vertex. Attachment indices are remapped to the final mesh and checked against the settled simulation positions; no final vertex is manually shifted to fit the clearance. The full simulation cage and static reduced shell remain separate objects in the Blender file. Rest UVs are physical meters, including the 6.5–9% width surplus, rather than normalized texture coordinates. No baked light, shadow, or ambient occlusion maps are produced.

The settled shells in `tarps.blend` use the shared `tarp.png`, `tarp-normal.png`, and `tarp-properties.png` images packed into the file. Texture period is 1.5 m, tangent normal strength is 0.7, and roughness reads the properties map's green channel. Each form uses a muted tint. The source simulation cages retain neutral clay material so shape review remains independent of texture detail. To recreate the full library, run `model.py` in Blender; the checked-in JSON records the actual per-form solver values and resulting bounds.
