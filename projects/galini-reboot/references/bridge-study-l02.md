# Stone bridge construction (L02 addition)

[Inspected photograph](images/stone-bridge-gnto.jpg) · [Visit Greece / GNTO source](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/experiences/culture/archaeological-sites-monuments/famous-stone-bridges)

Observed: the masonry arch continues into broad rocky-bank abutments; the parapet is part of the bridge masonry, with a small relieving arch beside the main span. The real deck is humped. L02 adapts that continuous structural vocabulary to flat gameplay decks with reserved piers and abutments. Span, width, bed depth and load capacity are not measured or inferred.
