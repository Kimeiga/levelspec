# Reference study — Ninefold reboot

Fresh lookup on 2026-11-09 used [Blue Lotus Gallery's 2019 City of Darkness exhibition](https://bluelotus-gallery.com/new-events/2019/11/8/city-of-darkness-by-greg-girard-amp-ian-lambot), displaying photographs by Greg Girard and Ian Lambot. The [manifest](images.json) retains original URLs, photographer-section attribution, bytes hashes and inspection status. The eight selected images were actually viewed together in [the research board](../../../generated/ninefold-reboot-reference-board.png). Historical imagery informs an invented map; images are research evidence, not exported textures.

Observed relationships: street-facing shops sit below repeated residential bays; the perimeter reads as adjoining unequal buildings rather than scattered standalone boxes. Recesses and projecting slabs interrupt those facades. A rooftop room/terrace has equipment and antenna structures at different heights. The narrow service-space photograph shows machinery, water connections and overhead pipe tangles compressing an inhabited interior/outdoor boundary. A small shop/office demonstrates enclosed activity behind that dense frontage. Exact floorplans, access rights and dimensions are not recoverable from these views.

Design translation: build occupied commercial passages inside larger masses; concentrate open sky in selected courts; treat roofs as distinct service/inhabited precincts. Make a few convincing shared volumes and bounded observation relationships. Keep residential towers inaccessible where they are contextual massing. The proposed light wells, stair locations and objective rooms are inventions, not traced historical spaces. Physical stairs will remain wider/clearer than many photographed service gaps for the unchanged inspection body and team movement.

The original Ninefold contained no retained photographic reference directory. Its source/layout were inspected as the reboot baseline, not used as architecture references. [Original source hashes](../planning/original-source.json) establish which revision was compared.
