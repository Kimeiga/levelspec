# Veilcrest — spatial-interest review

**Verdict: promising.**

Scope: independent early review of `sketch.svg` and its rendered `reviews/evidence/sketch.png`, proposal 01. Brief: 5v5 CT/T bomb defuse in a secret mansion nested deep in steep mountains. No author rationale, detailed gameplay specification, or other review verdict was supplied before this assessment.

The drawing gives the estate a useful sectional identity. The garden ascent arrives beside the upper salon while the eastern drain reaches the lower vault; the interior hall connects to those wings at different levels. A journey through the foyer therefore offers a meaningful change of destination, while the western garden makes a larger commitment around the cliff. The solid library/rock core keeps the two objective wings from becoming one shared arena. The rear court joins them by a distinct, longer journey. These relationships are substantial enough to develop and fit a mansion excavated into a mountain rather than merely placing a themed building beside flat lanes.

The initial organization has three departures from T but they do not read as three equivalent parallel corridors joined by a bridge: the western route wraps a garden, the interior route branches inside the mansion, and the eastern route enters below a different wing. I have not inspected other projects for a novelty comparison.

Consequential development suggestions:

- Make the lower switchback's actual edges explicit. The continuous pale area around the foyer and drain currently looks freely traversable across much of the south/east footprint. Retaining walls and occupied volumes must establish which transfers really exist, so the stair routes are meaningful.
- Resolve the hall-to-salon threshold and salon-to-garden threshold as separate arrivals. The current drawing nearly merges the garden with the salon's entire southern edge; actual boundaries will decide whether the two approaches produce different clearing tasks.
- Clarify the CT rear court's +3 floor and its vault stair landing. Its pale fill currently uses the lower-level color although the label states +3. Preserve that reversible connection and show the occupied space beside the stair ends.
- Keep the steep scenic ridges outside playable boundaries, as marked. The drawing's mountain setting is promising, but actual compiled massing must still show an enclosed mountain estate without obscuring the intended routes in review.

This verdict permits detailed layout development. It does not establish physical reachability, combat sightlines, timings, target-engine behavior, or balance.
