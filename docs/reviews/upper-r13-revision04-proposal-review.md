## Required contract refinements within the approved correction

1. **Complete the common fan with depth-order directions.** Projected polygon edges alone are insufficient. At a query where affine depths have equal constant terms, split at directions where their first derivatives become equal. For example, two faces covering a neighborhood with depths `5+u` and `5+v` have no projected edge through the query, yet exchange order across direction `u=v`. Include the applicable projected face boundaries, equal-depth ordering lines and clipping/Boolean endpoints from actual faces, immutable baseline, new solids, old removal masks, retained islands and cuts. A sector must have constant incidence and lexicographic endpoint order. Do not discard a positive angular sector because it is narrow; uncertified incidence remains blocking.
2. **Use one sector identity through extraction, Boolean ownership and semantics.** Keep the original query. Evaluate mesh triangles and analytic polygons/holes using the same symbolic directional convention; apply removal, restoration, union and native-floor ownership inside each sector. Carry oriented crossings, physical contracts and multiplicity before union. At generic interiors, retain the existing contract. For ordinary closed material, invalid terminal/negative winding and unresolved open-shell evidence remain failures; positive winding multiplicity cannot silently collapse into a single occupied interval. Existing independent same-facing duplicate and positive-volume overlap checks remain mandatory.
3. **Preserve the distinction between tangency and adjacent material.** An isolated zero-length tangent contributes no positive-length material interval. A ray lying along a wall edge can have positive-length material intervals in its adjacent directional sectors; those must survive as side-specific evidence. Record zero-length contacts with incident sectors, orientations and derivative order rather than manufacturing thickness or identifying separated endpoints as contact. Finite 2µm and 0.5mm gaps must remain visible under the unchanged comparison contract.
4. **Integrate the same convention at every relevant boundary call.** The correction must cover expected baseline extraction, actual extraction, primitive/cut membership and endpoint physical contracts. Arrangement-cell interior calculations remain valid; when comparing an affine limit at a cell vertex, preserve that cell's approaching sector rather than pooling neighboring sectors or comparing one cell's extrapolation with all of them. Maintain old/new scope coverage and all exact witnesses. Do not introduce a boundary-only path that bypasses normal semantics or retention checks.
5. **Keep arithmetic predicates separate from geometric acceptance.** The prototype's fixed incidence/depth clustering constants are not approved replacements. Document how signs and equalities are certified, including degenerate projected faces and duplicate directions. The existing 1µm representation comparison and bounded 100µm arch exterior cleanup are separate contracts, not permission to snap queries to convenient edges, merge real gaps or enlarge contact tolerance. Preserve raw below-contract differences.
6. **Make extraction failure reviewable.** Write the proposed early diagnostics with exact input/checkpoint, query, stage, incident triangles, sector traces and exception. Mark failed/incomplete status unambiguously, preserve completed independent outputs and avoid claiming a building defect from a failed expected extractor. Continue other independent diagnostics only when they do not depend on the invalid extraction.

These refine the proposed algorithm rather than expand the architectural task. A simpler mathematically equivalent implementation is acceptable if it preserves the same side-specific, ownership and failure contracts. Avoid a generic geometry rewrite beyond the required shared query boundary.
