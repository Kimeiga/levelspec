## Proposals and costs

**NF-ST-01 — retain and test the observation-role cost.** Actual V-WELL and V-SIGN are useful precisely because their occupants relinquish other coverage. Keep their bounded openings. The strongest next play test is a3v3 with the same North defender alternating aisle versus inspection bay while the lower pair either shows or withholds the bomb; record which information changes the next decision and whether the return arrives before the floor is lost. At A, compare Sign+ground against a two-body ground retake with equal initial positions. This tests decision value without granting imaginary utility. Status: retained geometry, target-engine play queue.

**NF-ST-02 / NF-V01 — turning School ascent and west roof gallery.** The raw proposal changes S5 from `(36,49,3.2)→(36,61,6.4)` to a lower north flight atx40, a90° landing at+4.8, a west upper flight and a+6.4 gallery around Boiler's west edge. It preserves the graph edge but changes its role. With two T holding School and two working lower Boiler, one climbing player could stage its reveal and potentially observe the lower pair's opponent. CT could pressure the newly exposed gallery from the court or punish reduced School staging. The corner also delays follower sight; the person supporting the lower court is no longer simultaneously supporting B-west or racing across the roof.

The price is a4 m strip of School staging, extra roof journey, another orientation change and possible high-to-low pressure over a ground route that currently has fewer upper threats. The existing roof witness already takes roughly24.6 s before clearing, so added travel may make it a dedicated early investment rather than a useful response. A panoramic gallery would also weaken the lower paired route and let one height role monitor too much. A wholly opaque gallery might add travel without enough strategic benefit. This trade cannot be resolved by the attractive turn drawing alone.

**Disposition: defer NF-V01 and retain R08.** If selected later, require explicit court/gallery visibility bounds and compare a School-held early roof pair against baseline, plus the same lower Boiler2v2 from both ownership directions. Inspect S1 crest/B staging, tank-ground clearance, both flights and lower passage; compare actual travel and support before/after the turn. Another reviewer must assess any design I substantially help select. No extra stair form is needed as a quota.

Previously proposed School–aisle aperture and Tailor service court remain optional/deferred. Their potential gains still carry North-retake and early East-arrival costs; none is necessary to fix a demonstrated R08 macro failure.
