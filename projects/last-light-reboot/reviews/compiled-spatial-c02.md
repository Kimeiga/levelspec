# Last Light C02 — independent focused spatial / visibility / construction return

**Verdict: accepted; recommend adopting C02 over C01 for this bounded native local scope. No required local repair.** The curved west public stair creates a more substantial shared-atrium journey, a different order of threats and a usable lower crossing. This recommendation does not decide the separate macro timing or architecture gates and does not promote the canonical source.

- Across those54 lower centers, **none** sees C-SIDE or C-LAND. All paired-crouch nominal information rays remain blocked. The broader lower region strengthens the case for retaining O-W, while the narrow upper admission and limited body exposure keep its role modest. Opening this into general stair/landing support would materially change the encounter and is not required for acceptance. A future larger-body brief could justify a **bounded upper-pocket widening comparison** that retains the no-side/no-landing relationship; defer that experiment until such a body or multiplayer obstruction is demonstrated. Do not widen it merely to increase a sample count.

## Adoption, validation and remaining limits

Adopt the curved stair and its native construction package in this local scope. Retain the current lower passage, guard closure and tip infills. Retire the former immediate G1 information claim and retain the explicit O-W lateral move. The curve earns its place through the shared volume, changing facing and receiving choice; there is no reason to force comparable curves into the three unaffected stairs.

The final ordinary strict build result reports success, with 25 NAV_ISLAND warnings and the R-CORE/R-SK1 ROOF_STEP_OPEN warning. Final strict API and ordinary preview evidence use the same frozen source/physical/render geometry. Earlier compiler work-budget/route-validation failures are preserved history and are not current map blockers. This reviewer verified their frozen records rather than performing a redundant fresh build. UV baking remains unavailable. The nested roof warning belongs to the separate architecture judgment; the changed local stair evidence does not authorize inserting a wall.

C02 reaches the western point chain roughly4.55 s sooner than C01 and increases the public/service arrival gap to13.28 s on the nominated simultaneous departures. Earlier side exposure does not prove compensation for that timing. The independent macro return must decide that tradeoff. Target-game hero abilities, larger bodies, actor blocking, weapons/healing, respawn behavior and competitive fairness remain unverified. Finite views/rays and single-controller walkthroughs establish this blockout's supported local relationships, not multiplayer combat performance.
