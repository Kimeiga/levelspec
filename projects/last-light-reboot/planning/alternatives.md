Neither proposal is selected. They test different organizing ideas rather than alternate decorations on the old central court.

## A — architectural development hypothesis

The map's public identity is the north promenade, interrupted by deep storefronts and cinema ticketing. A large department store sits to its south, with several meaningful edges: street frontage, side shopping junction, parcel entrance and service back. The former escalator/lift core and tall display enclosures give the sales floor internal depth. They are building-scale parts of the room, not a field of unrelated crates.

West public arrival can stop in the window vestibule, move into the concourse bend, enter the store or continue through the bookshop to the promenade. The west service journey reaches the loading bay and south belt before the parcel threshold. East arrival uses the record-store frontage and arcade junction, with cinema and receiving alternatives. These routes need actual threshold baffles and measured reset volumes in the developed packet; names alone do not establish tactical independence.

The partial gallery crosses the north edge and returns into the store's western and eastern sides. It has only two ascent locations in S01. Review should test whether that creates useful commitment or an unnecessarily long reversal, and whether its sightlines overwhelm the ground retake. The proposed enclosed sales hall roof is +9.6 m; service sections may have lower independent roofs only if the vertical clearance and physical interfaces are explicitly drawn. The S01 plan does not establish those lower roofs.

## B — architectural development hypothesis

This proposal uses the food court as a raised destination reached from two different atria. The ground layer has public frontage and service circulation of its own. Passing below control is a rotation, not a scoring action. The kitchen pavilion is intended to make food-court occupancy sectional rather than allowing a single observer to see every stair arrival.

West public arrival progresses through the department threshold into the west atrium and grand stair X1; west service arrival uses back-of-house and food preparation to X2. East public arrival progresses through its department threshold into the east atrium and X3; the delivery/receiving side reaches X4 and the south gallery. The north and south upper entries give service arrivals a different final approach from the grand stairs. The lower passage is 10 m deep in plan and must retain a continuous clear envelope under the court slab.

The two atria should read as separate volumes from their ground approaches, then become comprehensible together from the food court. A +10.8 m roof/skylight envelope is proposed over the atria; the lower transfer soffit is +3.25 m. The ground destination and upper scoring destination must be recognizable without revealing enemy positions through a void. Review should challenge whether four ascents are excessive and whether both sides have viable retreat after a failed climb.

## Limits before independent comparison

These are dimensioned architectural proposals, not developed combat layouts. Upper front guards, solid cheeks, roof construction, precise portal bounds, spawn blockers, occupied standing/crouch visibility, full-team clear areas and travel times remain unverified. Adjacent polygons may describe one shared volume. S01 must not be read as a set of sealed rooms, and thin program boundaries must not be compiled mechanically into walls.

The next step is independent comparison of the raw plans and brief. Any selected idea must receive detailed sections and actual portal/visibility evidence before AML. No packet metric or favorable author description substitutes for that gate.
