# Ninefold S01 — independent spatial-interest comparison

Reviewer: root, independent of the Ninefold author. Inspected the raw brief and both
actual frozen S01 PNG drawings identified by `planning/s01-freeze.json`, the original
L04 plan/source-matched overview, and the gallery reference observations. No sibling
verdict or selected-author rationale was read. Root had communicated observations
about the original maps and saw the author's short descriptions of both alternatives;
the concrete assessment below comes from their drawings.

**A / Courtyard Stack: promising.** The ground journey can pass through Print Court
and Repair Shop before climbing toward Laundry, or stay low and change direction
around the light well toward Machine Court. The continuous middle gallery connects
two distinct ascents and reaches a separate roof precinct. This is more developed
than simply raising the original east lane. Its main uncertainty is how much of the
drawn lower/middle relationship becomes actual shared architectural volume and
bounded observation, rather than two unrelated corridor systems separated by slabs.

**B / Loft Street: promising.** The Factory Underpass sits beneath occupied Machine
Loft, while School Landing and East Landing create different entry sequences into
that upper territory. The optional Roof Court/Reservoir route reconnects those sides
at a further level. A ground objective in Porcelain Works gives the upper territory
a consequence beyond reaching another stair. The underpass, light well, enclosed
commercial spaces and roof services can read as one inhabited urban block.

B is the stronger spatial candidate for this test because the inhabited building
and through-street relationships differ more clearly from the original. This is a
provisional preference, not a strategy verdict or production gate. The independent
macro proposals may favor A or a materially revised combination.

## Opportunities to develop

**ROOT-NF-01 — selected observation across the lower/upper seam.** In B, compare a
bounded opening from School Landing toward the Lift Lightwell approach with keeping
that edge opaque. A player after S1 could gather information for a teammate still
approaching through Butcher Arcade, then choose the loft entry or roof ascent. A
ground opponent should be able to approach another edge or withdraw behind the
court/building shoulder; opening the entire gallery would risk covering all those
responses at once. Draw the aperture and its opposing positions before choosing;
do not infer the needed view from the current blue dashed annotation alone. This is
an optional local experiment, with later physical/optical verification required.

**ROOT-NF-02 — building masses must have real vertical organization.** Preserve
visible distinctions between commercial enclosure, light-well void and inaccessible
residential mass when developing either option. The inspected street photograph
shows adjoining unequal buildings with setbacks and projections; the original
overview largely reads as one perimeter block. New massing should explain how an
occupied loft belongs above its underpass and how roof equipment is reached. This
is architectural development, not a requirement to make every residential floor
playable or to scatter decorative roof props.

The proposals have enough intermediate spatial development for a detailed pass.
Required next evidence is the selected layout's actual walls/doorways, sections,
stairs and landings, opposing/supporting view predictions, and independent macro
and section-by-section review. S01 does not establish physical reachability, utility,
acoustics, timing or balance.
