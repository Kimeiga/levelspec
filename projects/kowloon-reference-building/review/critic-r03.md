# Independent fidelity re-review — r03

**Verdict: two major carryovers remain.** The window and ground-contact repairs are now satisfactory for the intended foundational recreation. The broad canopy composition is substantially restored. The remaining work is concentrated in the character of masonry aging and the missing striped awning valance.

This is a visual judgment of that export, not approval of future parameter changes or hidden architecture.

## Accepted progression

- **Windows:** Curtains, a screen, selected crosspieces, and open sections now visibly break up the repeated black banks. The separate balcony door remains coherent. These close the foundational window request. More precise sash-by-sash reconstruction is unnecessary.
- **Metal hierarchy:** The reduction in bright chips restores green frame/rail and brown metal readings. Keep this improvement; do not increase metal wear together with masonry wear.
- **Ground:** The floating comb edge is gone. Continuous curb/paving and restrained dark ground contact close that defect. Do not restore a broad raised display slab.
- **Canopy transition:** The conspicuous red fascia is removed, the main fabric is one broad canopy, and the corbels remain exposed. These close the composition regression from r02. The striped palette is back in the right family, with the valance exception below.
- **Other established work:** Four-story massing, jade/charcoal blocks, actual balcony door, fan and hinges, cage/conduit layer, broad right sign, metal tank, threshold rack, and shop stock remain accepted. The roof door is now subdued enough that a separate door-material iteration is unnecessary.

## Required carryover 1 — Change the character of masonry aging

**What improved:** Hero now clearly shows gray exposed regions on the parapet, piers, wall panels, balcony fascia, and corner/base. This is materially closer than r02's isolated faint dots.

**What still disagrees:** `upper.png` shows mostly smooth, single-tone gray islands with soft/cloud-like outlines, surrounded by broad clean cream. Their internal surface and boundaries have little visual structure. The original has layered, fragmented wear: surviving paint islands, smaller chips adjacent to large peeled regions, mottled gray/brown substrate, thin edge loss, dark runoff, and accumulated dirt around caps, ledges, corners, and fixtures. The source's cornices and fascia are also aged; r03's trim still reads unusually clean and regular. The roof hut and parapet interior remain significantly cleaner than the main facade.

**Consequence:** r03 now reads as clean plaster with gray patches painted onto it, rather than the source's old flaking masonry. This is the last large-area identity gap.

**Concrete repair:** Retain the broad cream/gray balance, but break the gray fields and their borders into multiple visible scales. Add mottled substrate, more fragmented jagged edge erosion and nearby small chips, and darker causal dirt/runoff at cap/ledge/drain junctions. Carry the same architectural aging into trim, balcony fascia, and roof hut/parapet. Avoid solving the problem by merely enlarging uniform gray islands or covering everything with uniform noise. The chipped charcoal base also needs the same irregular edge/internal breakup rather than a few smooth gray cutouts.

Increasing wall texture resolution can improve edge readability in this close view, but resolution alone does not supply the missing material pattern. Higher wear/grime parameters are an implementation hypothesis; acceptance depends on the next actual images. Keep metal's now-restrained wear independent.

## Required carryover 2 — Restore the hanging striped valance

**Evidence:** The reference shop canopy ends in an obvious red/cream hanging segmented/scalloped strip. In r03 hero/shops, the canopy still ends as a nearly flat thin metal/fabric edge. That missing silhouette remains visible even at whole-building scale.

**Concrete repair:** Add a short hanging striped fabric valance across the broad main canopy, continuing its red/cream pattern and aged material, with a segmented/scalloped lower edge. Preserve the current clear gap/corbels above it, the single broad canopy, and the distinct muted-green left canopy. Do not reintroduce the deep two-bay draping or red shop-head fascia.
