# Veilcrest — brief and mechanics

User brief: CT versus T defuse map, a secret mansion deep within very hilly mountains. “585” is interpreted as 5v5. Two objective sites, ten spawns, no respawns during a round. Original editable AML project, geometry/runtime exports and a live read-only reviewer.

The discovered harness supplies radius 0.30 m, standing height 2.0 m, crouch height 1.1 m, step 0.25 m and slope 42°. Inspection speeds are 3.4 m/s standing, 1.7 crouched; standing/crouched eyes are 1.86/1.023 m above feet. Jump impulse 5.2 m/s, gravity 20 m/s². No required jump, boost, drop, ladder or crouch-only path. Flights rise 3 m with 20 × 0.15 m risers and at least 0.4 m treads. Navigation sampling at 0.125 m.

Provisional mode assumptions: 115 s round, 40 s planted timer, 3.2 s plant, 10 s defuse or 5 s with a kit. These are design assumptions, not implemented game settings. Hitscan rifles, one scoped long-range weapon available per side, finite smoke/flash/frag utility. No weapon penetration, destructibility, grenade, economy, audio or multiplayer logic is supplied by the reviewer. Optical occluders and collision are explicit geometry; material names confer no ballistic behavior.

Five attackers can stage on the lower road then choose the west switchback, front foyer or east drainage approach. Five defenders can split A/B and contest the hall, with the rear court as their rotation. Assess full 5v5, paired entries, an isolated anchor, 2v2 post-plant and low-utility 1v2 retakes. No balance claim without actual players.
