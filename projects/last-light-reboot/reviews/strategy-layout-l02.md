## Raw assessment before author explanation

Inspected repair-comparison-l02.png, sections-l02-5.png, ground/upper L02 PNGs, exact developed-l02.json and provenance before layout-l02.md or local-review rationale. Compared changed objects/routes directly with L01. Reuse the unchanged objective, ascent, upper-front and macro assessment in strategy-layout-l01.md.

- SW-VR closes the west public vestibule's northward line and sends public departure around SW-B1's east aisle. It retains the outside west service switch rather than sealing it with an outer wall.
- SE-VR separates E-REG's service view from its public view, but moving outside between those departures now goes south around SE-B2. This is a real extra regroup commitment even though the normal east-service opening chain is unchanged.
- Both repairs separate the close, shared observing stations; neither drawing removes all deep pressure near the doors. A response must identify teammates and the enemy's committed position, not count two doors as automatic counterplay.
- Added roof-step walls are overhead closures. They change no floor connection or objective claim in the exact data; no macro reinterpretation is necessary for those changes.

All observations are drawing hypotheses. Named eye rays and body route samples cannot establish firing, healing, escape success or target-game spawn protection.

## Verdict — accepted for the affected L02 paper strategy scope

The repairs preserve both home departures and their outside switching while making a nearby opponent commit to one frontier. Their costs are explicit and proportionate. Reuse L01's accepted macro scope and deferred opportunities; no strategic geometry change is required by this return. Independent local acceptance was reported in the assignment, but its rationale was not consulted for this judgment. Compiled and target-engine gates remain open.

**Retained tradeoff:** do not add another partition simply to erase every near-door dual view. The current repair removes the cheap shared station while preserving physically different departures. A stronger closure could repeat the rejected loss of outside switching.

**Concrete next comparison:** in compiled/engine review, put one opponent at the deep intruder and a second at the corresponding receiving approach; compare a paired internal challenge with a full-wave sortie from the other departure. Record the support's same-threat view, the intruder's withdrawal, and the time before either side can rejoin the point. Also follow each survivor around the east stock return; named endpoint visibility alone does not demonstrate support during the switch. This is later validation, not a new selected paper-layout experiment.
