## Scenario conclusions and remaining work

**Opening / reinforcement asymmetry:** retain R01 for testing instead of inserting a geometry delay solely to equalize marker distances. West approach/roof views show a supported fight before standing on the central marker; no compiled evidence establishes an E position sealing both W choices before any contest. Nonetheless the2–3s scoring-entry and roughly3s reinforcement gap is material, including after opening. Neither25s unlock nor side swaps proves fairness. First playtest should compare E early cupboard occupation against a coordinated W court/roof approach, then reverse player sides.

**Roof hold / retake:** two roof players gain useful information but spend two bodies off point, divide their frontage at TEA-SCREEN and must attend to two stair entries. A3+2 retake can attack the ground cupboard while a second pair pressures an ascent or Book Walk. Actual views establish those opportunities, not retake success. The broad west-front scoring view is a high-priority combat test, especially for ranged damage/healing.

**3v5 / full wipe:** ceding outer streets and awaiting a wave is plausible when enough percentage remains; returning all the way to the protected yard adds travel again. At proposed1%/s, marker-return estimates plus0–10s wave wait are approximatelyW16–27% and E13–24% free scoring, before enemy delay. First scoring-boundary entry can occur earlier. Repeated recovery and late stagger behavior need playtests; overtime and contest/touch rules are external, so no optimal last-second recommendation is accepted.

**Spawn push:** both close holds see two departures. The large attention turn between thresholds, possible counterfire from five fresh players and the cost of pushing away from point prevent rays alone from proving an irreversible lock. Those costs also do not prove recovery. Spawn protection/exclusion and practical recovery must be implemented/tested in the intended engine. If play demonstrates one close position suppressing both exits despite protection, revise that local yard/interface while retaining the district around it.

Required next evidence is target-engine5v5 play: opening/recovery asymmetry,2-roof/3-ground hold versus paired retake, close spawn pressure, repeated wipes and expensive reset choices. Hero sizes/abilities, bullet/utility/audio rules, destructibility, healing restrictions, multiplayer blocking, scoring/waves/overtime and protected-volume behavior remain unverified. No human playtest or combat simulation occurred. Static validation, sampled optics, camera views and constrained replay cannot establish competitive balance or destination-engine continuous collision.
