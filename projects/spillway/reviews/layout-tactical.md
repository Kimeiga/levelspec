Acceptance schedules the checks below; it does not establish balanced gameplay, actual trade windows, utility effectiveness, clearance or continuous reachability.

The meaningful territories are the outer approaches, lower mid, ground short connectors, upper bridge and rear gallery. Their value changes with objective ownership. The bridge has routes to both sides but its north screen prevents it from simply watching every defensive connector. Teams can concede it while defending sites, or contest its ends from nearby site territory. Ground mid remains consequential independently of the bridge.

## Relationships to retain or clarify

- **S-02 / A-short:** the filled 4 m flight occupies x26..30, y66..82, inside the eastern edge of A. A ground approach arriving from the A-short corridor at y72..78 does not have an unobstructed straight line into A through that solid flight and its future enclosures. Its useful continuation may be around the north end toward the rear, or via another approach. This is an acceptable distinct commitment, but the map notes and route checks must not advertise a direct ground opening through the stairs. Check how a mid teammate can contribute during an A gate entry.
- **U-V / M-01:** the upper bay points south while M-01 interrupts part of the lower approach. A 1 m parapet with the provisional 1.05 m low eye can create a very small apparent exposure. Inspect near-edge and backed-off upper poses, reverse ground views, actual body/weapon assumptions and the spectator-camera limitation. Avoid presenting that low camera as tested crouch movement or a fair duel.
- **Bridge opportunity cost:** screens limit broad surveillance, while committing a defender to an end weakens coverage elsewhere. Retain this choice. An extra sight window or new unguarded drop would change that assessment and requires a focused recheck.
- **No upper monopoly established:** the bridge is useful but neither objective requires it. Ground site plans remain viable in the diagram. Whether the upper path becomes mandatory in practice is a playtest question, not a finding from the plan.
