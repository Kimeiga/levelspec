# Galini architectural image study

Inspiration mode, not source-fidelity reconstruction. The four retained photographs were opened and visually inspected on 2026-11-09. They establish architectural relationships and material/settlement vocabulary; no dimensions, population, map timings or gameplay properties are measured from them. `sources.json` retains exact image/page URLs, local paths, bytes hashes and credits. These are research images, not licensed game textures.

No photographic reference folder was found in the original Galini project. The old L04 plan and B09 cutaway were inspected as historical authoring evidence, not as real-world architectural references.

## 1. Symi harbor / cliff settlement

[Photo](images/symi-harbor.jpg) · [source page](https://www.lato.at/symi/index.php)

Observed: houses occupy successive heights above a narrow shore, with substantial masonry terraces and retaining faces. Stairs belong to the spaces between built volumes. The lower harbor and upper dwelling fronts are close in projection while access bends around intervening houses and rock. Roofs form a continuous populated hillside, not a few isolated facade screens.

Adopted relationship: Harbor Fold's waterfront/market/upper civic terraces and short cross-level views with separate access. This is an invented organization, not a copy of Symi's street plan. Do not infer a playable roof simply because roofs are visible.

## 2. Kali Strata stairs, Symi

[Photo](images/symi-stair.jpg) · [Aegean Islands page](https://www.aegeanislands.gr/pois/symi-kali-strata-en/)

Observed: external stairs widen at landings, turn alongside a yellow building and sit behind a substantial white retaining/parapet wall. A bell tower remains visible beyond. Building walls, stair walls and street edges belong to one architectural system. The image watermark reads SymiGreece.com / June 2010.

Adopted relationship: stair arrivals should be small inhabited porch/court places with exits, not ramps deposited in a blank pad. The drawn 4 m rises and 12–14 m runs are gameplay assumptions. The photograph does not establish these dimensions or adequate two-player circulation.

## 3. Monemvasia town and cliff

[Photo](images/monemvasia-town.jpg) · [Visit Greece page](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/mainland/peloponnese/monemvasia)

Observed: dense full building volumes and tiled roofs establish narrow crooked streets. Courts are localized openings within a continuous settlement. A large rough cliff behind and seaward wall in front constrain the town. The visible settlement has repeated roof families without an identical grid of buildings.

Adopted relationship: use massing to create the playable void, and let nonplayable town/rock explain boundaries. Both S01 drawings retain substantial building footprints and a site edge; later architecture must make complete volumes and roof continuation. The scenic ravine in B is an invention, not claimed to exist in the photographed town.

## 4. Monemvasia stair and lower arch

[Photo](images/monemvasia-passage.jpg) · [Visit Peloponnese page](https://visitpeloponnese.com/en/prdct/monemvasia)

Observed: a masonry stair runs to one side while a lower arch continues beneath an upper landing/building. The upper doorway and lower passage share a substantial envelope. Near/far silhouettes arise from actual thickness and occupancy, not from a decorative arch standing alone.

Adopted relationship: roofed through-building paths at the winery, taverna gateway and inn archway; stairs can arrive beside a passage within a shared inhabited mass. No unmeasured underpass headroom is assumed. The Phase A sections reserve 3.0 m clear height as an invented starting constraint; later AML must verify it.

## Consequences for the reboot

- Streets are the space between buildings with usable fronts; courts are occasional wider places within that fabric.
- A change of level should arrive in a different district or porch, with a local decision and an understandable retaining condition.
- Roofed passages are reserved for consequential connections, with explicit entry and exit, not scattered decoration.
- Scenic cliff, sea and off-map town justify the envelope. The sea does not become a gameplay route.
- Cohesion should come from plaster/stone retaining walls, plausible repeated roof forms, porches and harbor uses. These references do not justify an arbitrary jumble of stylistic props.

These are author interpretations to test against independent spatial proposals. They are not proof of map quality or balance.
