# User-selected size and substance benchmarks

For an ordinary whole-map 5v5/6v6 brief, default to a developed amount of playable
space and content comparable to these user-selected examples, rather than a small
prototype with only a handful of rooms and short links. Honor explicitly compact
briefs and explain a materially smaller proposal through its actual spatial depth.

These references calibrate extent and substance only. Do not copy their topology,
architectural style, objective arrangement, room shapes, crossing scheme or elevation
recipe. Invent the new map's organization for its own brief.

| Example | Approximate XY floor envelope | Substance to compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Spillway](../../../../projects/spillway/README.md) | 84 × 92 m | Its [declared graph](../../../../projects/spillway/audits/node-graph.md) contains 19 named spaces and 25 connections, with developed intermediate approaches and occupied upper circulation. |
| [Expanded observatory R02 sketch](../../../../reference/map-sizing/observatory-r02.svg) | 142 × 83 m, as declared in the sketch | About 25 named rooms/courts/gallery spaces plus consequential junctions, long circulation and quieter intermediate journeys. This is a rough sketch, not a compiled or gameplay-validated map. |
| [Ninefold, the Kowloon-inspired map](../../../../projects/ninefold/README.md) | 53 × 75 m across authored walking-floor profiles | A denser network of winding passages, intermediate pockets and five stair flights across three occupied elevations. Its compact footprint carries more development than a few open plazas would. |

Dimensions are context, not minimum rectangle sizes, floor-area measurements or
travel-time targets. The graph counts use different abstractions and are not quotas.
The observatory example endorses its amount of content, not every aspect of its layout.

Compare the proposed journeys and amount of developed space: intermediate rooms,
halls, streets/courts, route choices, transitions, separation and usable levels.
Dense interior proposals may occupy less ground; open proposals may need more
separation and developed circulation. More empty area or obstacles cannot replace
missing spatial substance. Apply [size and encounter pacing](../SKILL.md#size-and-encounter-pacing)
to the actual mode, population and movement assumptions.

The initial interest review should flag a clearly thinner whole-map proposal even
when its outline is attractive or non-grid. Keep this a rough scope comparison;
later stages verify traversal, contact and rotation costs. None of these examples
establishes competitive balance or an approved layout template.
