# Apogee design hypotheses

The retained random seed suggested an offset west summit and clustered east lab wing. The organized plan replaces raw links with shared airlocks, instrument rooms and a protected underpass. Compared a flat ring with sites north/south and a symmetric fork; selected separated elevations and unequal site shapes so routes differ in information and commitment. Other local maps use urban alleys/castle walls/industrial courts; this design uses a broad octagonal dome, lower lab suite and northern stacked rotation, rather than copying their central crossing.

T can probe Calibration then commit up Optics to A or through Cleanroom/Data to B without returning to spawn. Rim gives a separate A staging room but spends more travel time; a partner at Optics can divide site attention. Telescope pier blocks front/rear sweeps; rear and bridge entries support distinct retakes, with rear Control exposed once the dome is lost. B can be split between airlock and Data; Cryo chamber protects only one side at a time, with the plant strip east of it. Control/Decon offer distinct exits but need teammate coverage. CT protected but costly rotations use the north bridge/operations/stair sequence; taking lower Transit/Archive risks late flanks into that rotation. No observation-only crossing is counted as movement.

Cover sequences: Service→Rim→A airlock→west instrument bench; Mid low bench→Optics spectrometer→front/rear A benches; Equipment screen→Snow refuges→B airlock→Cryo corners/site benches; Data server spine→support bench→Archive refuge; bridge two staggered refuges. Tall screens do not replace these low refuges. Low cover can be defeated from a side or an upper angle; inspect both standing and crouching.

Prioritized multiplayer tests: (1) first contact/CT setup margin at each site with 5T rush; (2) 4+1 B airlock/Data execute and defensive retreat; (3) 2v2 A postplant with rear vs bridge retake; (4) late rotation from lost site, measuring actual timer feasibility; (5) learned sniper holds in Transit and A rear. Do not claim balance until target-engine play.

Layout timing review estimates the protected A↔B rotation at100–110m, 29–33s at harness speed before clearing. That route is a commitment, not a last-second universal retake. Measure target-engine rotate+clear+defuse against the chosen clock before competitive acceptance.
