# Second Friday — revised brief

Create a new 5v5 hero-shooter map, themed after a flea market in a Middle Eastern country. User correction: make the map bigger and more complex; it is outdoors; first place buildings, then shape the map as spaces between buildings.

B01 responds with an invented Amman, Jordan district, 136×108m (the superseded compact proposal was84×56m). Eleven freestanding building footprints were placed first, defining crooked streets, alleys and connected outdoor market courts. No playable building interiors. The only upper route is one tea-house roof with two external stairs. The source drawing is sketch.svg, with actual footprints, heights, anchors and provisional journeys. All tan space is outdoor floor; dark polygons are inaccessible solid buildings. Spawn courts are outdoors at opposite NW/SE corners. Single-point control is the chosen mode assumption; target hero roster/engine unknown.

Old compact studies and reviews are archival only, not acceptance for the new building-first design.
