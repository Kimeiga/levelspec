# Roast-chicken restaurant study

Themed invention, not a reconstruction of a particular shop. The photographs
inform practical relationships; dimensions and period details remain authored.

- [Joy Hing, Will Fly for Food](https://www.willflyforfood.net/hong-kong-food-guide-19-must-eat-restaurants-street-food-stalls-in-hong-kong/), inspection copy `generated/restaurant-props/references/joy-hing.jpg`: inspected actual photograph. Hanging dark roast meats and trays form a layered display to one side of the service opening. Uneven piles, hanging packaging, small storage appliances and containers use lower space; the chopping/serving station is a working area, not a merchandise shelf. Warm task lighting and cooler street light separate surfaces. Modern equipment/signs are context rather than period assets to copy.
- [Lin Heung Kui, Journey.tw](https://journey.tw/lin-heung-kui/), inspection copy `generated/restaurant-props/references/lin-heung.jpg`: inspected actual photograph. Golden chicken and dark roast bird have visibly different proportions and finishes; their hooks have different drops. A steel tray groups sauce pots, a squeeze bottle and tiny bowls. Repeated shelves and bowls are orderly, but stocking is not uniform. Broad wiped counter areas contrast with accumulated grime at frames, seams and the hood.

Source photographs remain study-only, never exported as material textures. The
existing screenshot `generated/shops-dev/cap3/roast-wide.png` is historical baseline
evidence, not a claim that it matches today's source exactly. The new project
capture records actual current production geometry separately.

## Direction

Keep the recognizable compact roast-meat/chicken takeaway. Prioritize the food
silhouettes, steel workbench, ceramic tile, chopping block and condiment clusters.
Reuse approved jars, bottles, boxes, sacks, scale and stool where appropriate;
script exact rails, hooks, shelving, light/fan parts, cookware and supports.
Use small shared food meshes and compact PBR maps, with real hook contact and
uniform fitting. Reserve the entrance and chopping surface. Preserve the trade's
room envelope and rendering-only contract.
