# Design-development review

Use this during new-map exploration, developed layout and compiled blockout review.
Reviewers proactively propose changes that could improve strategies, encounters and
spatial relationships. The [review skill](../SKILL.md) owns evidence and verdicts;
the [workflow](../../fps-map-workflow/SKILL.md) owns when to revise and advance.

## Assign complementary perspectives

For new whole maps and broad redesigns, distribute work across a wider panel of
independent agents. Use several macro perspectives and several local encounter
assignments, chosen for the actual mode and map. Useful assignments include the
following. They describe questions to investigate, with freedom to invent solutions.

| Perspective | Design opportunities to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| Territory and rotations | Changes of plan after contact, access to key territory, reinforcement, staging and the cost of switching objectives. |
| Opposing plans and information | Credible fakes, information gathering, adaptation to a learned setup, ways to contest a dominant strategy, and the attention each response consumes. |
| Spatial composition and architecture | Shared volumes, relationships between routes, massing, enclosure and terrain, informed by actual reference images. Challenge an underlying organization that limits play. |
| Local encounters | A site and its approaches, a junction, a hold, or an exit sequence. Examine leader/follower support, crossfires, preparation, repositioning and opposing responses. |
| Movement and vertical interfaces | Ascents, descents, crossings and ability-dependent routes. Relate access and departure to exposure, costs and neighboring control. |

Split local assignments by meaningful encounters, with enough adjacent context to
see consequences. Assign shared interfaces explicitly so boundaries between tasks
receive attention. Section reviewers may also own the detailed visibility scope
required by the review skill. Retain independent macro spatial and strategy coverage
and the architectural scope required for whole maps. Run agents in waves when slots
are limited. A local repair uses the relevant perspectives at the encounter's scale.
For a bounded standalone critique, honor its assigned scope and report the coverage
performed. A proposed broad alternative can include a follow-up review roster;
suggesting it alone does not expand the current assignment into a full redesign.

Provide the same raw revision, brief, user constraints and movement assumptions,
plus the references and views relevant to each assignment. Let agents record initial
observations and proposals independently, before reading author rationale or sibling
verdicts. When multiple candidates exist, use contrasting assignments or independent
comparisons before selecting a direction. The author can then share rationale and
invite discussion of conflicts.

Existing briefs or source labels may already contain design defenses or historical
verdicts. Identify that exposure, distinguish explicit user requirements from author
claims, and assess the available geometry and scenarios independently. Treat embedded
claims as hypotheses requiring evidence and describe the review's actual independence.

## Propose a changed experience

Ask for the strongest supported opportunities, even where current checks pass. An
agent may recommend retaining a relationship when it has no worthwhile improvement.
Preserve intentional strengths, commitments and quiet preparation where they serve
the brief. A proposal can remove a route, increase a meaningful cost, move an
objective, reshape a shared volume or reorganize an entire journey.

For each consequential proposal, record a short ID and the following information.

- The inspected revision, affected areas/connections and proposed physical change.
- A concrete situation before and after the change, including player positions,
  objective state and the decision it enables or improves.
- How teammates can exploit it and how opponents can respond with the available
  information, time, movement and resources.
- What the change sacrifices or risks, including neighboring encounters, other
  territory, clarity, exposure, travel and architectural coherence.
- Supporting observations, remaining assumptions, and the comparison or experiment
  that would establish whether the intended benefit occurs.

Show a marked plan, section or graph delta when the change is difficult to explain
in prose. For example, a proposed flank should identify its entry and exit, when it
becomes useful, and what defenders give up to cover it. Selective openings and shared
volumes need actual boundaries and opposing views. Revisit reference photographs for
new architectural relationships or unfamiliar construction.

Distinguish an observed defect, a design opportunity and a proposed experiment.
Keep the existing stage's evidence standards. At sketch stage, use provisional
spatial relationships. At blockout, inspect actual geometry and views. Preserve
uncertainty about mechanics that need engine verification. Proposal counts and
reviewer agreement do not establish design quality or competitive balance.

## Select and test coherent alternatives

The author collects independent proposals, groups duplicates, and identifies
conflicts and dependencies before editing the canonical map. Review agents can
draw or prototype alternatives in separate working locations within the authorized
scope. Keep a frozen baseline for comparisons.

Choose coherent combinations using the brief, architectural references and expected
player decisions. Retain a credible competing version when evidence is insufficient
to select between ideas. Record each consequential proposal as adopted, selected for
an experiment, deferred or declined, with a short reason. A supported defect retains
its required review finding until resolved. An optional opportunity becomes part of
the current work when selected. Agreement alone is insufficient reason to adopt it.

Prototype selected changes at the smallest useful scale. Draw changed layout
relationships before production edits, then recheck the implemented approach,
occupation, use and departure from both sides, including follower support and
neighboring threats. Review macro route and information effects when a local edit
changes them. Reconcile any graph with the physical connections actually created.

Have another reviewer assess the consequences of substantial contributions from
a proposal author. Reuse unaffected evidence and repeat the affected required
scopes. At handoff, selected experiments should be resolved and supported defects
closed at the current evidence level. Retain optional ideas and remaining engine
questions for later tests. Further proposals alone need not restart the round.
