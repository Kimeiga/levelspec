# Fusion — source brief and raw observations

User: apply the strategic FPS research to level-making skills, then push LevelSpec
with a new map named Fusion, based on ITER. Model the reactor toroid accurately,
balance its scale with a substantial map and micro opportunities/creative strategy,
and incorporate it into play, for example a partially finished circular hallway.
Confirmed mode: 5v5 tactical, two objectives.

## Reference evidence

See ../references/sources.json and original images. ITER images remain reference
evidence, not game textures. They are credited to ITER Organization / the attributed
supplier; assembly gallery material is CC BY-NC-ND 3.0. No image edits were made.

- `vessel-0.webp`: official cutaway shows a tall D-like chamber section, a straight
  inner wall, curved outer bulge, upper/lower lobes and equatorial rectangular ports.
  It is not a circular pipe cross-section. Central hole remains distinct from plasma space.
- `vessel-1.webp`: fabrication photograph shows sector seams, stiffeners, substantial
  port collars, temporary work platforms and workshop glazing.
- `assembly-0.webp`: actual cylindrical concrete pit, radial port cells, service
  ring, overhead yellow crane and local scaffold access. The ring geometry has an
  architectural cause. Hall axes and lifting clearance justify rectilinear surroundings.
- `assembly-2.webp`: large D-shaped sector tooling in a tall roofed assembly hall;
  lower work decks are human-scaled against enormous machinery. Galleries overlook
  a shared volume without requiring a traversable roof on the reactor.

Published dimensions: vessel outer diameter 19.4 m, height 11.4 m, nine 40-degree
sectors (ITER vacuum-vessel page and F4E poster). Eighteen D-shaped toroidal field
coils, each 9 x 17 m; central solenoid 4 m wide and 13 m tall excluding support;
cryostat nearly 30 m across. See https://www.iter.org/machine/vacuum-vessel,
https://www.iter.org/machine/magnets and https://www.iter.org/machine/cryostat.

Accuracy boundary: retain published reactor envelopes and D-section/radial topology;
infer unprovided intermediate section coordinates from the cutaway. Do not claim
manufacturing CAD accuracy. The map is a fictional unfinished assembly state,
with temporary decks and selected missing shell/cryostat segments. Surrounding
site, objective placement and passage connections are invented for play.
