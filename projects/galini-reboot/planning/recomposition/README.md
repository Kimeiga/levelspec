# R02 contour-town recomposition

Root found the L01 developed draft had regressed into broad terrace pads and eight repeated parallel flights. R02 is the architectural response, not a closed detailed gate. Read the actual `composition.png`, then aligned `floor-0.png`, `floor-4.png`, `floor-6.png` and `floor-8.png`. SVGs and coordinate data are retained alongside.

The two shoulders, middle/upper crossings and ground shore end-run survive. Actual journeys change: Laundry undercroft returns through a stair inside the house to an upper frontage; the civic ascent occupies a +6 m chapel porch and travels around Chapel; Press enters West Homes from below and exits onto its A-facing street; School contains a useful lower arcade beneath an occupied upper floor, plus an internal stair; Bell has a public turning ascent; Customs becomes a sloped cart road.

The old L01 packet remains under `../developed/` solely for comparison and is marked superseded. R02 needs the revised detailed boundary/opening/guard/section/route packet after the recomposed relationships are inspected. It is not production AML, physical proof or balance acceptance.
