## Consequential corrections to design predictions

**Spawn pressure is not defeated geometrically by the mere existence of two routes.** In plan, a nearby opponent around `(14,82)` has a view north up the west strip toward both W yard exit thresholds. Around `(131,20)`, an opponent can see toward both E yard's western departure and northern service-road departure. These are close local holds, not score-to-spawn leaks: B10 and B04 plausibly screen the actual protected markers from the scoring area and roof. Once a player rounds the far side of B10/B04, the building can separate the routes again. The packet's universal claim that one close corner cannot cover both exits should be replaced by this conditional relationship. This does not require geometry repair under the stated engine-protected-yard assumption; close-hold, exit support and protection boundaries remain mandatory engine/playtest questions. Avoid claiming that spawn markers or opaque buildings implement team-filtered protection.

**Tea's west bay can influence most of the point.** A standing observer near `(64,78.5,4.874)` can aim obliquely toward most of x76–90/y56–70 without crossing the central south screen x66–74. B06 lies southwest of the scoring rectangle and does not generally block these roof-to-point rays. AUCTION-CABINET can hide a subset of lower positions, but a frontage screen does not restrict each remaining bay to a narrow sector. Accept this as a deliberate high-ground risk for compiled counterfire/ascent examination, rather than asserting B06 provides point protection. The bay still does not score; its stairs require attention and standing users expose upper body above the1.15m parapet. Whether those responses are sufficient needs actual view and timing evidence.

The author accepted both corrections in the B02 design packet before production and withdrew the independent-exit guarantee. They clarify predictions without prescribing new props or changing the outdoor district. The subsequently specified cloth-canopy bounds (clothes, tailor, appliance, books; underside4.3–4.4m; corner posts radius0.12m) do not close a mandatory passage in plan. Their actual optical effect and post clearances remain compiled checks.
