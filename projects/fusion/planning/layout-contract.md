# Fusion layout contract and proposal dispositions — L03 / B04

Read `brief.md`, `layout-l03.svg` and references first. This records the developed
layout and its B04 construction deltas; revision-matched acceptance lives in the reviews. Candidate A was selected
after independent spatial, organic and strategic comparison. All coordinates are
meters. Reactor axis is (0,4), Z=0 is the temporary equatorial work deck.

## Selected changes

- Adopt S01 spatial/organic open-sector proposal: five 40° full vessel sectors
  from 0° to 200°; four lower-shell sectors complete the bottom, while the temporary
  annular deck continues through the open southern/eastern arc. Fictional assembly state.
- Adopt differentiated journeys and separate objective entries: A from Cask south,
  west-port east work bay, and offset rear; B from the lowered bus approach south,
  east-port west bay and offset rear. The reactor does not funnel into an outer
  route's final doorway. No fourth port into Rear Services.
- Adopt interrupted annulus: the area outside the vacuum vessel contains field
  coils and inaccessible assembly pit, with only three deck bridges. It is not a
  second full walking circuit. Cylindrical concrete shielding contains the pit.
- Adopt bounded west gallery: Cask stair reaches +3.2m; a port-facing view and
  A-facing view are separated by a tall screen. A second stair connects to A at
  ground level. Rear control contains no view of both sites.
- Adopt offset rear arrival screens at A and B. Protected rear travel does not
  include uncontested objective influence. Compare it with the risky ring transfer.
- Decline a second whole-hall machine/continuous crescent gallery for this revision:
  they would compete with the vessel as the map's central relationship and add
  continuous upper control. Retain as future alternatives, not current experiments.

## Review scenarios and predicted relationships

1. T split: Cask entry with a partner through W port. Removing the partner should
   change the clearing plan; gallery support requires an additional player and access cost.
2. Reactor pair: south junction controlled by opponents; compare north-arc bypass
   with contested southern arc. The central core blocks diametric views; oblique chords are not assumed occluded.
   Actual curved corridor views still need compiled inspection.
3. Information: gallery observer sees W-port arrival; a staged teammate can act
   immediately, but the observer's own stair return has substantial delay. Test
   support alive, suppressed and absent; no automatic speed-up is prescribed.
4. Fake and rotation: D at (0,30) responds through rear services, or gains a port
   to take the contested ring. After planting, rear screens require a new entry
   contest. A/B plant positions must not be visible from rear circulation itself.
5. Casualty state: 2v2 after a plant; evaluate two locally separated retake arrivals
   without assuming spare players cover every ring port. Separate arrivals require survivors already in different territories or sufficient time to capture a port; two players both in Rear Services cannot assume a timely opposite-port flank. Bypass gallery when the
   remaining clock makes acquisition unattractive.

Predictions are hypotheses. Standing/crouch eye heights 1.674/1.023, FOV 75°.

The reactor-hall wall at x−18 has a ground door y1.5..6.5, Z0..3.2 and an upper support aperture y0..5, Z4.3..6.2. These are different openings. The A-facing gallery edge remains guarded.
