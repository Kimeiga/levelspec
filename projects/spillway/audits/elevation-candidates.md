# Spillway — independent local elevation candidates

2026-11-08. Design critique against the current R05 source/proposal and user request for elevation that changes visibility and micro-positioning. No AML/source geometry was edited. These are candidates for drawing, independent spatial/tactical layout review, then compiled paired-view and route testing; they are not accepted geometry.

The R05 tall building masses, alternating LOS baffles and low-cover sequence should remain. Elevation changes should not restore a global upper perch or force jumping. Two distinct local changes are a useful first scope; the third below is an alternative if its construction/commitment tradeoff is desired.

## A: a shallow raised plant platform

- `A-dais`: XY X18..24,Y70..77, top Z+.60. It preserves the current A plant footprint. Move `site-a` to(21,74,.60) and paint the plant face on this new floor, not on buried ground.
- `A-up-south`: axis(21,68,0)→(21,70,.60), width6m,3risers of nominal.20m. Filled body; open endpoint interfaces.
- `A-down-north`: axis(21,77,.60)→(21,79,0), width6m,3nominalrisers. No assumed lateral jump access.
- Move `A-L01` from X21..24,Y67..69 to X12..15,Y68..70, retaining its3×2×1.1m dimensions. Its old footprint would overlap the south stair by1m.
- Move `A-02` to X18..21,Y80..82, baseZ0,height1.1. This clears the north stair and leaves its right3m approach open. Useful north-foot probe:(22.5,79.5,0); south-foot probe:(19.5,67.5,0).
- Keep `A-01` at ground Z0/top2.6. The platform standing-eye study is Z2.2, still below that screen; the platform does not become a view over every full-height crate. `A-02` remains low-ground cover, deliberately more exposed from the platform.

**Strategic hypothesis:** a plant/retake team can choose the raised platform's view over nearby low cover or stay lower to conceal more of its body. Three shallow steps change how the two players follow/trade and how a holder reveals themselves while stepping up. It does not confer a free view through the tall building strips or through S-02's solid body. After a plant, a lower rear holder and a raised objective player cover different relationships rather than simply occupying identical flat cover.

**Construction checks:** subtract plateau/stair footprints from the old ground walking contract, use solid support beneath the platform, physically join both stair ends and keep the .60m side ledges intentional. Native stair endpoint/riser semantics and serialized dimensions must be checked. Do not advertise the side ledges as ordinary .25m-step routes. Retain the filled upper S-02 and its existing base/landing clearances.

**Views/tests:** compare lower approach eyes atZ1.6/1.05 with plateau eyesZ2.2/1.65; include both sides of A-01, A-02, the south gate and rear gallery. Trace a two-person south entry and north retake; verify the follower's actual view before calling the elevation useful. Test a1v2post-plant with a lower attacker instead of assuming all players prefer the dais.

## East: a traversable raised saddle between the baffles

- Modified strip X82..90,Y40..50, with nominal usable interior X82.2..89.8 after the existing boundary walls.
- `E-south-ramp`: Y40→44, top0→1.20, width8m centeredX86. Nominal grade16.7°. Use filled support, not an inaccessible low pocket beneath a thin ramp.
- `E-crest`: X82..90,Y44..46, topZ1.20.
- `E-north-ramp`: Y46→50, top1.20→0, same width. The ground returns toZ0well before R-01's east foot atY61.
- Move `E-L01` to X82.2..84.6,Y44..46, baseZ1.20,height1.1. The low cover stays supported on the flat crest; its originalY42..44 footprint would straddle the south slope.
- Leave `E-L02` at its current ground positionY54..56. `E-02` stays baseZ0/top4.5; part of its footprint is buried by the descending surface, and it must remain opaque over the higher eye. Crest standing eyesZ2.8 remain below that4.5m baffle and the3m outer boundary.

**Strategic hypothesis:** the baffles still establish the main approach sequence, while the saddle changes what part of a moving player's body appears, the view over the low cover, and whether the following player can support from lower ground. Ducking behind the crest cover and standing over it have different exposure. The opponent can hold the farther turn or concede the crest and contest its exit, rather than being forced into one long-range duel.

**Construction checks:** replace the old ground surface in this strip; keep both ramps physically supported and joined, account for boundary wall bases and E-02's intersection, and update floor-relative cover heights. Exact body/eye settings matter; no generic claim that the higher player always wins. Do not let a raised floor accidentally make a3m wall a climbable roof shortcut.

**Views/tests:** paired ascent/descent views atY41,43,45,47and49; standing/low eyes on the crest and both lower approaches. Recheck all protected spawn-area rays, both long-route baffle gaps and the corridor-to-B/R-01 transitions. Inspect the first and second player's exposure continuously through the transition where possible.

## Optional alternative: lower-mid preparation pocket

- `M-low-pocket`: X44..52,Y32..38, topZ-.60.
- South stair X46..50,Y30..32 descends0→-.60; north stair X46..50,Y38..40 ascends-.60→0. Three nominal.20m risers each, width4m.
- Retain high bypasses west atX40..44and east atX52..60. The pocket is an optional commitment, not a mandatory funnel.
- A dedicated low obstacle could sit X44..46,Y34..36,baseZ-.60,height1.1; its low-camera shelter must be tested from the higher rim.

**Strategic hypothesis:** lower preparation can hide more of the body and limit observation; a teammate remaining on the rim supplies information/support. Leaving the pocket creates an explicit climb/reveal cost, and higher opponents may expose low-cover users. This differs from merely adding another raised perch.

**Risk:** this option needs a real cut through the existing .30m slab, a deeper supported floor, retaining faces and two carefully joined stair openings. It has more construction risk than the first two candidates and can become a grenade trap or an unused delay. It should be omitted if there is no meaningful information/preparation benefit after actual review.

## Acceptance limits

These changes create distinct local eye/body relationships; height variation by itself is not a quality metric. Long-LOS and100°entry warnings remain suggestions for review. Hidden entry portals, an aerial image or a lower risk-flag count cannot establish that a position requires looking behind. Recheck first-appearance frontiers, objective value, support, timing and opponents choosing not to contest the attractive height.

No multiplayer, crouch movement, grenade, penetration, sound, jump/boost or competitive balance testing was performed for these candidates.
