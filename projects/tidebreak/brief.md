# Tidebreak — exploration trial

User constraints: test deterministic graph and spatial generation as a way to disrupt habitual AI planning. Make the final map coherent through real architectural image study and independent macro/local proposals. The first sketch and generated topology are disposable. No engine, symmetry, route quota, or theme was imposed.

Trial assumptions (invented, adjustable): two teams of five, round-based objective pressure around two key zones; no respawn during a round. Ordinary walking, ramps and stairs; body radius 0.35 m, height 1.8 m, crouch 1.1 m, step 0.25 m, slope at most42°, estimated walking speed3.4m/s. No required gap jump, ladder, vent, vehicle, utility or game-specific ability. Exact weapons, economy, round clock, objective interaction and balance remain untested.

Setting: a working repair yard containing a sunken graving dock and one shared covered slip. Ships, retaining walls, apron space, partial galleries and industrial thresholds create territories. This is invented architecture informed by Chatham, Turks and Rochefort photographs, not a reconstruction.

Evidence: `planning/experiment.json` preserves seed shipyard-07, four restarts ×2000 proposals, four graph alternatives and twelve spatial hypotheses. `planning/alternatives.png` is the actual comparison board inspected before selecting a developed proposal. `references/study.md` records six original photographs actually inspected and independent architecture alternatives. Original research cache: `generated/exploration-reference/` (images are references, not shipped assets).

Proposal R01: playable limits roughly x[-46,48], y[-40,44], elevations-4/0/+4 meters. Sources use meters and right-handed Z-up. SVG drawing labels define intended interfaces; final geometry and navigation must verify them. Spawn/objective markers are provisional design anchors rather than certified balance.
