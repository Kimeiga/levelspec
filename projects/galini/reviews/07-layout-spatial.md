## Structure and circulation

All ten stair caps share their full 7.8m width with same-height slab polygons within .003m plan tolerance. The five flights have plausible risers and gentler scaled runs; nominal 7.4m between .4m sidewalls leaves ample central passage. No different-elevation floor overlap was found. Foundations, .35m slabs and filled flights are a coherent construction direction, pending actual unions and headroom.

An independent XY clearance study combined all floor/stair footprints and subtracted centered boundary walls, stair-side footprints, fixtures and the two interior shells with door gaps. After .3m body-radius erosion it remained one connected component. This is planning evidence only: it ignores stepping, roof/arch shape and actual collision.

F-TAVERNA originally missed the southern street. The repaired F-TMOUTH is a real 7.8m-wide approach with 23.65m² overlap into the street and 46.83m² into the court. It is not a point contact. Tight street→mouth→court→Arcade movement checks belong in the blockout evidence.

Winery, Arcade and Post Office clear roof height is 3.25m; chapel 6.75m; boathouse 3.15m. Proposed 3.4m-wide / 3.1m-high arched doors exceed the declared standing body. All four interior door centers have surrounding site support; the nearest outer boundary at a door center is approximately 4.57m away. Door arches, sill continuity, corners and overhead roofs still require compiled verification.

The B04 heel/ear findings remain construction requirements. Grounded stair-side retaining cheeks, overlapping wall ends and the Bell-house masonry join must be present and inspected in the first production revision. These are coherent wall junctions, not reasons to weaken the body, assertions or route limits.
