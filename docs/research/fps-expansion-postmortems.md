# Expansion: 18 developer map studies

Researched 2026-11-08. All 18 are distinct primary publications absent from the original guide. Relevant article text was inspected; embedded videos were not watched. These establish developer observations and intended effects, not controlled proof of competitive outcomes. Historical geometry and game-specific abilities limit direct copying.

## Sources

1. **Riot / Jo-Ellen Aragon, [VALORANT 7.04: Breeze](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-7-04/), 2023.** Closed one side of Mid Pillar and A Hall, simplified B, and removed Cave's split approach. The stated goals connect predictable threats to manageable rotations/retakes and a greater incentive to contest Mid. **Inference:** removing connectivity can increase useful choices by lowering the attention cost of responding. Strong counterexample to maximizing route count.

2. **Riot / Jo-Ellen Aragon, [VALORANT 8.0: Icebox and Lotus](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-8-0/), 2024.** Moving a hanging container simplifies simultaneous ground/elevated fights; a Tube window gives Mid control influence over rotations; a parallel Orange path was removed. Lotus adds room and cover near contested neutral spaces, while a longer door cycle permits utility, fighting, and traversal in one opening. **Inference:** support useful action sequences rather than isolated passability.

3. **Riot / Jeff Landa, [VALORANT 5.07: Fracture](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-5-07/), 2022.** Removing Dish's far path was intended to make that route more attractive because watching both sides was unnecessarily difficult. Drop's two-sided check was simplified; Ropes changed because one smoke controlled it too cheaply. **Inference:** route usability depends on manageable threats and the cost of denial, not simply existence.

4. **Riot / Jeff Landa, [VALORANT 4.0: Bind and Breeze](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-4-0/), 2022.** Bind cover changed to remove an overly strong one-way smoke; Breeze's wider A Main removes competing checks, crates support both contesting and advancing, and rear cover gives defenders fallback/retake options. **Inference:** cover should be assessed from several phases and ownership directions.

5. **Riot / Jeff Landa, [VALORANT 5.06: Pearl](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-5-06/), 2022.** Lowered a wall so it cannot hide a player completely, removed corners, moved boxes across B Link to reduce simultaneous threats, and narrowed A's choke to improve smoke coverage. **Inference:** removing hiding capacity can make more deliberate contesting possible. Narrowing and widening both help when they repair different costs.

6. **Riot / Jo-Ellen Aragon, [VALORANT 6.0: Split](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-6-0/), 2023.** Widened initial A engagement space, reduced a boost box to create a new view, removed an under/over arrangement, enabled a silent drop, and smoothed a hard corner. **Inference:** standing space, vertical checks, and compulsory movement sounds each constrain tactical alternatives differently.

7. **Kenny Cameron interviewing Joe Lansford and map team, [Lotus: Unearthing VALORANT's Lost City](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/lotus-unearthing-valorant-s-lost-city/), 2023.** Designers explicitly sought fluid pivots through connected sites and doors, with sound/minimap signals supporting mind games. They measure corridor widths for smoke/fire and important angles but do not intentionally script individual grenade lineups. **Inference:** design conditions for player invention rather than prescribing every tactic.

8. **Chris Carney and Salvatore Garozzo, [The Birth of Ascent](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/the-birth-of-ascent/), 2020.** Mechanical doors let players alter connections; greybox testing tunes encounter distances and opening widths. Art maintains clear player-silhouette zones and recognizable landmarks. **Inference:** strategic flexibility benefits from understandable geometry and deliberately changeable access. Process evidence, not evidence that three lanes are universally optimal.

9. **Alex Wakeford interviewing Tyler Ensrude, [Halo Season 2: Catalyst and Breaker](https://www.halowaypoint.com/news/season-2-map-previews-catalyst-and-breaker), 2022.** Describes Catalyst's connected catwalks/light bridge and Breaker's dynamic cutting beam, and explains starting from an encounter, mode, or other motivating idea. **Inference:** moving hazards and connections can make route value depend on timing. Less granular causal detail than the Riot revisions; primarily a design-intent source.

10. **Alex Wakeford interviewing Cliff Schuldt and map team, [Halo Season 3 Maps and Modes](https://www.halowaypoint.com/news/echoes-within-maps-modes-preview), 2023.** Oasis lets Warthogs choose open routes threatened by heavy/air vehicles or interiors where pursuers risk entrapment. Cliffhanger's zones influence sightlines, weapons, and team allocation. **Inference:** a route can exchange one kind of vulnerability for another; mode rules change a space's strategic meaning.

11. **Alex Wakeford interviewing Cayle George, Cliff Schuldt, Ryan Bergstrom, [Halo Season 4 Maps Overview](https://www.halowaypoint.com/news/maps-overview-season-4), 2023.** Scarr's launcher requires entering vehicle territory; side lanes emphasize infantry but admit some vehicles. Translocator equipment changes the commitment of crossing or guarding distant zones. **Inference:** evaluate geography together with the movement verbs and reversal tools available to players.

12. **Alex Wakeford interviewing Cliff Schuldt, [Halo Season 5 Maps Overview](https://www.halowaypoint.com/news/maps-overview-season-5), 2023.** Forbidden combines long sightlines, central power equipment, alternate routes, and skill jumps for flag situations; Prism adds shootable environmental hazards. **Inference:** routes and objectives can reward several forms of mastery. This is a promotional designer interview, not proof of achieved competitive quality.

13. **Ubisoft, [Operation Void Edge: Oregon rework](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/seasons/voidedge), 2020.** New Kitchen corridor and Freezer stairs accompany removed doors and a changed Attic connection; the stated purpose is safer/easier rotations for both sides. **Inference:** useful reconfiguration combines opening and closing paths rather than simply adding them. Destruction changes the network further.

14. **Ubisoft, [Operation Shifting Tides: Theme Park rework](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/seasons/shiftingtides), 2019.** Constricts outdoor sightlines against spawn peeks, removes the separating Rail Platform, joins building halves through breakable walls, and relocates the main entrance/objectives. **Inference:** reducing separation and unwanted early visibility can shift action toward contestable interior relationships. Intent rather than measured outcome.

15. **Ubisoft, [Operation Neon Dawn: Skyscraper rework](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/seasons/neondawn), 2020.** Dragon becomes an interior bridge, roof rappel adds an approach, while some entrances and balcony sections disappear to reduce outside camping. **Inference:** more interior circulation can coexist with fewer protected exterior positions; route type and exposure matter more than count.

16. **Ubisoft, [Operation Shadow Legacy: Chalet rework](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/seasons/shadowlegacy), 2020.** Adds a Basement site connection and Bar/Gaming hallway, moves stairs, and repurposes Bathroom as a rotation point. **Inference:** objective viability depends on the support/retreat network around the room, not only its cover.

17. **Ubisoft, [Operation Steel Wave: House rework](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/seasons/steelwave), 2020.** Larger rooms and an extension create circulation/objective space; Garage changes add access while making planting harder; reduced northern sightlines and breakable floors change exposure. **Inference:** one change can deliberately improve approach while increasing objective commitment. Explicitly aimed at a casual map, not professional balance.

18. **Ubisoft, [Crimson Heist: Border rework](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rainbow-six/siege/news-updates/seasons/crimsonheist), 2021.** Expands Bathroom to create a soft wall between sites, extends Archives, and adds an interior balcony and exterior stairs. **Inference:** connections can support defense, attack, and player-created openings differently. Weakest rationale source in this set: geometry changes and general improvement goal are documented; their detailed tactical effects are hypotheses to test, not attributed developer conclusions.

## Candidate principles and counterexamples

**Preserve actionable alternatives under pressure.** A good position or route gives players a meaningful next action after contact: advance, reorient, support, delay, or retreat. This does not mean all positions need safe escapes. Commitment can be the cost of an otherwise valuable position. Halo traversal equipment also shows that the map alone does not determine reversibility.

**Make the cost of control selective and understandable.** A team should choose which threats to cover and which resources to spend. Excess parallel paths can exceed useful attention capacity (Fracture Dish, Icebox Orange); one smoke denying too much can erase counterplay (Fracture Ropes). Geometry can therefore improve by adding or removing an angle.

**Design sequences, not objects.** A door's useful opening is long enough for the intended sequence of preparation, contest, and movement; a crate can be an entry foothold or a trap depending on nearby angles and exits. Do not classify features as good independent of player movement, weapon ranges, utility, round phase, and objective rules.

**Counterexample to universal prescriptions:** widening helps coordinated movement but narrowing can make a smoke viable; a new route can enable recovery but another route can dilute defense beyond useful choices; protected cover can enable support but overprotected cover can discourage advancing. The generalizable unit is the relationship between advantage, cost, information, and response.
