# Persistent specialists for large reference collections

Use specialists when reference volume or architectural complexity overwhelms a
single coherent working set, not for every map. Give stable scopes such as a
stair and both landings or a gallery and its adjoining wall planes. Keep original
images, observations and decisions durable across revisions. Do not restart broad
source intake at every handoff.

Give each specialist a small related source set with discriminating alternate
angles where available; choose the size for the question rather than a fixed image
count. Keep authoring separate from independent fidelity review. Reconcile shared
levels, boundaries and route interfaces at integration checkpoints and retain a
whole-structure assessment so locally plausible pieces form one coherent map.
Regional scope does not certify unseen neighboring geometry.

Assign focused follow-ups for named uncertainty, reuse unaffected accepted work,
and record unresolved interfaces. Specialists are an escalation of the proportional
review in [level-authoring](../../level-authoring/SKILL.md); they do not introduce
universal drawing, FPS, image-count or reconstruction-runner gates.
