## Population and objective-state scenarios

### A — Department After Hours

**Opening 5v5 and full rush.** A west team can move through Window Vestibule/Concourse toward the west store opening, while an east team approaches through Record-store Threshold/Arcade Junction toward the east opening. The core divides the scoring area, so reaching a near sector does not imply knowledge of the far sector. A full team can choose immediate ground pressure, but a single entrance concentrates the group. The response is to yield part of the store, hold the other side of the core, and threaten a different opening rather than necessarily meet the rush at the first doorway. Exact visibility and capacity remain untested.

**3+2 split and first contact.** Three players can maintain ground contact while two invest in X1 or X2 and the upper frontage. Alternatively, the pair can remain on the ground and approach the north shop doors or south parcel threshold. These are not interchangeable: the upper pair does not score and must return through a stair; the ground pair can eventually join the contest from another side. After losing the west doorway fight, the west group can fall toward its vestibule while the pair maintains pressure elsewhere. A successful counter is to push into Concourse only with a teammate preserving the store, because the public/service circulation can bring an opponent back behind that push.

**Tank-led occupied point with paired supports.** The core offers an intelligible reason to distribute a team across near and far sectors, but it also interrupts assumed support visibility. Keeping both supports on one upper side cannot be presumed to sustain the entire point. The defender must choose between upper information, a ground support position near the core, and holding an outer entry. The attacking response can target the ground connection while avoiding the upper investment; upper control alone cannot satisfy the scoring hypothesis.

**3v3 after losses.** A side of the core and a return entrance are manageable territories to communicate. Taking the long north or south route may create a useful new front, but it may also leave the remaining ground player alone. It should be a deliberate regroup or split, not the default way to touch the objective. The upper Returns Balcony has a single route back through the north gallery; this is a possible commitment cost, not automatically a defect or a reason to add another stair.

**2v5 stagger and full regroup.** Two survivors should be able to leave the store toward their home vestibule or stock/receiving district and meet the next wave, rather than feed through the same exposed opening. Both drawings supply candidate circulation for that withdrawal; no walls or spawn barriers yet prove a protected recovery. Once five players re-form, they can select the opposite store mouth or contest the gallery together. The occupying team can pursue into the outer belt, but doing so ought to relinquish some coverage of the point and another return. This cost needs to exist physically; a caption is insufficient.

### B — Twin Atrium Exchange

**Opening 5v5 and full rush.** West can take Department Threshold/West Atrium/X1, while east can take its threshold/East Atrium/X3. These are explicit climbs to a scoring floor. A full rush commits the group to a stair arrival and its receiving space; it does not simply run through the lower passage onto the point. An opponent can retain the core-facing side of the food court or invest in the other arrival. The first upper engagement should occur after both groups have a usable arrival decision, not while one group is still forced onto a landing.

**3+2 split.** West's three can use X1 while a pair uses Back-of-house/Food Preparation/X2 and the North Gallery/North Entry. East can make the equivalent choice between X3 and Delivery/Receiving/X4/South Gallery. The pair's purpose is to establish another upper front, not merely add two bodies behind the same stair. If X1 is stopped, a west survivor can withdraw to the atrium, join the service approach, or go below the objective toward the other atrium. The lower transfer is promising because it changes the next ascent while giving up immediate scoring pressure.

**Occupied point and grounded retake.** An upper holding team has both scoring presence and access toward the stair fronts. A grounded attacker cannot contest from G1 despite being under the point. This makes the cost of leaving the objective for lower information consequential: a defender chasing into an atrium can threaten a transfer, but should cease to cover an upper entrance. If one upper position can supervise the capture area, both local stair arrivals and the lower exit, the stated route choices would collapse. That is a risk to test, not established visibility in the unpartitioned drawing.

**Tank and support distribution.** A tank can take an upper arrival while a support follows and a pair develops the service-side entry. A support remaining below is not assumed to see through the floor or maintain healing. The team must decide when to transfer the support resource upstairs and whether the second front is ready. Retaining a non-scoring upper pocket before the final contest would let those decisions occur without requiring a hero ability.

**3v3 after losses.** One player on the point and a pair attempting a lower transfer will be separated by a floor and an ascent. That can produce a useful delayed squeeze, but it can also surrender the point. A legitimate alternative is to leave together, regroup at an upper approach, and re-enter as three. The circulation must support both outcomes without prescribing that a mobile hero bridge the separation.

**2v5 stagger and full regroup.** The losing side should normally withdraw and regroup; the map need not make a two-player attack on five defenders successful. It does need a body-only way to regain a staging boundary. West has a candidate rear approach through Back-of-house/X2; east has Delivery/Receiving/X4. If the holding team can lock both grand and service stair tops from the same objective-facing position, re-forming as five would not produce a meaningfully different choice. The lower underpass gives a further flank, but should not be the only recovery answer because it enters the opposite atrium and can intersect enemy reinforcements. Its timing, exposure and usefulness remain open.

## Proactive physical proposals

### LL-S01-M1 — Separate A's outer circulation from home regroup territory

**Type:** macro opportunity; spawn exposure is not yet measured. **Scope:** A, Window/Bookshop/Stockroom and Record-store/Arcade/Receiving interfaces.

**Change:** Reserve a small inhabited regroup chamber on each home side, with a direct ground-store choice and a separate public/service choice. Put an opaque stock or concession volume between the chamber and the outer cross-map circulation. The perimeter flank should meet the chamber at a contestable dogleg, not share the spawn-facing sight axis. Keep the existing routes rather than adding exits by count.

**Before → after:** In a 2v5 withdrawal, the survivors currently have only named areas, not an established boundary at which to join respawns. The chamber gives them a place to regroup and choose a new store front; five returning players can make a coordinated choice after assembling. The point holders can push the chamber entrance, but must occupy the outer district and give up a store-facing position.

**Cost and test:** This can make regrouping too protected or lengthen a direct touch. Compare full-team recovery and a late single-player touch using the actual body. Do not assume a spawn shield will repair an exposed chamber.

### LL-S01-M2 — Give B a recoverable upper staging choice on each side

**Type:** opportunity / hold snowball risk, not a demonstrated balance flaw. **Scope:** B, X1/U7/U1 and X3/U8/U4, in relation to X2/North Entry and X4/South Entry.

**Change:** Arrange the grand-stair receiving area behind a full-height concession/service core, outside the scoring band. From it, keep one deliberate objective threshold and a reversible upper connection toward the service-side entry. Position the service arrival so it reaches another receiving boundary, not the back of the same exposed landing. Reserve the corresponding footprint on each side without requiring mirror geometry.

**Before → after:** A stopped 3+2 entry risks becoming five players repeating one stair-top loss. With a receiving pocket, the three can retain an upper foothold while the pair establishes the other front, then choose whether to contest or retreat together. In the next respawn wave, a fully regrouped five has a staging objective short of capturing the point.

**Opponent response and cost:** Point holders can leave scoring territory to contest the pocket, or concede it and defend the final entrances. The pocket must therefore be reachable by a defender and must not become a protected firing nest over the point. Extra staging depth can lengthen retakes and split support sightlines; verify those tradeoffs in developed plans and compiled approaches.

### LL-S01-M3 — Preserve B's lower transfer as a different information problem

**Type:** opportunity. **Scope:** B, G1, atrium exits and food-court/gallery edges.

**Change:** Keep G1 enclosed below the scoring slab and place its atrium exits around a service/concession return. Bound upper-to-lower views to selected atrium segments. An upper player who wants to inspect an underpass exit should move to a specific edge outside the principal objective hold, rather than see the exit along the same axis as a stair arrival and the scoring circle.

**Before → after:** In a 3v3, a pair can relinquish upper pressure and transfer below while the third delays or withdraws. The defender must choose to investigate the lower exit or continue covering the upper approach. This makes a lower fake and a delayed re-ascent plausible decisions; it does not guarantee either will succeed.

**Opponent response and cost:** A coordinated defender can assign a player to the lower edge and accept the reduced upper presence. The cost is less continuous visual spectacle between atria and the point, plus possible hidden travel. Preserve architectural orientation through the shared volume and visible landmarks; do not require imaginary audio cues to make the route understandable.

## Next evidence

The selected development should identify the actual scoring band, a full-team regroup place for each side, each approach's first contact and retreat, and the cost of lower transfer versus service ascent. Run the 3+2, lost-fight/full-regroup and contested 3v3 cases in both directions. A successful body traversal alone will not prove healing visibility, wave timing, hero counterplay or multiplayer balance. Finished cover, exact sightline rays and timing are intentionally not prerequisites for this early spatial-interest verdict.
