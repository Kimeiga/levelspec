## Construction and circulation planning

Six-meter flights, 10m run and 4m rise with 25 equal .16m risers are consistent with the declared .3m step and 42° slope assumptions. Nominal 5.6m between centered .4m sidewalls leaves passing/support room. .35m slabs and filled flights provide a coherent construction direction. No crossing routes need ambiguous stacked headroom; all movement interfaces are at matching elevations. This is a parameter/plan assessment: first and last treads, support below, slab undersides, actual unions and wall-end intrusions still require compiled inspection.

Within the former L02 brief, no additional supported consequential topology defect was identified. The user has since required a substantial spatial-form revision, so production is pending the replacement review. The outer sea-edge diagonal and low-cover effectiveness against raised opponents are explicit inspection targets, not established defects and not implicitly passed. Optical lines, movement samples and screenshots will not establish weapons, utility, crowd blocking, trading or competitive balance.
