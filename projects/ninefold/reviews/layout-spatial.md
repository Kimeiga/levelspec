## Macro assessment and retained hypotheses

The retained arrangement has meaningful spatial differences: the west approach changes direction around crowded masses and opens into a ground factory; Mid shares immediate territory with A and the half-height gallery; east commits to a two-stage climb before a rooftop encounter. Those distinctions survive removing the freestanding props. Inaccessible mass occupies the shared boundaries and explains the intervening land. The schematic therefore has sufficient developed spatial intent to proceed after the above connection repairs; it does not need arbitrary extra routes or a wider whole-map redesign.

The 3.4 m lanes offer considerably more nominal passing room than the .5 m body diameter, with wider market/laundry pockets for preparation. That does not establish five-player throughput through the bends or the trades possible beyond each entry. The B tank creates a local divider but both B entries remain toward the southern half; concrete site-entry views and support distances will determine whether it offers useful repositioning. The gallery is an observation route toward A, not another physical A entrance. Its aperture must remain observation-only and bounded rather than silently adding a drop/traversal route.
