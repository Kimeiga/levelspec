# Deadwater R01 — independent spatial-interest review

**Verdict: promising.**

Reviewed the user brief and `proposal-r01.svg` visually, using the matching
`generated/deadwater/sketch/proposal.png`, before receiving author rationale.
Scope is the early spatial proposal only.

The lower cargo-to-valve and freight-to-compressor loops create real opportunities
to change direction before the separate climbs and site thresholds commit players.
The elevated drilling floor joins the north muster/control deck, while the process
site remains below it and receives a distinct stair approach from the rear. Those
relationships make a legible working-platform crescent, with ocean voids separating
the machinery wings. It has enough connected territory and contrasting approaches
to develop as a 5v5 two-site map. The proposal does not read as three parallel
corridors joined by one transverse bridge.

Carry these questions into the developed layout:

- Give the A short-stair landing an explicit plan and section. Its two nearby
  thresholds into A currently look capable of creating redundant clearing angles;
  clarify whether both serve a useful movement relationship.
- Develop the north muster/control connection as occupied circulation with spawn
  shelter. The drawing currently leaves its direct A-facing doorway and the
  process-stair route too schematic to judge spawn exposure or rear rotation.
- Preserve the meaningful machinery-shaped voids and integrated industrial
  volumes when modeling. The broad drilling/process areas need local encounter
  structure, but their present size is not itself a reason to add corridors.

This verdict supports further layout development. It establishes neither balance,
physical reachability, detailed visibility nor target-engine behavior. No previous
map proposals were inspected for similarity.
