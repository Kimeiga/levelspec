# Station references

Santa Bruma is an invented passenger through-station in a rail cutting. Its earlier terminal studies supplied the setting and an archive of station photographs. The remake uses their original photographs as references for building relationships. Coordinates and station operations are design choices.

| Photo | Observation carried into the remake |
| --- | --- |
| [Rossio platform shed](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o_do_Rossio,_Lisboa,_2016_(16).jpg) | Parallel rails and a high glazed ridge establish the long view. Repeated columns mark the platform bays. |
| [São Bento platforms](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Inside_Porto_Sao_Bento_station.jpg) | Low rails between platforms, standing trains, canopy posts and a masonry tunnel portal reveal the section at a glance. |
| [Wemyss Bay concourse](https://www.reiachandhall.co.uk/public/wemyss-bay) | The ticket island breaks up a shared hall. Travelers circulate around it under one roof. |
| [Wemyss Bay aerial](https://www.networkrailmediacentre.co.uk/resources/53wzt-tx72y-4ub06-4axof-ifb9u) | Passenger, road and rail movements follow different alignments. Station approaches form their own spaces outside the building. |
| [Coventry parcels loading](https://warwickshirerailways.com/lms/lnwrcov569.htm) | A deep loading portal joins a working apron to the parcel building. Vehicle clearance and structural framing are visible at that threshold. |
| [Bricklayer’s Arms goods shed](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bricklayers_arms_goods_shed_1895307_0cf1e484.jpg) | The loading bank has depth, repeated supports and handling fixtures grouped around the work floor. |
