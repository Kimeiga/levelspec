# Spillway — current brief, R08

An original 5v5, two-site defusal blockout on approximately this repository's Dust II scale. The setting is a waterworks and its service buildings. Round-based attack/defend with no respawns is the design assumption. This is editable LevelSpec geometry, not an installed Counter-Strike package.

The envelope spans X6–90 and Y4–96 meters. Spawn anchors are T(55,8,0) and CT(44,92,0). A is at(21,74,.60); B at(78,75,0). The original wide spawn bounds remain in the optical audit: T X40–56/Y4–18, CT X40–56/Y86–96. Moving anchors alone is not the protection mechanism. Start with the [named-space graph](audits/node-graph.md), [layout](layout.svg) and [feature inventory](inventory.md).

Ground is Z0. The A plant platform is +.60m, reached by opposing three-riser stairs. The east approach crest is +1.20m with filled ramps. The dogleg gallery is Z4 with a .30m slab and 3.70m underside. Buildings reach8–9m; the T vestibule reaches7m. Thirteen low-cover features include three gallery refuges. D01 is an optional four-meter drop; ordinary return routes use stairs/ramp. Target-engine falling, damage and jump-back behavior remain unverified, and no artificial navigation link was added.

The authoring body remains2m tall, radius.25m, step.25m, slope42°. Optical studies use1.60m and1.05m eye offsets above supported feet. These are provisional camera studies, not authenticated CS2 cameras or recorded crouch behavior. Weapons, penetration, smoke/grenades, sound, economy, clocks, multiplayer body blocking and human coordination remain unconfigured or untested. Normal routes require no jump.

Review scenarios cover5v5 openings, defaults, takes, rotations and retakes, plus relevant2v2 and1v2 postplants. Both macro spatial/tactical and focused section/interface reviews are required. The [principles record](reviews/principles-record.md) links all user guidance to the reusable skills. Static evidence does not establish competitive balance.
