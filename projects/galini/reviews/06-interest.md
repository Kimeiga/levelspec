# S04 independent spatial-interest review

Verdict: **promising** against the updated size and playable-depth brief.

Reviewed `reviews/evidence/sketch-s04.png` and the raw L04 plan identity before a replacement developed packet or rationale. User steering: the earlier map felt too small in playable depth and its connections too simple; make it bigger while retaining bendy coastal paths, stairs and plazas. L03/B04 acceptance does not carry forward.

This proposal adds actual journey choices as well as extent. The west route can leave the coast through Laundry/Winery and reach the central town before committing to A's switchback, so a shoreline player gains a consequential reset rather than only a longer walk. Taverna Court and the roofed Arcade connect the eastern approach back toward Fishmarket. Customs introduces a local split around a substantial building before the boat district; that is distinct from simply widening the same entry. The upper Post Office passage offers another movement choice around the rotating lane's mass. The two-door chapel and boathouse provide occupied interiors within the sites, changing local clearing and support possibilities.

The larger irregular piazzas, curved shore roads and offset stair systems still read as one terraced settlement. The expansion therefore has enough spatial substance to develop; it is not merely a scaled version of S03.

Development targets: make Laundry/Winery's shared thresholds and Customs' split/rejoin boundaries unambiguous; keep interior door connections, roof/ceiling levels and post-office exits explicit; preserve useful occluding fronts as the enlarged courts are built, so additional area does not become undifferentiated exposure. Broader dimensions and extra links can change travel and defensive coverage materially, requiring fresh tactical and visibility review rather than inherited balance assumptions.

This verdict is an early spatial-interest gate only. Detailed layout, construction, visibility, timing and compiled traversal remain open.
