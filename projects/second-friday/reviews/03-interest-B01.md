# B01 independent quick spatial-interest review

**Verdict: promising.** First inspected revised `brief.md` and `reviews/evidence/sketch-B01.png`, before any new author rationale or other verdicts. This is a fresh proposal review; compact L-series acceptance does not carry over.

The new district answers the revised brief in its actual spatial organization. Large freestanding footprints now create the playable space: B07 and B06 pinch and turn the west approach into Carpet Bend; B05 redirects the east approach between an appliance yard and a southern cross; B08 makes the north edge of Auction Square and supplies an outdoor roof route. Northwest spawn can leave above or below B10, while southeast spawn can pass either side of B04/B03. These journeys have meaningful turns, recombinations and intermediate open courts rather than enlarged corridors with building labels. The irregular central building row gives the point a district setting and establishes enough spatial substance to develop for ten players.

Consequential development targets:

- All tan ground is currently playable, including the broad continuous north and east perimeter. Decide what the long service-street/outer-building journey contributes to contesting the point, and give its endpoints useful interfaces. Avoid leaving a largely unobserved perimeter route that only materializes at a spawn.
- The control rectangle visually overlaps the northwest edge of B05. In the developed layout, draw the actual usable scoring boundary around that footprint or move the boundary/anchor so no inaccessible building floor is presented as scoring space.
- B08's roof is much nearer the northwest staging journeys than the southeast spawn, and its south face overlooks the approach into Auction Square. Develop the building's facade, roof edges and opposing routes together so the roof's information has a legible limit and a feasible response. This is a height/timing question for later compiled review, not a rejection of the roof.

The enlarged outdoor composition is worth developing. Street widths, spawn safety, long views, roof access, point occupation and objective timing remain detailed-layout and blockout work; the sketch does not prove combat balance or physical traversal.
