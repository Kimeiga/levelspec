# Last Light Galleria · L01

User brief: new 5v5 map for a hero shooter, themed after a dead mall. Mode is provisionally single-point Control, two respawning teams of five. Original invention inspired by real mall atria, not a reconstruction. `layout.svg` and `layout-data.json` are the raw developed packet; inspect these before author rationale.

No target hero shooter or ruleset exists in the repository. LevelSpec supplies geometry/collision/navigation/inspection, not combat or objectives. Design assumptions: one tank, two damage, two support per team; base run 5.5m/s, some ranged hitscan/projectiles, area denial/healing and short mobility abilities. Respawn wave 8s, unlock after 20s, uncontested score to 100, best of three rounds. These are design hypotheses and are not implemented. Health packs are not required by this first blockout; supports and regroup routes supply the design's reset. Spawn rooms need team barriers/invulnerability in a target engine; metadata alone does not protect them.
