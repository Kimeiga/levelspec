## Architectural findings

**Town enclosure and coastal setting — accepted.** [Roof-on](evidence/c11/roof-on.png) and [north roofs](evidence/c11/north-roofs.png) show complete roofed masses on two substantial shoulders, with lower shoreline construction and two bridges across the open ravine. Twenty buildings have paired pitched roof surfaces, closed facades and gable closures. The [cutaway](evidence/c11/cutaway.png) exposes actual rooms and stairs within those masses. SEC02/03/05/07/08/09 standing interiors show continuous ceilings, floor-to-wall contacts and usable openings rather than roofless corridor scenery. The repeated rectangular openings and plain facades are an appropriate blockout abstraction; extra window/detail quotas are not needed for acceptance.

The [coastal retaining view](evidence/c11/coastal-retaining-junctions.png), reverse overview and native source establish continuous ground/retaining mass below the elevated streets. The six `F00/F04/F08-…-rock-backing` extrusions extend from z−6.4 to the relevant slab underside, avoiding the occupied lower passages. The setting currently reads most strongly as a heavily retained cliff town. More varied exposed rock silhouettes would be optional later art development, not a missing architectural support requirement.

**Both bridges — accepted.** The [Market arch view](evidence/c11/market-arch-and-banks.png) and [Civic arch view](evidence/c11/civic-arch-and-banks.png) show genuine openings through substantial substructures, with continuous curved intrados, spandrels, piers, decks and bank contact. `BR-M` occupies x50–78/y56–62: deck top z4, 0.6 m thickness, substructure to z3.4 from z−6. `BR-U` follows the bent x54–76/y87–98 plan: deck top z8, 0.7 m thickness, substructure to z7.3 from z−6. Arch cuts belong to the complete substructure assemblies; the bank abutments and cliff backing supply actual end mass. The native Market section independently corroborates the openings and deck. Flat gameplay decks and simplified masonry faces are acceptable adaptations of the reference bridge. These are not unsupported thin paths with decorative arch marks.

**School lower arcade and suspended terraces — accepted.** [School support](evidence/c11/school-suspended-support.png), [SEC08-L](evidence/c11/SEC08-L-standing-actualwalk.png), [SEC08-O](evidence/c11/SEC08-O-standing-actualwalk.png), and [TURN-S8-N](evidence/c11/TURN-S8-N-standing-actualwalk.png) show a real lower occupied passage beneath the upper floor.
