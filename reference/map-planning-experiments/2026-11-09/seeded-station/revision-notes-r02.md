# R02 — focused architectural revision

Read the [station plan](station-level.svg), [lower plan](lower-level.svg), [headhouse study](headhouse-study.svg), [quay study](quay-arrival-study.svg) and [transfer study](transfer-mouths.svg) first. Each has a complete PNG rasterization. All five were visually inspected before freezing the drawing/source manifest and sending the paths for independent return review.

The independent [R01 interest review](interest-review-r01.md) found the station/embankment proposal promising and identified three architectural/interface points. R02 retains the precinct, objective/spawn anchors, passenger shed, two occupied datums and long quay journey. It does not advance into mechanics development or AML.

| Review point | R02 change visible in the drawings |
| --- | --- |
| 02–03–06–07 still partly read as adjoining titled polygons | 02/03 now share one exterior perimeter, floor and high ticket-concourse volume. Their old internal wall/threshold disappears. The central kiosk and full-height buffet/luggage flanks shape movement inside that volume. The floor continues south into 06 beneath an overhead roof-step beam; no partition crosses the join. Three broad pier-and-arch bays open the buffer hall into the railway head. 07 is explicitly an open-sided canopy with columns and roof outline, reached through a broad portal rather than another enclosed room cell. H–H and P–P show the shared floor, roof relationships and open arcade. |
| Eastern quay was chiefly a long bent strip | A continuous five-metre retaining face and structural buttresses now define the station side. The quay widens before a double arcade into the working court. The lower court is covered by the rail/service deck, with load-bearing piers and an east road opening; the lower freight road hall is its northern destination. This also corrects R01's ambiguous “open sky” court beneath upper station geometry. The Q–Q section shows a real under-rail arrival. The long waterfront remains quiet; no extra rooms or intermediate junctions were added. |
| West island approach and N/I/C/G mouths were ambiguous | A continuous terminal-head apron runs west of both red buffer stops at x65.5 m. The buffer hall's arcade opens onto this apron, which joins the island at z0 without walking across a rail bed. N/I/C/G now have explicit top and bottom landings, identical XY in the two main plans, and enlarged plan pairs. I and G turn to eastward flights with about 11 m run; N and C descend south. I's lower foot opens north, then turns west to the subway, bypassing the low stair toe. C's foot opens south into the apron; G's opens north into the road hall. |

The headhouse follows the already-inspected Wemyss Bay photograph's useful relationship: a substantial kiosk distributes people within one roofed volume, with different onward directions visible around it. The reference does not establish Bracken Quay's footprint, dimensions or roof heights. The rail/service deck, double arcade, stair dispositions and sections remain inventions, not newly observed source facts. The original five-photo observations and attribution record remain in [source observations](source-observations.md).

R02 moves further away from literal sampled geometry. The seed still explains the broad distribution of civic, working and lower-return areas, but its internal polygon boundaries were least useful where a shared headhouse volume was needed. The new apron, structural arcade, covered arrival and explicit landing geometry are authored responses to review, rather than generated features. This revision supplies no new evidence that random seeding improves speed or quality compared with another starting method.

The drawings remain schematic architectural proposals. Intended clear heights, approximate stair runs, landings, slab openings, guards, roof loading and path continuity require later detailed construction and compiled inspection. The orientation cone is an architectural hypothesis, not measured combat visibility. No timings, collision-aware traversal, utility, shooting, 5v5 play or balance claims were added.

The entire R01 drawing/source set was copied before revision and hashed. The original raw seeds, prototype code and baseline renderer were checked against their earlier SHA-256 values and remain unchanged. R02 is frozen separately in `frozen-manifest.json`; this prose note was written after that drawing freeze to allow independent return review to begin from raw drawings first.
