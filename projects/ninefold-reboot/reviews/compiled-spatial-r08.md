## NF-V01: disposition and useful next experiment

**Recommend experiment, retain R08 as the accepted baseline until its outcome is assessed.** The drawing makes a substantive change: S5 starts earlier/east on School, turns west at z4.8, and reaches a west gallery at z6.4 above Boiler's occupied ground court before turning back onto the roof. The proposed benefit is not merely a curved silhouette. It gives a midlanding a distinct clearing task and lets a sustained upper route accompany a lower courtyard journey. This directly addresses the user's interest in neighboring levels and turns more meaningfully than re-shaping a stair inside the same sealed shaft.

The exact option is not yet construction-ready. Lower flight(40,43,3.2)→(40,49,4.8), landing x38–42/y49–53, upper flight toward(32,51,6.4), and west gallery x29–33/y53–64 consume School staging and overlap the existing surrounding mass organization. The proposed gallery also changes the roof's current isolation. A new view into Boiler cannot be accepted merely because movement is physically possible below it.

Develop one bounded comparison with the adjoining School/context rebuild, lower tank/passages and complete guard/soffit profile drawn together. Keep the same School→roof graph edge. Inspect leader/follower/opponent positions before the lower flight, around the quarter-turn and at the gallery turn; specifically test when the follower loses the leader's court or roof opponent. At Boiler ground, compare north and south paths around BOILER-TANK against the new upper edge. Keep enough opaque edge to avoid observing both lower exits from a protected gallery corner, and show the useful counter-view if a court-facing segment is open. Resolve actual usable widths, headroom and changed approach length before selecting AML. A curved or spiral stair need not be added if this quarter-turn/shared court experiment supplies the intended relationship more coherently.

A second, optional future experiment is to compare Sign's existing narrow standing view with a shallow local recess that moves the upper player closer to the same aperture **without enlarging its lateral span**. The purpose would be to test less alignment-sensitive information while retaining shoulder-only attention. It is deferred: current geometry is not defective, and widening the window without a full body/plant-frontier study could create a stronger unwanted hold.
