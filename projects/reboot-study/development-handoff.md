# Developed-layout handoff

The three S01 alternative panels are independent of the map authors, but inherited thread context makes them author-independent rather than blind experiments. Each report records its exposure. The original maps remain unchanged. This document coordinates work; map-specific review reports and dispositions remain authoritative evidence.

## Ninefold

Develop **B / Loft Street**. Root, macro, opposing-plans and spatial reviewers each found the occupied factory above a continuing ground street worth developing. Retain that relationship while reopening exact boundaries. The northern Boiler arrival and southeastern East arrival must become distinct receiving territories, not two names for the same east-side fight. Compare bounded lower-street observation positions and record the choice and cost. Keep the roof only if its intended early territory/approach-switch role survives the developed journeys; it need not be a late emergency retake.

Use complete factory and adjoining residential volumes. The source photographs support uneven building identities, deep street fronts and setbacks, not one uniform extruded remainder. Detailed sections must show the underpass, loft, lightwell and optional roof, with actual clearances and apertures.

## Last Light Galleria

Develop **B / Twin Atrium Exchange**. Keep the occupied food court above a usable, non-scoring lower transfer between distinct atria. The important test is recovery after losing the point: a regrouped team needs a contestable upper staging choice before the scoring threshold, and public/service arrivals must reach meaningfully different receiving positions. This does not imply invulnerable pockets or an automatic way for two players to beat five.

Compare the bounded underpass-mouth and lower-information proposals together. Preserve useful support and orientation in the shared mall volume without allowing one objective position to supervise every ascent and lower exit. Give the stalls/service core real architectural thickness, and distinguish public grand stairs from screened service ascents. Actual roofs/skylights, columns, gallery edges and storefront depth must survive the move to AML.

## Galini

Develop **B / Ravine Quarter**. The author's selection weighs the stronger distinction between a same-bank A advance, a captured-middle approach to B and the committed shoreline transfer against A's continuous inhabited harbor. Keep the two bridges and shoreline end-run; do not add crossings solely to increase options. Middle Taverna and upper Inn arrivals should have different spatial and information costs, with usable receiving places and understandable neighboring districts. West Homes can separate upper-crossing observation from direct coverage of A's local ascents. The lower harbor should provide a building-defined pause before the site while retaining the shore route's commitment.

Retain the reference-derived town and retaining logic: actual buildings, roof families, rocky terrain and occupied landings. A bridge may be a regional control advantage without becoming a single position that governs every district exit. Detailed and compiled review must establish those distinctions. In the unselected Harbor Fold, the eastern +4 m stair island is separate from Market; a side connection would be an optional new experiment, not an existing connection implied by equal height.

## Shared requirements for the next authoring wave

Create one coherent developed layout per map, with concise dispositions for all consequential proposals. Adopt compatible changes and decline conflicting ones with reasons; do not accumulate every suggested feature. Actual labeled SVG/PNG plans and coordinate sections must resolve floor edges, walls, movement openings, observation apertures, stairs/landings, guards, roofs and consequential obstacles. Keep useful relationships as well as blocked sightlines visible in the review packet.

Supply named sections covering spawns/exits, approach territories, objectives, connectors/holds and every vertical interface. Record retained body/eye assumptions and predicted leader/follower/opponent/support relationships. Provide concrete routes and scenario positions for the later collision-aware walk and opposing-view captures. Explicitly distinguish predicted geometry, measured harness behavior and unavailable target-engine mechanics.

Independent strategy and dedicated section-by-section spatial/visibility review will inspect the developed packet before production AML. Reviewers may require focused corrections, but need not perfect all game balance on paper. The compiled stage will test height-dependent predictions and architecture separately. Do not start production AML until root reports the detailed gates closed.
