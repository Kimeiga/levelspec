# Theme references

Study mode: inspiration. The map is invented; no exact building or market reconstruction is claimed.

- `friday-market.jpg`: Wikimedia Commons photograph, source page https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:سوق_الجمعة_عمان.jpg . Observed: overlapping blue/orange tarps, narrow poles, shoes and clothing on dense tables, residential buildings beyond. Useful spatial relationship: sheltered market tables under a patchwork lightweight canopy, with open edges.
- `canopy-aisle.jpg`: photograph on Macalah Pcolar's Amideast article https://www.amideastedabroad.org/home/pcolar-blog3 . Observed: metal canopy frame and ridge, hanging garments behind lower racks, bulbs, translucent pale roof. Useful relationship: tall rack backs versus low tables, continuous shelter without masonry separating every stall.

Photos are retained for private reference study; they are not textures or redistributable map assets, and no license grant is assumed. Local coordinates, dimensions, garages, terrace, auction courtyard, stairs and route network are inventions for play. The project uses contemporary everyday secondhand commerce rather than a historic-monument setting.
