# S03 independent spatial-interest review

Verdict: **promising** against the updated user brief.

Visually inspected `reviews/evidence/sketch-s03.png` and the raw L03 plan identity before an updated mechanics/design packet. The user asked for bendy paths, stairs and plazas rather than a grid-like coastal city. This is a fresh assessment; the superseded L02 acceptance does not carry forward.

The spatial form now responds to that request. The Sea Ribbon follows an uneven cliff edge, widens at its northern end, then reaches A through a switchback and intermediate +2m landing. That is a different journey from the diagonal Bell Stair off the irregular Olive Piazza. On the other side, the Taverna and Harbor Crescent sweep around an inland mass before meeting the boat court, while Fish Market gives the square a separate approach. The upper lane bends with the town rather than completing another side of a rectangle. The asymmetrical piazzas create recognizable destinations within those curved journeys.

The revised shape is coherent and substantial enough to develop. It preserves route decisions and contrasting upper/lower districts while changing how players arrive and orient, so the curvature is consequential rather than decoration.

Develop the irregular floor/stair interfaces carefully: the switchback's two stair ends and landing, Bell's upper pad, and the diagonal Boat Stair need supported shoulder width at their oblique joins. During massing, let clustered whitewashed buildings and retaining edges meet the plazas and bends; avoid making the coastal town read only as separated road ribbons around generic empty ground. These are development targets, not reasons to replace the composition.

This first-proposal verdict establishes spatial promise only. Developed visibility, construction, circulation and tactical review remain required for L03.
