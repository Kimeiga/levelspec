## Reactor and lower-route interfaces

The retained native D profile constrains the proposal. The independent radial calculation in `evidence/l04-radial-reservation.py/.json` finds a standing-height radial interval of approximately R3.803..7.537 at floor−3.2, before body radius, guard, deck, support and azimuthal-mesh allowances. The selected lower floorR4.1..7.3 and local fans are sensible reservations; this calculation is not permission to shrink or remove installed magnet envelopes.

The revised vessel ramps R7.1/−3.2→R9.8/−2.8 meet the lower fan across their full2.6 m width, with start corners atR≈7.218. Distinct vessel, cryostat and bioshield openings remain required. Bridge underside−3.05 is .20 m above PF top−3.25. The western bridge continues toR27.7 then descends inside the freight well; the eastern bridge toR24.3 then descends inside its service room. Room ramps now continue radially, rather than being treated as arbitrary axis-aligned shortcuts.

The hall openings are sufficiently wide in the drawn section: the210° bridge crosses X−22 with centreY≈−8.702 and a2.6 m projected width of about3.002 m, insideY[−10.35,−7.05]. The350° bridge crossesX22 with centreY≈.121 and projected width≈2.640 m, insideY[−1.35,1.6]. These small geometric margins make final rail, jamb and capsule checks necessary. Each lower bridge is an exposed commitment between rooms; neither is assumed to be safely tradeable merely because it is2.6 m wide.

Central stair has a real hatch,20 nominal .16 rises and distinct top/bottom landings. The retained ground outer strip avoids forcing every equatorial player down. The lower bypass is only .98 m nominal clear after the outer guard, adequate as a single-file reservation for a .70 m body but not two-player passing. Filled stair material, parapet ends and shallow support beams determine the actual turn. Compile must test the complete curve in both directions, outer bypass, adjacent lower endpoints and follower positions. Support beams underside−.8 provide2.4 m above the lower floor; old temporary posts atR6.5 must not survive unnoticed through this route.

The T-to-future-crown sample is blocked by existing reactor geometry near(−3.844,3.384,5.841); an initially plausible doorway hypothesis was therefore not an established open line. Neither result substitutes for revised rays after L04 compilation.

B04's old gallery landing also had a qualified local A/near-W-mouth support relationship. Its two target bearings differed by118.95°, outside one91.31° horizontal camera frame, and it did not see the inner bridge sample. L04 changes those interfaces materially; preserve the distinction between near mouth and central bridge, and between acquiring two targets by turning and monitoring them together. Do not carry the old acceptance forward.
