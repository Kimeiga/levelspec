# Daiban native courtyard capability study

This is a new bounded, source-led AML example, not an R19 reconstruction revision.
Historical project sources, snapshots, acceptance holds and CURRENT pointers are
unchanged. The three user-supplied original images are copied byte-for-byte here
for a portable maintained example. No external rooms or complete building loop
are inferred. Dimensions below are estimates, not a survey.

## Source observations and proposed bounds

Primary user-03 shows circular screens on adjacent planes at different heights:
the right aperture is higher. The left opening has lower semicircular opaque
infill and radial framing; the right opening has a light rail and an inclined
soffit behind it. The nearer stair has a distinct stepped underside/edge.
User-02 shows stacked elongated gallery/planter edges, a broad lower straight
flight, and a substantial column supporting slab/gallery construction. It does
not establish a complete circulation loop or equal storey heights. User-01 is a
materially different high-angle cross-check, not a second crop of user-03.

## Phase 3 acceptance evidence

Independent source-first plan/section review released the bounded geometry trial.
Subsequent source-visible backing surfaces and the estimated physical planted base
are disclosed above. Native compilation and four local physical walkthroughs pass;
five projected floor-gap warnings correspond to separate authored storeys and do
not establish missing support on these routes.

Photographic fidelity remains **HOLD**. The current primary camera is approximate,
not calibrated: portal spacing and foreground railing occlusion remain materially
unmatched. High and overhead camera registrations are incomplete. These differences
cannot yet be attributed solely to geometry. Reference copies remain exact originals;
no historical reconstruction acceptance or CURRENT pointer is superseded.
