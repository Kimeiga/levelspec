# L02 author disposition and architectural choice

B / Ravine Quarter was chosen because gaining a shoulder or its middle district changes the attack front and the investment needed to change objective. A's inhabited harbor continuity remains a credible alternative, but the cross-town commitments offered the stronger new experiment. This was a judgment about journeys, not a reviewer vote. No extra bridge, ladder or ravine shortcut was added.

The first precise L01 pass undermined that premise by turning the town into terrace pads and repeated flights. Root's return finding was accepted, and L01 is retained as rejected evidence. R02 reopened the actual architecture: Laundry undercroft/upper frontage, Chapel's occupied +6 porch, the Press stair house, School's useful lower arcade beneath occupied upper rooms, a turning Bell ascent and a cart road. Root found that recomposition promising; L02 resolves its joins without claiming that this early return closes detailed gates.

The counterfactuals in the variant comparison preserve surrounding topology. M0 removes the return (outlined ghost); M1 retains it. U0 describes a continuous low/open south edge; U1 selects the opaque edge with bounded W02. W03 is a separate School balcony toward Fountain. Openings grant no movement and no automatic knowledge of the bomb or B plant.

Architectural decisions: all buildings have complete shells and roofs. Nonplayable building foundations remain solid unless an explicit occupied lower floor or arcade is declared. School's lower arcade lies north of the +6 landing, with 3.7 m clear below the upper slab. Stair wells remain open over their flights. The +8 external School terrace uses declared colonnade supports rather than rock filling its lower route. Bridge arches/abutments use real-image vocabulary; exact spans and flat decks remain invented.

L02 also corrected two plan mistakes during the narrow sanity pass: Cooper's waypoints now go around the Press corner, and the Press passage's east door is placed on the actual recessed facade at X43. A disconnected 0.4 m² residual triangle is classified scenic, not counted as circulation. The original body and original Galini source were not changed.
