## Bridge low cover and attention

Proposed U-C01: X48..51/Y61.8..63.6, baseZ4,height1.1. Proposed U-C02: X60..63/Y58.4..60.2, baseZ4,height1.1. They join the respective north/south screens without a tiny underside gap. Each leaves approximately3.4 m transverse bypass; the separate full-height baffles retain2.2 m nominal bypasses.

The arrangement lets a player crouch-like low camera behind local cover while a standing peek remains exposed. C01 follows the northern bypass of U-B01; C02 follows the southern bypass of U-B02. Movement alternates sides rather than entering a permanently safe straight cover strip. A supported hold from one end must still account for the opposite bridge end or rely on a teammate. Low cover should not be promoted to full standing cover, and the actual game crouch/body/muzzle behavior remains unverified.

Check both sides at standing and low camera heights, including an approaching target at a different elevation on the ramp. Confirm usable space behind each cover item, exposed transfer distance to the next item, no feet-only seam and no unintended jump/roof access. Do not assume a1.05 m camera proves the engine can crouch at that pose.

## Scenario implications

**5v5 openings:** segmented long approaches provide more stopping/clearing decisions while tall masses prevent the upper route cheaply observing all alternatives. Mid is still valuable; long-angle warnings there may be intentional if the holding player pays exposure/attention cost and opponents have feasible nearby responses. The new layout does not need zero35 m flags.

**Site commitment:** A and B still support a principal entry plus a different rear/short or upper threat. New A-04/B-04 should force a useful handoff between watching an approach and watching the objective. Avoid claiming independent crossfires through S-02 or treating ramp and East Long as unrelated directions at every B hold.

**2v2 post-plant:** inspect pairs taking one cover side each, retakers holding a baffle instead of rushing and attackers rotating behind newly hidden cover. Test whether one player can still inspect the plant plus all feasible emergence bands from a nearby replacement corner. Bounds must be explicit if a one-sided transit hold is retained.

**1v2 post-plant:** the solo player can seek a more isolated fight as sightlines are segmented. More cover also imposes clearing and movement time, so a new long route that arrives after the objective resolves is not a meaningful response. Equal winning odds are not required.
