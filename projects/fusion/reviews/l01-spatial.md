## Reactor section and bounded visibility claims

The section preserves a 19.4 m outer diameter and 11.4 m height with a tall inner
leg and curved outboard wall. The equatorial deck from R 4.1 to 8.8 fits inside the
drawn chamber and has sufficient nominal standing volume after body erosion.
Published outer dimensions are distinct from the 4.7 m temporary work-deck width.
Intermediate profile coordinates remain inferred, as the contract correctly states.

The missing four upper sectors and full lower shell express an unfinished machine
without disguising it as a circular tube. No ordinary standing route is implied
between magnets or around the outside of the vessel. Essential circulation rests
on the three bridges and annular work deck.

One wording correction would improve precision: the core can block **diametric**
views across the hole; it does not prove that every oblique chord or every pair
of arc positions is mutually occluded. Preserve glancing lines along the open
arc and across port seams as named compiled studies. In particular, compare
south-junction-to-E-port and W-port-to-south-arc views. This is a claim-scope
clarification, not a demonstrated geometry defect requiring a redesign.
