## Spatial-interest verdict: promising

The proposal is a recognizable repair yard through its spatial organization: an occupied dock is carved into one service ground; a continuous hall wraps a large hull; a partial upper gallery shares that hall rather than becoming another room; two industrial masses interrupt the exterior circuit. The lower journey rounds H1 and returns through an offset descent, while the hall journey can pass either hull shoulder and contest its bow apron. Those are consequential relationships beyond a collection of boxes with attached stairs.

The approximately94×84m footprint has enough actual spatial substance to develop. This is not a pacing or balance approval. The dock-head and southern apron journeys still need evidence of purposeful preparation/contact rather than empty transit.

## Bounded improvement experiments

**P1 — Develop D2's arrival as a turn, if compiled views show simultaneous high and floor exposure.** Compare the current direct threshold against a west-hall opening shifted north toward y12..18, with D2's upper endpoint at x8 so a4m-deep apron remains before x12. The climber would gain information about the hall in stages; a follower could hold the dock-facing side while the lead turns toward the portal. The opponent keeps a defendable threshold and can pressure the turn from the hall. Cost: additional travel and a more committed corner. This should not be adopted solely to make ascent safer; test it against the useful teammate support currently afforded by the direct opening.

**P2 — Give the gallery a choice of support sectors if one position dominates both aprons.** Test an integrated, full-height machinery enclosure or a short interruption of the central front gallery, leaving a defined rear circulation path. The high player would need to commit to a bow shoulder or change position to support the other; opponents could threaten an ascent while the player supports the wrong sector. Cost: less immediate lateral support and possibly a narrow upper retreat. Compare actual standing/follower views and preserve the hall's common volume rather than turning the gallery into a corridor of rooms.

These are optional proposals. G1's construction conflict is the only confirmed mandatory repair in this review. Lower-objective exposure, high support power and long transit are design questions requiring the next evidence tier.
