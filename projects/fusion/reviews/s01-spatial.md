# Fusion S01 — independent spatial-interest review

Scope: early spatial proposal and macro/spatial opportunities. Verdicts concern
development potential, not physical reachability, accurate final reactor modeling
or competitive balance.

Inspected `planning/brief.md`, `planning/candidates-s01.svg` and its PNG, then the
actual `references/vessel-0.webp`, `assembly-0.webp` and `assembly-2.webp`. Reviewed
the FPS review and spatial-exploration guidance. No sibling verdict was consulted.
The agent inherited conversation context and the sketch-authoring steps; this is
an independent assessment of the artifacts, not a blind test. This review does
not cover the separately assigned organic-order gate.

A is substantial enough to develop at sketch scale: the outer journeys have room
for gathering, approach and site transitions, while the ring gives the center a
distinct movement relationship. Its dimensions alone are not the reason for this
verdict. The western sequence and eastern equipment path still need real thresholds
and intermediate decisions; filling their broad polygons with repeated crates would
not be equivalent development.

No comparison against unrelated completed repository maps was performed, so no
novelty claim is made. A has south-to-north outer routes and northern rear services,
but the drawn traversable torus, port junctions and one-sided gallery offer a
specific spatial basis beyond three renamed parallel lanes. Preserve those
relationships when turning the diagram into geometry.

## Consequential development needs

**S01-S1 — Show the unfinished reactor state.** All three sketches currently show
complete concentric circles. The user's most distinctive spatial opportunity is
not yet visible: where does an enclosed D-section passage become an exposed work
deck, and how does that opening relate to the larger pit? Before production,
draw the retained and missing shell sectors, physical deck, central void and at
least one radial section. This is a missing design definition, not a defect in an
unbuilt model. Keep the 19.4 m envelope and D-shaped radial section; do not make a
round pipe or uniformly shrink the machine to obtain cover.

**S01-S2 — Resolve the concentric floors.** A's band between the vessel and outer
pit wall reads as a continuous service annulus, while the amber vessel interior is
also a complete circuit. Decide which parts of the annulus are walkable, occupied
by coils, construction void or scenic. Two near-equivalent full circuits could
dilute the value of entering the vessel. A plan should distinguish movement ports,
observation apertures and inaccessible machine gaps without demanding more routes.

These definitions fit the next developed drawing; they do not require another
whole-map sketch round unless their resolution changes the core composition.

## Proposed spatial opportunities

**SP1 — Open a bounded southern assembly sector toward Receiving.** At A's vessel
south arc, retain the circular work deck but leave a contiguous sector of outer
shell/overhead unfinished. Connect the southern port to this exposed deck and the
two enclosed limbs of the torus. From Receiving, a team could pressure the exposed
segment while another player takes a curved limb; the central core prevents one
straight view from resolving both exits. The opposing team could contest the open
segment from a port-side position or withdraw into the retained shell. This gives
the same circle changing enclosure and exposure rather than repetitive tunnel.

Cost: the opening can make the short path too exposed or permit a gallery to watch
all three port decisions. Test the open segment against the enclosed alternative
with eye-level views from Receiving, both port mouths and the gallery. Do not
make the entire ring visible from one upper position. Exact missing angles are an
author choice; retain nine-sector construction logic rather than an arbitrary tear.

**SP2 — Let the west gallery observe the port junction before it reaches A.**
Preserve A's blue route from the Cask lane up the west side, but give its inner
edge a bounded view onto the western port arrival. Keep its A-facing section
separated by a substantial machine/partition return. A supporter could observe a
port contest while a teammate clears the lower approach; reaching the A-support
position would require moving and giving up the first view. The opponents could
force the supporter away or exploit the abandoned angle.

Cost: an upper/lower crossfire may become too difficult for a pair to clear, or
the gallery may provide stale information because arrival to A takes too long.
Compare a port-facing aperture with a continuously open gallery edge. Inspect
leader/follower positions on the ascent and both ends of the gallery. This is a
proposal to bound a useful interaction, not a requirement for panoramic height.

**SP3 — Differentiate the service annulus from the vessel passage by interruption.**
Use coil bases, port-cell walls and a construction exclusion at one nonessential
annulus segment to create local work bays instead of a second uninterrupted
ring. Retain a deliberate connection through the vessel between those bays.
Then the choice is between entering a machine passage to change sides or taking
the longer external building route, rather than picking two similar radii.

Cost: an interruption can create an arbitrary dead end or remove a needed defender
response. Show the affected bay's onward/return path and assess the same decision
after an objective interaction before selecting the interruption. It need not be
at a fixed compass direction. A continuous annulus remains valid if a developed
design demonstrates a genuinely different job for it.

## Handoff

Develop A with explicit shell/deck/void regions and one radial section. The useful
element from C is the relationship between huge lifting machinery and occupied
human-scale work decks; there is no need to add an entire second reactor or copy
its open-floor organization. B's rising route is optional, not an automatic extra
feature. Record whether SP1–SP3 are adopted, tested, deferred or declined. If a
substantial proposal is adopted, another reviewer should assess its gameplay
consequences. Proceed to detailed spatial/visibility and strategy review once the
selected drawing has real thresholds, cover and level interfaces.
