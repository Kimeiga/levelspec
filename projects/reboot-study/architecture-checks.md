# Architectural relationships to preserve in production

Root visually inspected the following retained research photographs in addition to the original plan/render comparisons and S01 alternative drawings. These are inspiration observations, not measured dimensions or fidelity requirements. Source credits/URLs and original image identities are in each map's reference manifest.

| Map / photograph | Observed relationship | Production question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ninefold / `references/images/gallery-07.png` | Adjoining occupied buildings have different rooflines, deep shop fronts, projecting balconies and visible setbacks. The street belongs to a neighborhood, rather than a courtyard enclosed by one uniform wall. | Does the factory-underpass/loft read as one supported building among distinct residential volumes? Are accessible roofs and inaccessible upper buildings unambiguous? |
| Galini / `references/images/symi-harbor.jpg` | A long stair and inhabited landings follow substantial retaining faces beside real rocky terrain; waterfront businesses and upper houses occupy different shelves. | Do stairs, bridgeheads, terraces and buildings have a plausible load-bearing/retaining relationship, with coherent town and terrain beyond the playable limit? |
| Last Light / `references/images/rolling-foodcourt.jpg` | Food stalls have thick recessed fronts, columns, overhead structure and neighboring service doors within one roofed volume. | Are stalls and service edges actual volumes that organize the food court, rather than low cover rectangles on an exposed platform? Do the two atria retain visible roof structure and storefront depth? |

An additional [GNTO bridge photograph](references/stone-bridge-gnto.jpg), obtained
from [Visit Greece](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/experiences/culture/archaeological-sites-monuments/famous-stone-bridges),
was visually inspected after Galini selected the ravine. It shows a continuous stone
arch and broad bank abutments, with the parapet belonging to the masonry. Its deck
is humped; Galini's flat gameplay decks would be an invented adaptation. The photo
does not establish map dimensions, load capacity or player clearance. Source metadata
and image hash are retained in [the manifest](references/stone-bridge-source.json).

Use actual roof-on exterior, cutaway and occupied interior views for the final comparison. A cutaway cannot establish missing roofs, and a roof-on overview cannot establish interior circulation. Styling does not substitute for enclosure, supported slabs, meaningful voids or usable clearances.
