# Independent fidelity review — r04

**Verdict: accepted for foundational exterior recreation.** The two remaining required repairs from r03 are visibly present, and the earlier accepted architecture/detail categories remain intact. This signoff means the demo recreates the concept's principal exterior identity; it does not claim an exact replica of its illustrated realism or all its incidental detail.

## Evidence

Compared the original full concept, its three detail insets, and the established source inventory against r04 `comparison.png`, `hero.png`, `upper.png`, `balcony.png`, `shops.png`, `roof.png`, and `side.png`. `build.json` and all seven capture entries identify GLB `d7c5f1bb2e3cabfc1064d40645adf1577fec79c9f4536e095e864adfc2dd2ad3`; capture errors are empty. Those records identify the reviewed candidate. Build/shader success is not the basis for the visual verdict.

## Closure of the two remaining targets

- **Fragmented masonry:** `upper.png` now shows jagged peeled boundaries, smaller surrounding chips, internal gray tonal variation, and darkened streaks beneath/near ledges. The change remains legible in hero. Balcony fascia, corner/base, and roof hut share the architectural aging while thin metal stays comparatively restrained. This closes the prior smooth-gray-island failure at the foundational threshold.
- **Striped valance:** `hero.png`, `balcony.png`, and `shops.png` show a hanging striped fabric edge with a segmented/scalloped lower silhouette. It belongs to one broad canopy and preserves the separate muted-green left canopy and exposed corbels. The r02 red-fascia/two-bay regression has not returned.

## Preserved accepted identity

The four occupied stories, broad banked facade and right return, clipped/wrapped corner, jade upper bands and dark plinth, low bracketed balcony with green rails/fan/door, differentiated shops, three main signs, external ACs/cages/services, asymmetric roof hut/tank, and grounded sidewalk still read together as the intended corner tong lau. Window curtains/screens/open sections and layered shop stock remain visible. No moss has been introduced.

## Residual differences, stated without reopening accepted scope

- The concept has a denser, more continuous mix of grime, small fractures, paint layers, and worn edge irregularity. r04 remains cleaner and more regular between its large damaged fields; its trim and roof interior are comparatively simple.
- Tile is somewhat more olive than the concept's cooler blue-green; the awning reads more brown/tan than salmon-red/cream. Their main material/color roles are nonetheless distinguishable and correctly placed.
- Window internals, shop stock, wiring, pipe clusters, and peripheral roof/side attachments are less varied and less densely accumulated than the concept. The shop is brighter and more orderly, with a more obvious central clear floor area.
- The concept's illustrative softness, perspective, and ambient shading are not reproduced exactly by this runtime-lit model. The camera is approximate. None of these observations justifies claiming measured dimensional correspondence or unseen construction.

These are meaningful limits to any claim of high visual fidelity, but they are not unresolved foundational defects in the inspected demonstration. No additional art iteration is required by this review. Architectural plausibility is recorded separately in `architecture-r04.md`; this fidelity signoff does not certify enclosure, structural engineering, collision, navigation, or gameplay.
