## What changed and why it is worthwhile

The useful change is the destination and orientation of the ascent, not its approximately 18.15 m run. C01 arrived northward near `(37,56,+3.6)`; C02 turns onto the tangent landing at `(46.2,52.65,+3.6)`. The point threshold is now 3.509 measured seconds from that landing, while the retained north receiving area requires a separate 4.411-second journey. The old straight arrival is guarded, so it cannot silently remain a second exit. A player must choose immediate west point pressure or north regrouping.

Early leader and three-meter follower samples can see the side hold `(43,55,+3.6)` while the direct landing remains occluded. Mid/late samples reveal both. The standing side opponent can already see the standing leader fully in the nine-point sample; crouching does not reliably rescue the climber, because that standing opponent sees 9/9 of the crouched leader at all three samples. Only the early crouched follower reduces that mixed-posture sample to 3/9. This is a readable side-first contest with later landing commitment, not a protected rush or a universal crouch advantage. North support at `(36,62,+3.6)` sees the named landing, but the turn, guards and food-court masses still restrict what it can support beyond it.

The lower G1 journey passes beside the ascent and beneath its raised end. The exact authored lower crossing succeeds in both directions, with a sampled minimum 2.443 m overhead for the inspection body. Direct upper/lower pairs across the soffit are blocked. Two teammates can occupy related space while facing distinct jobs; one does not acquire a firing or healing line merely by being close on the plan. This is a substantial spatial improvement over merely drawing another parallel route.

**Full-five opening and repeated waves.** West can assemble near the point earlier on these chains. The benefit repeats after deaths; it cannot be dismissed as an opening-only difference. At the controller speed both public chains arrive after the hypothetical unlock. At the separate 5.5 m/s paper hypothesis P01's nominated west chain was about 17.49 seconds versus east 20.62, potentially creating pre-unlock occupation. Those estimates are not a target-game result. The actual early side challenge and narrower flight impose real costs, but there is no evidence that they numerically compensate for the time saving. A full west public commitment also leaves service/north unoccupied by its own team. An opponent contesting C-SIDE spends a body away from the scoring area and must expose it to the climb; it is not a free sixth defender.

**West 3+2 versus an opposing five.** A tank/damage/support public trio plus damage/support service pair is credible only with a deliberate release time or staging decision. A 13.280-second no-combat arrival gap is too large to label simultaneous support. The trio can wait before committing, use north receiving, or change its split; it cannot be promised survival on point until the pair arrives. If both supports follow the public trio, the service pair has no independent support. East's unchanged, smaller public/service gap makes a coordinated split more accessible there. That is an explicit asymmetry: west specializes in direct return, while east preserves easier split synchronization. Neither is declared the winner without the game rules and players.

**Failed entry, 3v3 and asymmetric recovery.** A surviving west trio can give up immediate point pressure and retreat from the new landing to receiving in 4.411 seconds. From there the known direct north switch takes another roughly 14 seconds; the whole landing-to-switch-end chain is 18.465 seconds. This preserves a meaningful recovery option while avoiding the false claim that the new arrival inherits C01's immediate north positioning. One player staying on point while two take the 21.486-second lower change remains an isolated hold, not a rapid rescue. Returning west teammates help the public side sooner, but cannot thereby reinforce a separated north/service survivor.

**Two survivors under pursuit or a returning wave meeting a retreat.** The clear flight shrinks from 5.76 to 3.76 m. Inner/center/outer controller paths succeed, with a 3.2 m admitted body envelope; that does not verify tank/support passing, teammate collision or simultaneous traffic on the receiving turn. Reverse traversal and a 25.678-second point-side home retreat chain show an escape exists, not that it is safe while pursued. The faster reinforcement and more constrained passing are both retained costs. C01 spawn-front restrictions and lack of spawn-protection guarantees remain unchanged.

## Disposition, improvements and validation

**Keep the candidate.** The P01 decision rule is satisfied: the actual curve changes information order, arrival choice and shared-volume use, while north retreat and lower traversal remain available. No sample demonstrates uncontested universal point access or an unanswerable information post. Faster west arrival, slower west split coordination, separate northern recovery and narrower passing are intentional costs accepted for this blockout. They are not presented as a proven balance equation. No additional curve, spiral, route or cover quota follows.

The strongest next design question is whether full-west rushes and repeated west waves consistently erase east's ability to establish a contested front. Test those alongside west 3+2 with a planned delay and failed-entry recovery. If that dominance is demonstrated, the focused improvement is to redirect the **landing-to-point departure toward the existing north receiving area**, recovering a staging commitment while retaining the curve and lower crossing. That would sacrifice the current direct-arrival benefit and require a new local timing/visibility return; it is a deferred alternative, not a repair justified by today's finite evidence. Do not preemptively add an opaque wall or move spawns to force equal stopwatch numbers.
