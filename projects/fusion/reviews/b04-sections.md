## Evidence and limits

Inspected all 32 actual images in `generated/fusion/capture-b04-sections`: 31
local views and the low machinery support view. The manifest matches B04 and
records no browser errors. Also retained the nine original B04 views and ordinary
exterior view; architecture/source correspondence has its own report. Raw captures
preceded sibling B04 gameplay verdicts. The reviewer previously proposed L02
clearance corrections, so this is an independent return on author implementation,
not a blind first exposure to the layout.

`generated/fusion/sections-b04-r2/report.json` records 31 successful standing-body
placements, all ten actual spawn starts placed, and four successful performed
walking traces: southwest arc both ways, gallery ascent and gallery A descent.
Stair camera feet come from those 0.04 m replay traces rather than a fictitious
smooth slope. Early requested stair-height placement failures in r1 are superseded;
they do not demonstrate a broken stair. Other views use directly supported feet.

Standing camera height is 1.674 m above grounded feet. The two low-eye comparisons
use 1.023 m over the same standing-body placement; they are **optical studies, not
performed crouch movement or crouched capsule tests**. Captures use 75° vertical
FOV at 4:3, approximately 91.31° horizontal. No multiplayer body occlusion,
ballistics, penetration, grenade paths, movement timing or trade success is proven.
The original `power-hall-b` free camera was too high for its ramp location; use
the grounded B-entry and trench series for player-height conclusions.

## A landing / near W-mouth qualification

Independent script and original results:
`reviews/evidence/gallery-landing-rays-b04.mjs` and `.json`. These use the exported
B04 runtime's complete opaque render mesh, double-sided triangles and finite rays
with 15 mm endpoint exclusion. Six prescribed platform positions at x=-27.5,-25,-23
and y=15,17.3 use feet Z=3.21 and standing eye Z=4.884; nine targets each give 54
rays. These six coordinates were not independently body-placed or walked, although
their platform footprint is supported by source and adjacent captures.

At (-27.5,15,3.21), both A plant (-34,14,1.674) and the **near** W-mouth
(-19,4,1.674) have an open optical ray. At the other five coordinates that near-mouth
ray is blocked. None of the six sees inner W-bridge center (-12,4,1.674). Do not
generalize the inner-bridge result into complete separation of A and every W-port
position.

The plant and near-mouth horizontal bearings are -171.254° and -52.306°:
**118.948° apart**, exceeding the capture's approximately 91.309° horizontal FOV.
One stationary coordinate can look toward both by turning, but does not hold both
within a single ordinary frame. The near-mouth angle may help a teammate leaving
the ground connector, with the upper player exposed to reciprocal optical view
and looking away from A. This is a plausible bounded support relationship, not
demonstrated firing, effortless multi-entry coverage or a required geometry fault.
No extra camera for this exact coordinate was inspected before the scope expansion;
that limit remains explicit instead of claiming a visual return that did not occur.

## Return scope

The raised roof, separate gallery aperture, guarded interfaces and named east bypass
survive the compiled return. Low cover produces actual posture-dependent optical
differences, and upper/lower routes produce meaningful visibility costs. The
available evidence supports retaining B04 as a physically inspected design baseline.
It does not establish competitive balance, complete hold/frontier coverage, reliable
multiplayer trades or finished game mechanics. The new complexity request should
retain these observations as regression targets while revisiting the layout.
