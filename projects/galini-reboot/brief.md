# Galini reboot — Phase A brief

Revision S01, 2026-11-09. This is an architectural/spatial exploration of the existing Galini map. Both alternatives remain unselected. There is no reboot AML, compiled geometry, navigation claim, timing claim or balance acceptance at this phase.

## Preserved user intent

The original brief records a calm Greek coastal city with cliffy terrain for CT-versus-T, 5v5 defusal. The user rejected a grid-like/SimCity appearance and requested bendy paths, stairs and plazas. Later feedback rejected insufficient playable depth and overly simple connections. The current conversation asks for a reboot using the new iterative graph/spatial exploration and independent review workflow, using real architectural reference images, explicit floor levels and deliberate cross-level movement.

Retain the original inspection body: radius 0.30 m, standing height 1.80 m, crouching height 1.10 m, step 0.30 m, maximum slope 42 degrees. Standing/crouching inspection eyes are 1.674/1.023 m; harness walking speeds are 3.4/1.7 m/s. All indispensable routes should work without a boost, jump-only gap or unsupported drop.

## Previous author choices reopened

The old southwest attacker arrival, northeast defender arrival, Chapel Court A at +4 m, Boat Court B at 0 m, broad three-front arrangement, district positions, cover, exact loop connections and existing footprints were author choices. They are not user-mandated anchors. The new drawings retain a civic and working-waterfront contrast as a useful identity hypothesis, but move site levels and test different relationships. Site and spawn circles on the alternatives are provisional orientation aids, not locked game entities.

The original level is preserved byte-for-byte. Baseline hashes and inspected historical evidence are retained in `planning/baseline.json`. Original successful checks do not transfer to the reboot.

## Current design hypotheses

The geographic envelope is approximately 124–132 m across and 108–114 m deep, measured in meters, right-handed Z-up. This is a design assumption informed by the old scale, not a measured Greek town. Occupied street levels are 0, +4 and +8 m, connected only by drawn stairs; apparent XY proximity and view rays do not create movement. Complete buildings define negative-space streets, courts and porch pockets; selected roofed passages make some buildings spatially consequential.

The provisional ruleset carried from the old design assumes rifles, grenades/smokes/flashes, CS-like economy, a 115-second round, 40-second planted clock, 3.2-second plant and 5/10-second kit/no-kit defuse. These are design assumptions, not implemented gameplay. The old planning estimate of 5 m/s running speed must not be substituted for the inspection harness speed when reporting a measured walk.

## Two independent spatial organizations

**A — Harbor Fold:** a working harbor wraps the cliff foot beneath an inhabited market shelf and upper chapel district. Several stair choices create short visual relationships with longer access journeys. Intervening wineries, shops, boat sheds and housing form changes of direction and occupied intermediate territory. The eastern descent is an actual two-stage 8→4→0 m stair journey with a turn at +4 m.

**B — Ravine Quarter:** two inhabited shoulders face a dry ravine. Middle and upper bridges cross at different north/south positions and arrive in through-building gateways. A low shore route goes around the ravine toe. Upper civic A and lower waterfront B lie on different shoulders, so district access and cross-town transfer are different commitments. The ravine is inaccessible scenic terrain, not a jumping route.

Neither organization is a literal conversion of a lab graph. Both were drawn as actual building/site proposals after looking at multiple graph and spatial alternatives. They remain open to independent replacement proposals.

## Phase A gate and later work

Review the raw drawings before the author's exploration rationale. Independent reviewers should identify interesting journeys, weak or absent choices, excessive openness, lost connections and stronger alternative arrangements. In particular, test whether the building masses create real streets instead of decorative objects on open pads; whether intermediate places support meaningful decisions; and whether level changes produce distinct territories rather than stair collections.

After selection/development, a separate detailed layout must resolve every floor boundary, cliff edge, roofed passage clearance, stair landing, doorway and section. Independent layout/visibility and strategy review precede production AML. Later compiled walking, camera-verified roof-on/sectioned views and fresh architecture/combat review will be necessary. The current drawings do not satisfy those later gates.
