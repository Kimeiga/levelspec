# Kiln Yard — 5v5 defusal brief

Original terrorist versus counter-terrorist two-site defusal map, no respawns. The user withdrew the steep-incline requirement and requested a good map, with interesting spatial relationships instead of isolated rooms joined by long straight corridors. Use irregular room and map shapes, useful adjacency, shaped connectors, local alternatives, bounded vantage/hiding positions and purposeful intervening space. Randomized starting sketches are a design aid; the chosen layout must be deliberately reshaped and reviewed.

Setting: a ceramic works with a loading court, arcade, kiln forecourt, central saddle, wash yard, inspection passage, two objective spaces, and a shared service yard. Plant A is the press court; plant B is the basin yard. Ground-level circulation is intentional; no mandatory jump, boost, drop, ladder or crouch-only passage. The outer footprint follows the spaces. Solid kiln and storage masses separate neighboring routes. Their boundaries and traversable/observation openings must be explicit.

Provisional LevelSpec player: height 1.8m, radius .3m, crouch 1.1m, step .25m, maximum slope 42°. Actual reviewer eyes are 1.674m standing / 1.023m crouched; 3.4m/s standing, 1.7m/s crouched. These are harness settings, not authenticated CS2 movement. Ground floors are Z=0, native slabs .25m thick. Walls are opaque physical solids. Colors do not establish bullet resistance. Bomb sites and spawns are metadata; shooting, plant/defuse timers, utility, economy, sound, damage and multiplayer collision are not simulated.

Assume conventional rifle/flash/smoke tactics for hypotheses only; no design acceptance depends on a precise smoke radius or penetration value. Review five-player defaults and rushes, a 4+1 split, site loss and rotations, 3v5 retake, 2v2 and 1v2 post-plant. Distinguish drawing hypotheses, compiled inspection, sampled physical/visibility measurements and actual multiplayer playtests. Balance remains a playtest question.

Required evidence: a readable labeled layout with shared origin, room/route footprints, individual cover IDs, portals, plant areas, and the sight aperture's supported observer/target positions; two independent layout review scopes plus focused interfaces; native AML source; route/clearance validation and collision-aware traversal; actual browser views and visibility checks; independent blockout review. The preview is an inspection harness, not a playable bomb-game implementation.
