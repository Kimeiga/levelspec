# Floor-plan skill forward test

Reviewed `reference-level-authoring/SKILL.md` and its schematic, staged-authoring,
independent-review and reconstruction references on 2026-11-01. This was a textual
workflow test; no architectural source, plan or 3D model was authored or changed.

## Scenario and expected behavior

For a partially photographed split-level courtyard with two circular facade
openings, closed doors of unknown swing, occluded stairs and unseen route segments,
scattered rectangles and photo traces do **not** satisfy the gate. Before any 3D
trial, the agent must inspect originals, distinguish observations from estimates,
and draw editable, coordinated top-down architectural plans for every relevant
level. The plans need common orientation/scale, named levels and spaces, courtyard
and stairwell voids, wall cut thickness, observed opening constraints, key dimensions
and a legend. Stage one can defer detailed enclosure while preserving its known
locations and clearance constraints.

Door locations use conventional wall-opening symbols, with unknown hinge/type/swing
labeled rather than fabricated. Closed doors do not prove unseen rooms or access.
Windows interrupt walls with frame/glazing conventions; the facade's circular shape
belongs in elevation, not as circles or holes in the floor. Openings outside the
plan cut get labeled projected treatment. Stairs show flight limits, supported
treads, UP/DN, landings, breaks and consistent flight/level IDs across sheets;
occluded counts and endpoints stay explicitly uncertain. Known routes require
supporting floors and coherent estimated junctions, while hidden routes remain
bounded unknowns.

Independent plan review must inspect the editable source and actual readable
rendered sheets against originals, checking conventional readability and spatial
fidelity separately. Freeze the reviewed revision, resolve supported errors and
only then build dependent circulation geometry. Reuse requires unchanged reviewed
relationships; affected relationships must be redrawn and re-reviewed.

Supplementary sections, elevations and enlarged details are explicitly optional
when unnecessary. This scenario likely needs a section for ambiguous split-level
stair relationships and an elevation or detail for facade-opening projection;
it does not require a comprehensive set of detail sheets. Supplements cannot
replace the coordinated plans.

## Finding and resolution

The initial pass found one loophole in `references/schematic-workflow.md`,
“Author and review before modeling,” step 2: preliminary 3D studies were allowed
without an explicit prior review condition. The coordinator corrected it during
this review. Reinspection confirms that preliminary studies now require the
relevant floor plans and necessary supplements to pass independent review first,
and model-derived sections cannot replace or independently validate pre-model
plans. The gate now handles this scenario and its uncertainty consistently.

No additional mandatory-detail overreach was found: requirements are scoped to
relevant levels/current-stage geometry, unknown dimensions need no false precision,
and unnecessary supplementary sheets are expressly excluded.
