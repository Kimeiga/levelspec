# Design and playtest hypotheses — L04 / R05

The retained proposal offsets a ground factory and an elevated east waterworks terrace. Two-roof-site/single-core and directly stacked-objective arrangements were rejected because they concentrated access and made callouts ambiguous. Shared residential masses shape real winding alleys; these are not detached rooms connected across empty land.

West progresses through Market and Sink pockets before Drain and A. Mid offers immediate A-short pressure or the SM climb to Service/B; its gallery branch is a committed observation perch, with no claimed window traversal. Laundry commits attackers to two climbs separated by a dogleg landing. Service reaches B's west frontier through the long upper wrap. B-tank divides local fights and some views; some northeastern offsets still see both entry frontiers and plant within a narrow sector. No claim that every hold must look away is made.

Independent layout reviews found and closed missing A-short connectivity, an obstructive/orphan SA stair (removed), S3 landing overlap, a narrow SM/Service connection, converging B entries, and B-crates across its entry. Compiled review then closed fixture-back slivers and 2 mm portal seams, retaining player size and checks. See review records for revision-scoped findings.

The approximate 3.6 s center-spawn CT arrival lead at B is a risk to test across all spawn offsets and real movement speeds. B retakes normally contest Service/west first and change tank-side pressure from there.

## Visibility decisions

The longest retained sampled view is44.18 m across the T feed, a deliberate rear connector. Additional bounded relationships connect Laundry to an east-feed offset (~36 m), Service down the SM/Pipe corridor (~35.65 m), and a CT rear offset toward Drain (~35.34 m). These afford information/long-range holding at the cost of local position and attention elsewhere. Reciprocal compiled views were reviewed; they do not expose the configured T spawn pocket or create a whole-map panorama. Retain as explicit weapon/utility/playtest risks.

The gallery aperture has sill z4.4/head z5.6. Standing inspection eye z5.06 sees into A; crouch eye z4.223 drops below its sill. This demonstrates camera concealment at the inspected pose, not full body/bullet protection. Low fixtures are 1.2 m tall and must be evaluated relative to opponent elevation; the gallery can defeat some ground cover.

## Engine playtest priorities

1. **5v5 opening:** record each spawn offset's first contact, B defender setup time, split/stacked executes, and Mid defender allocation. Compare1A/1Mid/3B versus2A/1Mid/2B CT setups.
2. **5T rush each site:** measure passing/body blocking through bends and20-riser flights; test smoke/flash/incendiary denial duration and teammate trading from follower positions.
3. **2v2 postplant A:** rear/east retake against press/window holds; B: Service entry, tank-side trades and already-owned Laundry support. Do not assume a fresh Laundry rotation fits the bomb clock.
4. **1v2 retakes and 1v1 late rotations:** measure remaining defuse/clear time and whether an information hold can safely abandon its front.
5. Verify target-engine dimensions, actual FOV, sound separation between levels, penetration, grenade sky/ceiling interactions, boost/climb access to cover, and falling behavior. No mandatory jump/drop route is authored.

These remain hypotheses until engine or human playtesting provides evidence.
