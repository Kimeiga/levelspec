# Exploration and open alternatives

Two latest-engine experiments each ran 3×1,400 proposals: `ninefold-well-31` at verticality .85 and `ninefold-seam-74` at .35. Both begin from the engine's four-node starter and retain three alternatives. Packets are `explore-well.json` / `explore-seam.json`; `engine-source.json` freezes the source version. All six graphs and shared-volume interpretations were visually inspected in `exploration-board.png`. Derived demands and unresolved geometry remain in the packets/summary; no selected graph is a playable map.

The first well run suggests several dense local groups whose circulation can be shared across an inhabited layer, while its very sparse alternative turns into an oversized circuit and offers less intervening play. The seam family suggests a long journey whose intermediate choices are close in XY but lie on different floors. Its shared-volume outputs also show why direct graph-to-floor conversion is inadequate: broad ribbons, overlapping envelopes and irregular fractional topology can erase the intended choices. We extracted relationships and discarded their literal polygon geometry and one-node/one-room reading.

The two authored alternatives deliberately diverge. A organizes lower commercial courts, an occupied middle gallery loop, and a roof tank precinct around open-to-sky voids. B organizes a bent street below a U-shaped factory loft, with a longer roof detour connecting the loft's approach districts. A's upper objective is an outdoor precinct; B's upper objective inhabits a larger building volume above real lower circulation. Neither repeats the old street-factory plus distant rooftop arrangement with only renamed connectors.

No preference is frozen. Independent reviewers should compare the raw brief and drawings first. Detailed wall openings, opaque screens, cover, exact stair construction and a small number of roof/section studies follow selection; do not rush from this phase into production AML or claim balance from topology.
