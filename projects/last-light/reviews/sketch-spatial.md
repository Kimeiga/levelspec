# S01 quick spatial-interest review

Verdict: **promising**. Independent first look at `sketch.svg` and its rendered PNG, before author rationale. Brief: new 5v5 hero-shooter map themed after a dead mall.

The diagonally opposed anchors open into different arms of a shopping concourse, then meet around a single octagonal fountain court. The west arcade and east cinema each add a local stacked volume rather than a bridge spanning a set of parallel corridors. Journeys from A can change between the south promenade and west arcade before committing to the court; B has the rotated equivalent. The court is a plausible mall gathering space and the lofts relate occupied retail edges to it. This is sufficient spatial substance to develop.

Consequential development notes:

- The actual south court edge meets the south promenade only at X34–36/Y24 (2 m); the north equivalent is X44–46/Y48. They read as larger mouths in the sketch but are much narrower than the lateral mouths. Draw deliberate usable door/threshold widths for a hero team before production; widen those receiving ends if they are intended as primary approaches.
- Each stair currently reaches its loft at a corner only: west flight ends across X10–16/Y34, while loft starts X16/Y34; the east pair has the rotated problem. Add a genuine top landing and positive-width connection. This is a layout-development issue, not a request for compiled proof at sketch stage.
- Preserve a useful ground approach beneath/beside each loft, and develop what portion of the court that loft can see. Full opposing panoramic loft views could flatten the otherwise distinct entrance decisions.

This is interest acceptance for development, not a gameplay balance or physical-reachability pass. No earlier unrelated proposals were inspected, so novelty relative to them is unassessed. Detailed dimensions, enclosure, cover, respawn pressure and compiled movement/visibility remain open.
