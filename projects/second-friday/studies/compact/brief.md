# Second Friday

User brief: a new 5v5 hero-shooter map themed after a flea market in a Middle Eastern country.

Chosen setting: an invented secondhand market in Amman, Jordan, informed by photographs of the Friday Market. This is thematic invention, not a reconstruction. Assumed mode: round-based single-point control with sides swapped between rounds. No target hero roster or engine has been specified.

The first proposal is `sketch.svg` (L01). North is +Y; dimensions in meters. Spawn anchors west (7,16,0), east (77,48,0), capture area (34–50,24–40,0). The sketch shows full-width connections between adjoining shaded spaces unless a dark mass intervenes. Terrace ground beneath is solid retaining structure; only the paired stairs lead to its +2.4m walking level.
