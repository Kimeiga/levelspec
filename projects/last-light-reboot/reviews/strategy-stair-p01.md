## Actual changed relationship

The18.15 m,80° arc is nearly the same ascent run as the old18 m flight. The large journey saving comes from its new arrival and onward routing: old X1 reaches `(37,56,+3.6)` facing north, while the new tangent landing centers near `(46.2,52.65,+3.6)` and approaches the west point threshold more directly. That changes the value of U1: northern receiving is still accessible, but no longer lies naturally on the nominated shortest point approach.

The lower G1 journey now runs alongside the ascending arc and beneath its raised final portion. This is a useful shared atrium relationship with real simultaneous occupation, not an extra graph edge or an automatic upper-to-lower firing connection. The existing observation opening remains, but its post moves to C-INFO `(42,48.7,3.6)`; occupying it remains a separate information commitment. The local review must verify that the new soffit, guard cut and former-arrival closure actually preserve both journeys.

## Focused native return and decision rule

Prototype P01 separately and preserve C01. The macro return needs only the changed X1/U1/G1 interfaces and their consequences, reusing the rest of C01:

1. Compare controller travel on the actual west-public chain, the receiving retreat, north switch and lower-change route against frozen C01; record real endpoints and source hash. Keep east's unchanged travel as context, not as a demand for exact equality.
2. Capture leader/follower/opponent before, during and after the curve, at both normal postures and inner/center/outer travel lines. Record first side exposure, first landing exposure, continued same-threat support, actual landing departure and retreat. Include the retained north supporter and the lower G1 neighbor.
3. Revisit an opening full five versus3+2 and a returning wave with the actual travel/visibility findings. If the direct west approach can preoccupy the point uncontested and repeatedly reinforce without a meaningful exposure or position cost, retain C01 or compare a focused receiving/approach adjustment. Do not assert that a longer stair or an extra opaque wall automatically repairs that; it could erase the new shared-volume benefit.
4. Keep the experiment only if its new tangent arrival, early information order and lower neighbor produce a worthwhile alternative while the narrower flight and faster public rhythm remain coherent. Otherwise decline it and retain C01. No requirement to add curvature elsewhere follows.

The strongest optional improvement is already the candidate itself: it gives the mall a different ascent/arrival relationship, with a deliberately altered threat order. No additional connector, symmetric copy, spiral or unrelated map restructuring is proposed. Final competitive timing still needs a destination game; the native return can establish whether the geometric costs and claimed support actually exist.
