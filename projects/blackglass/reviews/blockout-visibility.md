## Reconciled warnings retained for playtest

**V↔F long optical lane.** The longest R04 ray is62.418m: low eyes from V feet(18,88,+6.01) to F feet(22,26,+.01). Its path crosses B’s south PC portal atZ3.549, just below the3.6m head, skirts PC-rack’s west edge and continues through lower gallery/F portals. Paired actual captures show a vertically bounded peephole through several transitions, rather than a bare62m room. A.25m supported-position study over V X17–19/Y86–89 and F X21–23/Y24–27 found0 visible pairs whenever either optical eye is1.674; at both1.023,2,870/12,402 pairs were visible. This is therefore a real low-eye lane, not a single XY tangent artifact.

Retain provisionally: it does not see a spawn or the entire lower route, attackers can choose P instead of L, and occupying V costs the player attention toward U and the B/S6 approach. Those alternatives do not prove the lane is fair. Test crouched visibility, standing-body lower-part exposure, damage/penetration and whether repeated low peeks dominate the F-L crossing. If oppressive, revise the PC doorway/equipment relationship locally; do not change scan thresholds to hide it.

**Four A standing sector candidates.** Feet(68,76),(70,76),(70,74),(70,72) at+12 see all nine configured entry samples within horizontal spans79.93–96.75°. They also face the plant-side part of the room. The actual candidate and reverse images confirm a strong northeast hold. It requires standing exposure to retain all entry views: the crouched variant loses entry visibility behind low cover. A main can pressure the same visible frontage, the reactor creates a close wrap choice, and rear access is a separate direction that costs additional route control. Retain as an intentional strong hold hypothesis, with a priority2v2/1v2 postplant test. The sector flag is not proof that a defender can react, shoot or delay every entry.332 other holds have insufficient evidence; those are unknown, not safety passes.

## Appearance and remaining scope

The authored blue-black equipment, cyan status bands, orange reactor, suspended floor plates, high cliff wall and tower communicate the requested high-tech villain facility at blockout detail. Exterior silhouettes and room/stair hierarchy read in overview; A’s rock-facing opening and the tower above its walls make the mountain perceptible at player scale. B’s outward window currently looks into a largely white scenic void: it conveys an edge/open drop, but a finished environmental art pass could provide richer distant terrain. This does not establish an additional route or require geometry changes for this prototype.

The overview intentionally frames the facility and crops parts of background mountain at the top/right; it is not a complete landscape portrait. The ink view has clear structural contours and directional shadow separation. Materials mode produces conspicuous diagonal banding in close views; inspection color is clearer for the final hero. Roof-hidden aerial/interior captures are cutaways, and the roof-enabled spawn captures distinguish them from the compiled optical scene.

No human multiplayer, target-engine utility/audio/weapon physics, timer pressure, network peeking, whole-body continuous visibility, crowd blocking or continuous capsule sweep was performed here. These limits remain specific follow-up work; they are not unresolved defects in the delivered geometry evidence.
