# Scenarios, evidence and playtest planning

## Select populations for the brief

Cover the intended format and relevant stress cases, not a fixed matrix of 1v1,
2v2, 5v5 and 25v5. Ask whether asymmetry is a designed challenge, temporary casualty
state, equipment difference, or stress test. Evaluate it against that intent rather
than demanding equal odds. Note coordination/skill assumptions, equipment, remaining
time and the objective state. A local choke analysis does not certify the whole map.

Useful questions by situation:

- **1v1 / isolated survivor:** information, exposure, repositioning, objective forcing,
  and whether geometry permits the player to change the next duel.
- **2v2 / paired play:** trade visibility and timing, crossfires, support versus
  separate coverage, and whether utility can disrupt or restore the relationship.
- **Team format such as 5v5:** role allocation, map control, rotation cost, credible
  fakes, staging capacity, multi-directional pressure and retakes where applicable.
- **Large or asymmetric groups such as 25v5:** doorway/route throughput, stacking,
  congestion, body blocking, overlapping fire/support, reinforcement capacity and
  objectives that constrain numerical advantage. Five sequential duels do not
  establish that a passage supports a coordinated group.
- **Respawning/arena modes:** pickups, spawn exposure, recovery, reinforcement loops,
  movement shortcuts and whether one territory locks opponents out indefinitely.

Use only applicable cases. Intentional quiet/staging space need not create a new
dilemma every meter. Compare the attention required to track threats with the
information and time players actually have.

## Make predictions inspectable

Name initial positions, information, resources and intent. Trace the response as
well as the attack: where can a defender delay, retreat or punish; what can the
attackers change if the expected plan fails? Include behavior that challenges the
author's intent, such as holding the entrance instead of advancing or bypassing an
attractive tower. Consider different timings and opponents learning a repeated setup.

For consequential information and coordination claims, compare fresh versus stale
information and support intact versus absent or suppressed. Trace parallel tasks,
their dependencies and a disrupted handoff; include bypassing an off-site threat
where feasible. Record the trigger for leaving a hold and whether the return can
still affect the objective. Low route usage alone does not disprove a credible
flank threat, and an open route alone does not establish a timely response.

Attribute source evidence precisely: developer intent, practitioner prescription,
complete article text, partial interactive instructions, inspected diagrams and
reviewed footage are different scopes. Date mechanics separately from page metadata;
historical pages may contain updated panels. Unread downloads and unsupported success
badges do not validate a design. Preserve contrary examples and transfer limits.

For vantage points record observer location/height, visible approaches, exposed body,
access cost and exit. Inspect reverse views and nearby supporting positions. A
theoretical flank after the timer expires is not useful counterplay; a simultaneous
crossfire need not be clearable alone if team tools can feasibly contest it.

For map-wide visibility, entry-angle warnings and low-cover sequences, use the
[visibility and cover review](visibility-and-cover.md). Its section inventory and
height-aware predictions apply before modeling and again to actual compiled views.

## Keep evidence levels separate

- **Drawing hypothesis:** inferred relationships and predicted behavior; not actual 3D proof.
- **Compiled inspection:** actual camera/geometry observations on a named revision.
- **Measured mechanic:** travel, visibility or collision check using stated settings,
  assumptions and tools. An estimate from distance/speed is not a measured rotation.
- **Observed play:** behavior recorded from actual participants or explicitly named
  bot simulations, with populations, equipment, mode and limits. Bots are not evidence
  of human deception, communication or competitive adaptation.

Do not claim crouching, grenade coverage, penetration or audio propagation merely
because a scene renderer permits a camera position or a material has a suggestive name.
Use engine checks where supported; otherwise preserve the uncertainty.

## Propose or analyze playtests

State a prediction before a focused change. Where practical compare one change at
a time: width, crate placement/height, corner shape or fallback distance. Match
player skill/equipment and inspect more than one objective phase. Record successful
and failed attempts, opponent adaptations and player explanations, not just a
highlight or side win rate.

Look for whether teams choose differently against different setups, can recover
from disrupted plans, and can explain advantages and costs. Distinguish an unused
option that is dominated from one that is undiscovered, mechanically awkward or
deliberately situational. More routes, even route usage and longer fights are not
universal quality measures. When play is unavailable, deliver a prioritized test
plan and qualified spatial findings rather than claiming gameplay validation.
