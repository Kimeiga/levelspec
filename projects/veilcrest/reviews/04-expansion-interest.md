# Veilcrest — expansion interest recheck

**Verdict: promising.**

Independent brief recheck of the major r06 expansion. The reviewer visually inspected `reviews/evidence/layout-r06.png`, then the current `layout.svg` / `layout.json` boundaries and opening spans before new author rationale. Brief remains a 5v5 CT/T defuse map in a secret mansion nested in steep mountains, with the user's new request for substantially greater size and complexity. This is a direction assessment, not the detailed layout or blockout acceptance gate.

The expansion adds consequential journeys rather than merely stretching the old routes. On the west, garden → orangery → music room offers a new approach into the salon and a continuation toward the west gallery. On the east, drain → pump house → wine passage → cellar wing provides a second route around the vault and another uphill transfer to the CT side. The playable library changes the former solid central separator into an interior circulation loop around a retained core, linking hall and salon in a more convincing mansion plan. These additions create decisions about continuing around a wing, entering an objective from the side, or pushing beyond it toward rear territory. The estate reads substantially larger and less simple while retaining the upper/lower mountain-cut identity.

The original garden/front/drain departures remain, but the new side wings and interior loop materially change reconnections and territory. This is not just the same three approaches with renamed rooms. The retained rock spine continues to give the garden its own commitment instead of dissolving the whole plan into one arena.

Consequential development points:

- Treat the outer wing continuations as real rear-territory access. Music → west gallery and cellar → upper court can bypass a direct site entry. Their timing and information cost must be assessed with only five defenders, rather than assuming the old rear court remains protected until a site falls.
- Reassess A's clearing and anchoring burden now that west music and east library openings join the two southern and north arrivals. The additions are spatially interesting; their simultaneous threat burden is a tactical question for the detailed review.
- The long carriage court and broad cellar wing need deliberate local encounters, quiet staging areas and legible occluders. Their current large extents are a useful expansion canvas, but the length alone does not provide the requested complexity.
- Preserve physical and visual continuity where the new wing boundaries meet the old estate. Music/gallery and cellar/stair/court interfaces deserve clear dimensions and height sections before production; scenic mountain massing should enclose this larger estate without becoming unintended playable shortcuts.

Proceed to detailed development and independent layout review of the expanded connections, including the changed spawn/rotation relationships. This verdict makes no claim about competitive balance, practical flank timing, utility counterplay or physical reachability.
