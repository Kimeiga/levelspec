## Observations and local judgment

**UG bends and first appearances — retained.** The actual west/cross/east views agree with the architectural dogleg. West and east arms terminate in high screens and require a turn before the cross-leg fight appears. This gives distinct preparation and contact stages rather than a straight deck with arbitrary high crates. The counterpart is information debt: seeing an empty arm does not clear the next corner. A follower at (43,63,5.6), (48,72,5.6), or (64,71,5.6) can see the same arm and its bypass; the next bend still blocks the fight until the follower advances. A clean two-person trade around every corner is not established. Retain the turns and test that contact/follower spacing explicitly rather than flattening the route or adding more clutter.

**U-C01/C02/C03 — useful directional low cover, no universal refuge.** All three low-camera views are occluded by the fixture; the corresponding standing views look over it. The report's selected posture pairs independently agree: standing cover eye sees either target eye height, low cover eye sees neither. Supplementary standing counterviews show the open side/bypass and the fixture's end, so the cover does not create an unseen sealed firing pocket. With the authored 2 m standing body, .75 m of upper body rises above a 1.25 m fixture; an actual shot or weapon silhouette has not been simulated. The flat floor contacts and flush outer edge do not show a foot slit or narrow rear crevice in these views. An opposing player advancing past an end can undo the cover. A rear arrival is a separate threat that ducking forward does not solve.

The 6 m arms have 5.6 m between guard inner faces; fixture depth across the arm is 1.8 m, leaving about 3.8 m of clear bypass. This is ample geometric passing room for the provisional .5 m diameter body. The captures show readable travel space rather than a squeeze beside each fixture. No multiplayer body-blocking or simultaneous passage was performed. C01 → C02 → C03 is a sequence of preparation choices with exposed movement between them, not continuous protected travel. The screen bends themselves provide full-height staging and preserve an ordinary route when a low fixture is occupied.

**Coverage and posture have costs — intentional tradeoff to retain for prototype.** At sampled (52,71,4.01), west-turn and east-turn entry samples span about 193.8° at both optical heights. At (48,70,4.01), standing admits both turns and the lower-mid-fire frontier, about 249.1° horizontal and 57.5° vertical; lowering the eye occludes lower-mid-fire while both turns remain. These are sampled visible entries, not a completed all-threat or frustum proof. The bridge region deliberately remains `approachesComplete:false`, and all 662 bridge hold records are `insufficient-evidence`. They cannot establish a safe all-entry hold. In a paired gallery push, one player can contest forward while the other covers the cleared rear; when that player is lost or a rear stair route is unaccounted for, the survivor must turn, retreat or commit. Low posture trades information for concealment. No extra rear-facing cover is required by the inspected evidence.

**UL / U-LB and ordinary landings — retained with hidden approach acknowledged.** Actual S01/S02 and reverse UL captures show the landing service wall separating the stair sides while leaving a route around its east end. S01 arrival can stage before turning into the gallery; S02 arrival creates a different approach behind that wall. At (30,60,4.01), the audit sees south-stairs and bridge but hides north-stairs; at (28,64,4.01) it sees north-stairs and part of bridge but hides south-stairs. This is a clearing obligation, not evidence that the hidden flight is harmless. R01's upper landing turns into the east gallery arm and retains a visible open connection. No doorway collision, trapped landing or floating step is visible in the inspected interfaces.

**UM / supporting piers — coherent stacked boundary, not vertical access.** The forward and reverse underpass views show real overhead deck, adjoining tall façades, P01/P02 boundaries and the round bend supports. Ground clearance under the flat deck is nominally 3.7 m, exceeding the 2 m standing body. The pier view from (49,70,1.6) includes M-L02 in the foreground and P04 down the corridor; supports create local occlusion and corner checks but do not visually seal the intended route. P03/P04 rise to the underside rather than making occupied rooftops. A player below the deck does not gain gallery access merely by passing beneath it. This inspection does not certify every off-route pocket, nor bullet behavior through the deck.

**D01 — optional exposed commitment; mechanic remains open.** The 3 m south-edge gap X47..50 interrupts the surrounding guard and reveals supported ground around (48.5,67.5,0). The reverse view exposes the upper gap between guard ends; the slab lip limits views of a player withdrawn from the edge. Thus a player leaning into the opening can gain lower information while exposing the opening in return; crouching or backing away also sacrifices that information. A lower opponent can threaten the opening, but reciprocal full-body exposure during takeoff, landing and peeking is unmeasured. The landing area is not a private safe pocket: ground movement can approach along UM, and upper players can remain near the opening. The gap is outside the normal center of the 6 m cross-leg, leaving an ordinary gallery passage. Visual depth alone is not enough to certify accidental-fall avoidance.

D01 is not needed to complete the upper circuit. Ordinary return is via S01/S02 or R01. The gap has no fabricated navigation link and has not been passed by falling in this evidence. Fall distance, fall damage, air control, landing recovery, jump-back/boost possibilities and genuine one-way behavior require target-engine checks. Its drawn directed edge remains a design hypothesis.

## Prototype gate and next checks

**Accept this R07 local scope for continued prototype iteration.** No supported consequential geometry defect requiring a local repair was found in the inspected gallery/underpass/landing relationships. This is qualified spatial acceptance, not competitive balance or target-engine acceptance.

Before claiming gameplay readiness, perform paired contact tests at each bend with an actual follower, standing/low full-body and weapon counterviews at fixture ends, a rear-threat test through S02 while a teammate contests east, and D01 takeoff/landing/return tests with actual falling and damage rules.
