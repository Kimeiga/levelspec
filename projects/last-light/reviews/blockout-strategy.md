## Population scenarios — qualified behavior hypotheses

- **5v5 opening /3+2:** Two home entries remain feasible in the actual volume. A tank/damage/support group can stage at South while the damage/support pair uses the Arcade low mouth or pays for loft setup; B has the rotational counterpart. The Lift prevents automatic support across the point, so paired support must be maintained or deliberately reassigned.
- **Five-player rush:** The primary six-meter entrance is broad enough geometrically for the stated bodies to approach together. A rush gives up an orthogonal angle and remains susceptible to area denial at its chosen mouth. Reversing through the home loop is a commitment, while moving through Court is exposed. No combat-throughput claim follows from a single body path.
- **Tank-led occupation:** A point holder can use one side of Lift while a support shares that side. A second support/damage pair can cover the other side or maintain height; assigning two people permanently to a loft reduces ground point presence. The compiled cover does not let one point occupant safely watch every entry. The scan's broad angular spans at outer ring samples reinforce that this is a team territory, not a solo sentry position.
- **Ranged loft hold /grounded retake:** The height occupant gains oblique pressure but cannot score upstairs. Lift occlusion permits a choice of point side, and the reverse aperture views permit direct pressure. A retaking team can also attack the wing/stair, but this spends players and time away from the point. Whether direct ground damage or a particular hero's denial tool sufficiently punishes an established loft remains a target-engine test.
- **3v3:** With fewer players, holding every mouth plus both height positions is infeasible. Conceding a side and rotating around Lift offers a local concentration; keeping an isolated loft observer preserves information while weakening capture presence. The actual reversible stair route makes eventual regroup possible, with travel cost.
- **2v5 stagger:** Two players have no supported expectation of individually clearing all Court threats. A retreat behind kiosk/core and waiting for the next wave is the intended recovery option. The two mouths enable a coordinated re-entry, not compensation for a three-player deficit. Repeated independent entries may feed the hold.
- **Team wipe /reinforcement:** Navigation-to-marker route estimates are46.35m from A and48.73m from B; marker `(36,36)` is intentionally displaced within the centered zone, so this difference is not itself a capture-arrival imbalance. The performed chosen-waypoint approaches are61.77m per side through the primary path and54.51m per side through the side path. Dividing those selected movements by the assumed target5.5m/s gives about11.2/9.9seconds, not measured fastest-game timings. With the provisional8second wave, a wipe creates a meaningful regroup window. Actual time to the nearest scoring boundary, mobility timings and contested re-entry need game-engine testing.
