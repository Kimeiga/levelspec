# L02 independent spatial / visibility review

**Verdict: changes required to the shelter/cover contract; spatial arrangement and
provisional construction are suitable for blockout once those corrections are
recorded.** Compiled movement and height-dependent visibility remain pending.

Inspected `layout.svg` visually through its matching
`generated/deadwater/sketch/layout.png`, then `brief.md` and `inventory.md`, before
reading `design.md`. This is a drawing review, not inspection of constructed
geometry. The later L03 return assessment belongs in a separate revision note.

## Raw assessment and construction

The folded platform arrangement is legible. Cargo branches into the Sea Walk and
Freight circuits, with real changes of approach through Valve and Pipe Bend. A is
raised and connected to the rear service deck; B remains on the lower processing
platform. Ocean gaps deny walking shortcuts without requiring invisible barriers.
The revised SA head has one A threshold, resolving the R01 ambiguity.

Provisional 0.30 m radius / 1.80 m standing / 1.10 m crouched dimensions fit the
5–7 m normal thresholds, the 4 m O06, and 6 m stairs with generous passing space.
Sixteen 0.15 m rises reach +2.40 m without exceeding the 0.25 m step assumption.
SW/SA runs of 8 m and SB's 6 m run are plausible. Their ≥2 m landings permit a
standing body and follower to reset without relying on a jump. Flanking infill
must close the unused width around the flights, rather than leave accidental
under-stair or side routes. The 0.35 m slabs, explicit stair undersides, guard
contacts, caps and portal headroom require actual compiled examination.

Tall internal bulkheads must use the higher adjacent deck as their height datum,
as inventory specifies. The SVG's phrase “above local deck” must not be implemented
as a low-side 4.60 m wall where an upper player can stand beside it. No occupied
tower roof, derrick climb, underpass or jump-dependent connection is proposed.

## Consequential findings

**V-L02-01 — correct CT shelter, changes required.** Extend the screen's north
end sufficiently to cover the full doorway/anchor diagonal while preserving a
standing-body passage, relocate the exposed anchors, or explicitly limit the
shelter claim. The author has proposed extending it to y73.75; adjudicate the
updated drawing before production, then verify aperture and spawn offsets in 3D.

**V-L02-02 — correct A-reel role, changes required to documentation.** Its
x22–25,y54–56 location is west of the SA enclosure. It can prepare the West/A
alcove, not a player on the Short head. SA's reset is behind its actual doorway
walls. No additional prop is required merely to preserve the old label.

**V-L02-03 — upper-to-P sea-gap view, consequential compiled check.** Low sea
guards are not tall view blockers. For example, R (54,64), eye z4.074 to P
(54,46), eye z1.674 crosses R's y62 edge at z3.807, above its z3.65 guard, and P's
y48 edge at z1.94, above its z1.25 guard. A (42,60) can have a similar diagonal.
This can add a raised CT angle over the intended B split route. The designer may
accept it as a deliberate paid-for overlook, but must depict and review that
ownership/exposure; otherwise use a local tall edge screen. Actual rendered
occlusion and supported near-edge body positions remain unverified.

**V-L02-04 — qualify A hold claim, compiled check.** Do not describe the derrick
as universally preventing simultaneous T-entry sight. The east-side fan above is
plausible but exposed. Verify it, the toolhouse escape, both door frontiers and the
plant together before deciding whether a structural change is warranted.

## Author rationale considered and limits

The intended exposed SA climb, broad paired entries, machinery-separated local
sectors and earned rear rotation are consistent with the drawing. They remain
testable hypotheses. Statements that every CT anchor is screened, that A-reel
serves the Short landing, and that the plinth always separates both entry views
need the corrections above.

The next review must use revision-matched compiled opposing views and actual
unchanged-body walking, including the spawn offsets, low-cover positions,
intermediate stair positions, all three transfer interfaces and cross-sea edges.
No evidence here establishes bullet resistance, utility behavior, audio, trading,
continuous collision clearance, multiplayer balance or fun.
