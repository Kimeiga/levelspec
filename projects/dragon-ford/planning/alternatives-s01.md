# S01 candidate organizations (raw, no author preference stated)

All three are provisional. Coordinates are meters, +Y north. North street grade is one storey (3.4 m) above the south apron, following the reference study's stepped site. Attackers (T) start south outside the tower wall; defenders (CT) start on the north shop frontage. These spawn sides are themselves open to challenge.

## A — "Stepped Yamen" (drawn: `s01-a/plan-0.png`, `plan-1.png`, `plan-2.png`, `section-NS.png`, `section-EW.png`, data in `s01-a/layout-data.json`)

The central sky void holds a yamen compound that climbs the slope court by court (z 0 → 1.7 → 3.4); it is the middle of the map and CT's shortest A↔B rotation crosses its rear terrace. West: a tunnel lane with a stair flight (Sai Shing) and, parallel to it, the Free School building whose ground arcade, internal stair and first-floor gallery overlook the yamen courts through windows; both arrive at Bombsite A, a hemmed temple court at the upper grade (z 3.4). East: a factory street at the low grade arrives at Bombsite B, a double-height noodle works whose floor is at z 0 and whose mezzanine (z 3.4) is the defenders' arrival level. A long east–west tunnel (Lung Chun Road) crosses the yamen forecourt (Cannon Court). A roof route (z 6.8) leaves North Lane W, overlooks the yamen from the School roof, and descends a stair into A's west side.

## B — "Ring around the hollow" (not drawn)

The yamen court is a sunken, mostly inaccessible sacred void: visible from every side through arcades and windows but crossed on foot only by one exposed diagonal path. All circulation is a two-storey ring around the void (ground alleys below, shared corridors/galleries above) with stair shafts at the four corners. Bomb sites sit on the east and west of the ring (factory hall, temple lot); spawns north and south. Rotation is always a choice of clockwise/anticlockwise and upper/lower ring; the void supplies cross-map information and long shots but little movement.

## C — "Factory street section" (not drawn)

The map is organized as three stacked east–west streets on the slope: a low covered factory street (z 0), a middle shop arcade facing outward (z 3.4), and a roof street of terraces (z 6.8), tied together by three shared stair shafts and two double-height workshops that all three streets look into. The yamen void sits at one end as a single large junction court. Sites: one inside a double-height workshop (reached from all three streets), one on the roof terraces above the temple. Spawns at the west and east ends, so teams fight along and between the stacked streets rather than up a slope.
