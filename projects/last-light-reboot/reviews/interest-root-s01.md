# Independent spatial-interest comparison — S01

Root reviewed the raw brief and actual A/B plan PNGs before selecting an option or reading sibling verdicts. Image identities match `provenance.json` and `projects/reboot-study/root-initial-view-hashes.json`. This is spatial interpretation, not compiled reachability, combat or balance acceptance. The original plan and source-matched overview were also visually inspected.

**A — promising.** The department store, public promenade and service belt form a much more substantial mall journey than the original pair of diagonal home loops. The partial upper gallery could support movement between different fronts while remaining part of the same enclosed building. The risk is retaining a very large central combat room with surrounding access and asking interior props to create all of its decisions. Develop thick store fronts and a meaningful change of ceiling/threshold before relying on freestanding cover.

**B — promising; provisional spatial preference.** The occupied food court over the ground transfer gives the two atria a genuine vertical relationship. Ground movement can change the eventual ascent without immediately entering the scoring volume. Different public and service ascents can have different arrival conditions, rather than simply adding four stairs to equivalent doors. This departs substantially from the original two isolated lofts.

## Proposals for development

- **ROOT-LL-01: make the lower transfer a useful decision, not just a non-scoring detour.** Compare a limited view from one lower arrival into an ascent receiving space, versus a fully opaque transfer. A regrouping pair could obtain local information and support the start of a climb; an upper holder must still be able to break contact or contest another receiving edge. Do not expose the entire food court or permit the underpass to score. This needs actual apertures, targets, section and opposing positions in the detailed layout, then compiled rays/views.
- **ROOT-LL-02: divide control through architectural programs.** Develop the food stalls, structural/service core and gallery fronts as thick occupied boundaries. Compare where a tank can occupy while two teammates support from a different frontage and where an opposing group can redirect. Avoid a complete upper viewing ring with every support position covering every entrance. A reduced panoramic view costs immediate awareness but creates distinct retake stages. Geometry and final game abilities must establish whether that cost is useful.
- **ROOT-LL-03: preserve the shared mall volume in the AML.** The two atria need actual roof/skylight structure, readable storefront depth, supporting columns and occupied galleries. A roof-on exterior, cutaway and opposing interior views should demonstrate the same building. The plan's architectural advantage would be lost if production became open platforms inside a perimeter wall.

Neither alternative closes detailed strategy, visibility, construction or compiled gates. The reference study supports shared roofed volume and substantial storefront transitions; the raised scoring floor and precise circulation remain inventions.
