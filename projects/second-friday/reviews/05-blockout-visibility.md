## Decisive retained tradeoffs

**Spawn protection remains external game work.** The finite optical study casts4800 opaque render-triangle rays from configured point/front-and-rear roof samples to all ten spawn markers with eye1.674/1.023 and lateral offsets±0.25m. All are blocked in this sampled configuration. That supports the intended building screening from those sampled central positions; it does not prove every viewpoint hidden, nor does it contradict the observed close-hold exposure. The two thresholds can require different look directions; these rays do not prove that one player can suppress both simultaneously. Enemy exclusion, damage/healing filtering and protection extents must be implemented and exercised in the target engine.

**Roof influence is broad and partly reciprocal.** `roof-west-front` and `point-to-roof` plus reciprocal clear rays from `(64,78.6,4.874)` to `(86,64,1.674)` at26.60m confirm substantial point influence and a clear return eye line from that point pose. The central screen withholds one frontage section, not every oblique view from a side bay. B06 does not screen most scoring floor. CUPBOARD occludes a subset; low displays leave upper bodies exposed to height. This is an intentional contested support position, not demonstrated balance.

**Book Walk counterplay is position-dependent.** The exact Book Walk `(86,76,1.674)` to east bay `(76,78.6,4.874)` ray hits the parapet near `(78.11,78.05,4.20)`. The east-stair reverse view still sees much of the flight. Do not generalize a blocked occupied-bay ray into no ascent pressure, or generalize a visible flight into guaranteed counterfire on the bay.
