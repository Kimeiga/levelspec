## Width, low cover and physical construction

The width report is honest. A6 m flight has guards leaving **5.76 m geometric clearance**. Successful centers at−2.1/0/+2.1 m demonstrate a **5.0 m body envelope** for the unchanged radius.40 player. Exploratory±2.35 m centers fail near the guard/navigation boundary; they are retained, not silently discarded. This is not evidence that the usable flight is only a narrow one-person channel, nor is it proof that every path in the full5.76 m clear span works. The demonstrated envelope is adequate to accept the declared body-scale route. Larger heroes, several bodies abreast and tank/support passing remain target-engine stress cases. Any handoff should continue reporting geometric and demonstrated width separately.

LC-W/E/N/S and LC-GW/GE are1.15 m high on their own floors. Their relationship to the1.0695 m crouch eye predicts a short ground-level duck margin; the1.86 m standing eye remains above them. Actual views show side exposure and the nearby tall masses that bound the next move. The public stair/body samples demonstrate that relative elevation can remove or retain only part of support even with the same nominal guard height. No image or height number proves protection from every upper angle, feet/weapon concealment or penetration. Do not add extra props solely to create uniform low-cover counts.

The nine construction fills are proportionate realizations of closed residual volumes, not new tactical walls across intended routes: FIX-GNW/GNE close cinema-base pockets to the3.25 slab underside; FIX-UW/UE and FIX-USW/UNE close inaccessible upper ledges to4.7 guard height; FIX-GSLOT joins the west planter to its wall; FIX-USW-SLOT/USLOT close guard junction slots. I inspected their ground/upper overlays, exact bounds, route relationships and corresponding compiled views. Their heights preserve the upper sightline structure. The two overlapping fascia caps and six high skylight mullions address construction/detail without reaching player routes. No supported evidence requires undoing these fills. Absence of a nav island alone would not have justified them; their declared closed-volume positions and actual views do.

## Improvements and retained tradeoffs

**Select P01 for a bounded experiment only if strategy accepts its approach-time/attention change.** The [focused P01 local review](spatial-stair-p01.md) accepts its paper construction for that purpose and records independent inner/center/outer checks. It offers a true curved ascent beside and then above the lower journey. It also reduces nominal width to3.76 m, opens early side exposure and shortens the nominated west-public path by about14.66 m. Those costs prevent calling it a cosmetic swap or automatically superior. Unaffected C01 evidence can be reused, while X1/U1/G1/OW and the old arrival need a native return.

The current G1 mouth is physically present but difficult to understand as a whole from the atrium. Retain that as a **legibility risk**, not a failed connection. A useful future comparison would frame the destination beyond one return from an admitted lower preparation position, using the same roof-on view in both variants. Favor a small change to the mouth/return relation only if it improves route recognition without restoring a full straight pressure lane. A giant opening or a new cross-level firing window is not justified by the current evidence.
