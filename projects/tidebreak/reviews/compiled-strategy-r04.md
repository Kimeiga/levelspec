## Strategic reading

The recessed dock and covered slip support different kinds of commitment. At A, the hull separates the two lower-side journeys; arriving from D1 does not grant the same immediate information as arriving from D2. Leaving the lower level requires a real ascent, and the northern rim is a separate task for an upper teammate or opponent. At B, the hull divides approach information while the partial gallery trades an ascent for local support over the bow. The full wall between these spaces prevents the upper gallery from becoming general map-wide surveillance.

The two sites are not equally near either spawn. Sampled navigation distances are S1→A63.0m, S1→B100.4m, S2→A84.8m and S2→B52.5m. These are navigation paths, not observed first-contact times. They support an intentionally asymmetric opening hypothesis: S1 can pressure the dock earlier, while S2 can establish the hall earlier. Do not advertise equal setup opportunity or silently assign a conventional attack/defend mode from these markers.

The northern exterior route is a substantial commitment. The performed journey from the west service road around the pump house and apse to E1 takes approximately45.7s at the inspection speed; its reverse also passes. It can support an early investment, withdrawal or prepared flank. It is not credible to call the full detour an immediate response to a short objective interaction. The southern S1-to-hall-entry journey takes approximately18.8s; traversing either hall apron onward to B takes about13.0s in its separate performed walk. Clearing and combat can only add costs to these unobstructed test journeys.

## Population and transition scenarios

- **Opening5v5:** a plausible S1 split places a pair through D1, a player on the western rim/service road and a pair toward the southern slip. S2 can contest the hall from E1/G2 while committing other players toward D2 and the dock head. This is a spatially plausible allocation, not an observed optimal plan. Neither team has manpower to clear every perimeter, hull shoulder and gallery position simultaneously.
- **A occupied, switching toward B:** D2 is the shorter actionable relationship between sites. A lower player can climb and decide at grade rather than immediately entering the hall sightline. Leaving a teammate below retains some lower control but does not supply automatic support inside the hall; moving both upward concedes lower information. A defender can hold the new threshold or pressure the north apron rather than having to watch every part of the flight from B.
- **B occupied, gallery held:** the standing gallery-front sample sees all nine tested body points on each bow shoulder but only the three high points at B itself; crouching behind that gallery rail loses the sampled B view. The gallery is useful for approach information, but its rear position gives up that information. A lower group can pressure one apron while a teammate contests the relevant ascent. The evidence does not establish whether real weapons/utility make that response timely enough.
- **A held against rim pressure:** the north-near rim sample sees9/9 points at A while standing, but0/9 from the tested crouched eye; stepping back to the north-back sample also loses A. That makes rim occupation and exposure meaningful. It does not justify declaring the whole basin exposed or relocating A purely from the plan. The lower team can use the hull shoulder while an upper teammate contests the rim; exact objective use may force a less favorable position and needs an engine test.
- **3v2 or isolated survivor:** losing a player reduces the ability to combine lower occupancy with rim information, or B occupancy with gallery information. A survivor can change which side of a hull they occupy and surrender a viewpoint, but cannot assume a45s perimeter route will resolve an imminent objective. This is a meaningful commitment cost rather than a demand to add emergency exits everywhere.

The independent local probes also show a long G1-landing view back toward S1. In this no-respawn trial that is a late route-control risk, not automatically an opening spawn-kill defect: G1 must first be acquired, and sampled S1/S2 opposing exits do not see one another. Test a southern retreat under gallery control before generalizing this map to a respawning mode.
