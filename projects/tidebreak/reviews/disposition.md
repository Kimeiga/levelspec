## Review coverage and stop decision

Independent exploration covered architectural alternatives and macro candidates before a developed plan. Layout review then covered spatial interest/circulation, strategy and local dock encounters. A separate compiled wave covered strategy/player behavior, local spatial/visibility encounters, and architecture. The author selected a coherent bounded experiment rather than adopting every suggestion.

All selected experiments and required findings are closed at compiled blockout scope. Optional improvements remain explicit future tests. The trial should now move to actual play with specified weapons, objective rules and clock rather than accumulate more untested geometry. Priorities are D2 pair staging, B/gallery response time, A's rim pressure, and the long northern detour. A G1 view back toward S1 also needs reassessment if this no-respawn brief becomes a respawning mode.
